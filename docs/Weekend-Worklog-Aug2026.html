The whole app froze every few minutes

Stability
Before

A recurring ~16-second freeze hit the entire backend worker - every request it was serving stalled at once, which showed up as "random slow modules" and bursts of CORS / 502 errors.

→
After

A single crash blanked the entire app

Reliability
Before

Any render error - or the brief gap while sign-in was mid-flight - could unmount React and leave the user staring at a white screen with no way back.

→
After

Error boundaries around the app and every view, plus an auth busy-state loader. A crash now shows a recoverable message; the screen never goes blank.

Self-service

Only HR could see or touch a timecard. Employees had no visibility into their punches and no way to flag a missed one except by email.

→

Big lists hung the browser tab

→
After

Rendering is capped across every heavy list, and data fetching is cached (TanStack Query). Large data sets stay responsive.

→
After

Mon · Aug 3

Fixed-salary payroll, end to end

28 commits

Built the monthly-salary pay model, wired the employee timecard into it, then hardened the whole thing with a multi-agent audit. Payroll math was re-checked by an end-to-end test on every change.

Fixed monthly salary new

  • New fixed pay type (backend model): paid a monthly salary, deducted per missed weekday, plus flat weekend overtime - a calendar-month timecard, separate from the hourly engine.
  • Attendance bands: a weekday is Present (5h+), Half day (4–5h), Absent (<4h), or Working while clocked in - the current day never deducts until it has elapsed.
  • Currency (INR / USD) across the timecard, wage editor and pay summary; no rounding on salaried time; no "+add" on days that haven't happened yet.
  • Pay is frozen on finalize so a later raise can't retro-price a month that was already paid.
  • The inline wage and the Pay & Benefits comp record are now linked two-way - they can never disagree.

Employee timecard & approvals

  • Consolidated the redundant Time Sheet / Timecard views into one HR-parity payroll timecard, with missing punches added inline; dropped the duplicate People > Timecards tab.
  • Three-signature sign-off (Employee / Manager / HR) with stale-signature detection and a signed snapshot; employee sign reminder; HR edits notify the employee.
  • Every punch edit/request notifies the approver; the reason for a changed time is shown inline on the card, an approver "to review" badge surfaces pending items, and the request stack is actionable rather than a stale banner.

Audit & correctness fixes fixes

  • Ran a three-agent code audit and fixed a timezone-boundary bug that could deduct a full day's salary from a non-UTC employee at the day/month edge.
  • Fixed an approval-vs-signature conflict (an employee signature was blocking manager approval) and a period mismatch on approve / finalize / sign for salaried staff.
  • Payroll linking fixes P1–P3: attested hours equal paid hours, no retro-repricing, no silent stale sign-offs.
  • Live-testing fixes: today's status now reads "Working", the Break column shows away-time, signature-card padding, and currency-aware estimated pay.
