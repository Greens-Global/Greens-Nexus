# Nexus map for "Nexus Fundamentals" (from repo exploration, 11/04/2026)

Tiers: Employee (1) own work; Supervisor (2) supervisor screens only where grants open them; Manager (3) team approvals/schedules/People for own line, Shift Planner; Administrator (4) runs their company inside company walls (sees every company's tickets); Super Administrator (5) everything. Under a name the screen shows JOB TITLE; tier is a mark only (filled shield SA, outline shield Admin, people glyph Manager). Access Groups / job roles open modules on top of the tier (Sidebar isNavItemVisible: !minRole || admin || granted). Partners (externals) see only granted modules.

1 Sign in: "Welcome to Nexus"; Team Sign-In (Microsoft); Partner Sign-In (email code) for externals. First sign-in and each new policy version: policy + monitoring notice, Accept & Continue. Profile menu: Profile, What's New, Legal, Tools, Sign Out (sign out clears offline copy).

2 Layout: sidebar groups My desk (Dashboard, Workday, Team), Work (Tasks, Tickets, Files, Documents, Credentials, Workflows - supervisor+grant), Modules (IT, Construction, Operations, Item Management (everyone), Asset Management, Accounting, Investor Relations, People, Marketing), System (Help Center (everyone), Settings). Desktop sidebar clock dial: green in, amber break, red out; play/pause/stop; none for exempt; phone = floating capsule. Header: Search Nexus (Ctrl/Cmd+K or /; pages, tasks, projects, people, items, requests), + Create, Bell, ? Help, profile. Phone: hamburger menu (dot = something waiting), bottom tabs in Tasks/Documents/Items, popups = bottom sheets; desktop popups = sheets from the right.

3 Notifications: bell "N needs action - N updates" / "All caught up"; Mark All Read; Clear All (Closed, 30 days, restorable). Yellow priority bar for must-act items, Open. Push = same as bell rows addressed to a person; broadcasts never push; off until server keys set.

4 Phone install: Profile > Phone Notifications: Install Nexus (Android Chrome/Edge Install button; iPhone Safari Share > Add to Home Screen) -> "Installed"; Push to This Device switch (iOS 16.4+, installed app only; allow notifications first). Appearance: Dark Mode, Theme, Navigation Motion (Off/Subtle/Lively/Bold), Sidebar (Collapse Automatically / Keep Open). Other profile sections: Date & Time, Email Signature, Task Emails, Security.

5 Dashboard: tabs Dashboard, Approvals (managers/HR/anyone with something waiting), Notes, Links. Home: greeting, tiles Open Tasks / Checked-Out Items / My Equipment / Signatures Needed; Needs Your Attention ("You're All Caught Up"); Team Pulse; My Hours (7 days); Quick Actions (Request an Item, New Task, New Ticket, Ctrl+K hint); Favorites. Customize: Add Widget, saved views, Create a New View. Widgets anyone: Time Clock, My Requests, Due Back Soon, Coming Up, Action Required (My Day), My Work, My Time, Announcements, Calendar, Notes, Weather, Quick Links, Favorites; Quick Actions dock (New Task, New Event, New Email, Request an Item, New Ticket; options Request Time Off, Punch Correction, Ask HR, Fill a Form, My Shifts, Find a Person, Check Out an Item, Knowledge Base, Punch In / Out). Team widgets: Team Clocked-In, Who Has What (supervisor+); Pending Approvals, Time Off to Review, Team Time, Out Today, Time Exceptions (manager+). Announcements only via widget (not default) - Acknowledge when asked; only admins post.

6 Workday: tabs Overview, Time Sheet, Time Off, Performance (exempt: no Time Sheet / clock card). Overview tiles: Hours - This Week, Time Off This Year, Time With Us, My Tickets (maintenance), small tiles Ask HR, Reimbursements, Purchase Request, Fill a Form. Documents to Sign at top (Review & Sign). Time Clock card. My Schedule (My Week / Team, Confirm banner, Shift Requests). My Documents (signed copies, HR files, paystubs). Profile card Contact & Emergency (Edit; name/job/department/bank changes via Ask HR). My Equipment (Assigned / Checkouts, Open Item Management).

7 Time Clock: only valid buttons show - Punch In / Start Break / End Break / Punch Out. Status "Punched in at 8:02 AM". Today / This Week / Pay Period. Message box each punch: Beginning of Day ("Send & start the day"; skip tick "I already sent my login (BOD) message"); Going on break (reason required, "Start break & notify"); Resuming work ("End break & notify"); End of Day (summary, done, pending; "Send & clock out"; skip tick EOD). Posts to group Teams chat as the employee with "Sent by Nexus"; Say It dictation; field roles skip boxes. Location checked at punch only, never blocks: "at <site>" / "Out of Location, flagged for review" / "Location Off" / "No Location"; phone precise, desktop coarse. Early clock-in: "Clock In Anyway / Not Yet", manager belled. Company devices: monitoring consent at first punch; may require screen share. Break allowance "N min left of your 1h daily break" / "Break over by N min". Still on Break? bell at 60 min default, manager at 2x. Long shift banner: I'm Still Working / Fix My Punch Out; open >16h = Missing. No signal: saved on device, sends itself; "Your Punch Did Not Record ... don't punch out to fix it" Retry Now. California: no break question at clock-in; break statement at signing; Report a Missed Break (Meal / Rest) on Time Sheet then Send Back to Manager.

8 Time Sheet: red Missing cell / Add Clock-Out / Fix Break -> Request a Missing Punch / Fix a Break Punch, reason required; "Request sent to your approver - it shows on your time sheet as Not Yet Approved until they decide." Change Requests This Period (Cancel Request). After period end manager Review & Approve or Send Back; ONLY the employee signs in Nexus Sign (Sign & Submit / Review & Sign; Sign Updated Time Sheet after HR fix). Change after approval: punch fix request or decline in Nexus Sign with reason. Hours shown H:MM, never decimals. Say "Time Sheet".

9 Shifts: Workday > My Schedule: "Your schedule was shared ... have a look and confirm." Confirm; Usual Hours; Request Pending; weather alerts (weather departments only); calendar link. Shift Requests: Send Swap Request, Send Offer, Release Shift, Request Shift; Accept / Decline; Waiting On Teammate / Waiting On Manager. Trading/releasing/open shifts OFF by default per company. Team > Shifts read only; Shift Planner manager+.

10 Time Off (Workday > Time Off): Request Time Off - reason Personal / Sick / Vacation / Medical Appointment (configurable), From/To, All day or times, Note, "Keep this confidential", Request for a Past Date; Request. My Requests "Your manager decides - you get a bell notification"; cancel pending; some go to HR next. Leave balance card, Team Leave Calendar, Delegate My Approvals (managers). Approved time off is NOT auto-punched. Hourly company holidays are unpaid (office closed).

11 Ask HR / Reimbursements / Purchase Requests (85% panels; click outside nudges, does not close): Ask HR New Request - Request a Letter, Update a Document, Profile Change, Question for HR, Something Else; Send to HR; red dot = HR update. Reimbursements New Reimbursement - Travel / Meals / Supplies / Software / Equipment / Other, Business Purpose, receipts (Ctrl+V), Who Approves It; Send for Approval; Add Receipt later; HR approves once receipt attached; Fix and Resubmit. Purchase Request: What Do You Need, Quantity, Reason / Justification, Reference Link, Who Should Approve This; Submit Request.

12 Approvals (Dashboard > Approvals): Approvals (decide others', manager+) and Yours To Do (sign/accept/swap). History; J/K, A approve, R reject. Phone: one card, two decisions, Filter, no bulk approve.

13 Tasks (supervisor + grant; most job roles carry it - verify): tabs Home, Tasks, Projects, Portfolios, Teams. Home: Tasks / Updates / Bookmarks. Tasks views: My Day, List, Board, Calendar, Dashboard, Files, Time. My Day empties at local midnight. Create: + New Task or Create Task; fields title, description, project, team, assign to, collaborators, due, start, priority, status, recurrence, labels, subtasks, attachments (Ctrl+V). Add Several / paste a list. Mark Complete; Post Comment / Reply; @mentions email the people named (mention in a Note notifies nobody). Task emails batched ~60 min; mentions/urgent/due today-tomorrow instant.

14 Tickets: employees use Help Center > Submit a Ticket (help topic, Department, Title e.g. "Light out in the front office", What Is Happening?, Priority, photos/files Ctrl+V; Create Ticket; What Happens Next). Lists: Open Tickets / Tickets I'm Following / Closed Tickets; Mark Resolved, Confirm (star rating), Reopen (while Resolved). Statuses New, Open, In Progress, Waiting for User, Waiting for Vendor, Resolved, Closed. Scan to Request: site/equipment QR tag -> place sheet with Open Tickets + Report a Problem (area/Common Area, Details, Photos, Send Report). Maintenance techs: Workday My Tickets tile - Start, Mark Done (checklist + Photo of the Finished Work), Scan a Tag; preventive tickets need a finishing photo. Desk queues (grant): Needs Reply, Unassigned, Mine, Due Soon, Waiting, All Open, Done, Preventive, Raised by Me.

15 Directory (Team > Directory): search, All Companies / All Offices, My Team, Off Today, Organizational Chart, Export PDF. Profile: Chat, Call, Video, Email, Save Contact, View on Chart, View Tasks. Message on Teams / Call on Teams everywhere; no in-app chat. Presence dots only once Microsoft permission set.

16 Item Management: My Stuff, Borrow (everyone); Requests (managers/allocators); Inventory (managers). Borrow cart: Who Is It For?, Why Do You Need It?, Return Dates, Who Approves It; Request N Items; + Request an Item. My Stuff: Accept ("It's With Me", no photo for permanent), Confirm Receipt (photo), Take It, Start Return / Return With Photo / Confirm Return (photo + Condition), Report Lost, Report Dead. Photos are evidence (handover, receipt, return; not lost). Due dates from handover. Overdue banner on Dashboard.

17 Forms: Fill a Form from Workday tile, + Requests > Fill a Form, Quick Actions dock, shift card. Fields Photo, GPS (Stamp My Location), Scan, Rating, Yes/No, Signature. Send -> Fill Another / Done. Offline "Saved on This Phone", sends itself; same entry twice = one. Inspections, incident reports, vehicle checks, checklists are Forms. Submitter never reviews own entry.

18 Notes (Dashboard > Notes): All, Pinned, Today (Daily Scratchpad), Shared, Trash. + New Note (Quick Note, Ctrl+Alt+N / Ctrl+Cmd+N), New Board (whiteboard). Share, Make a Task, Mark Up (image/PDF), Pin, Full Screen; voice notes + dictation. Private to owner + shared people; autosave; Keep Mine / Use Theirs.

19 Documents / Nexus Sign: module supervisor+grant; employees sign from Workday > Documents to Sign (offer letters, hiring packets, W-4, I-9, direct deposit, probation letters, Time Sheets). Employee always signs first.

20 Knowledge Base & Training (Help Center tile): Library, Training (Manage for owners). Library search, collections, Applies to departments; Read and Sign policies (type full name, Sign v<N>; new major version = sign again). My Training: Courses (Not Started / In Progress / Completed), Compliance (Valid Through), Certificate. Course player: Mark Lesson Complete, Next (videos to the end first); Checkpoints are practice, don't use a try (Check My Answers); Final Quiz "X% to pass - N tries", Start the Quiz, Submit Answers (all answered), fail -> "Reread these before you try again", Try Again; In-Person sign-off "Waiting for Review"; See Your Certificate / Download Certificate. Due from start date or fixed date; escalation to manager after escalate days.

21 Help Center: tiles Submit a Ticket, Ask Nexus (AI off - may say not set up), Documentation, Knowledge Base & Training, How Do I...; ticket list under tiles. Header ? panel: Home / Messages / Help; Search for Help, Ask a Question, What's New in Nexus, Help with This Page, Take the Tour.

22 + menu: Work (New Task (grant), New Note, New Board, New Ticket, New Document (grant)), Requests (Request an Item, Fill a Form), Outlook (New Email, New Event); rows show only if the module is open to you.

23 Other: My Briefing (daily briefing email as a page; Approve / Reject / Comment). Workday > Performance: My Numbers, My Goals, My Review, My 1:1s (managers also My Team).

24 Offline phone: opens with no signal on last saved copy; Workday, Fill a Form, punches work. Bar: "Waiting to send N saved on this phone" Sync Now; "N not sent" Review; "N needs your choice" Keep Mine / Use Theirs; "Signed out - sign in when you have signal to send" Sign In. Offline copy per person, cleared at sign-out; nothing queued under Act As.

25 Phone vs desktop: drawer + bottom tabs vs sidebar; capsule clock vs sidebar clock; search sheet vs Ctrl+K; bottom sheets vs right sheets; approvals one card vs list+panel; ticket popup vs side pane; camera vs Ctrl+V; precise vs rough location; install + push.

Unverified: which job roles carry Tasks/Tickets grants; per-company shift settings; Performance/Approvals exact copy. Risk: Announcements only show if the widget is added.
