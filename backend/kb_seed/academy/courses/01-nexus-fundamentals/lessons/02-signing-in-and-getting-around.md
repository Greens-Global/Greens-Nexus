Luis sits down at the shared office computer at Valley Center for the first time. His manager wants the Fairfield site manager's phone number, and the next shift arrives in an hour. He needs to sign in, find a person and sign out cleanly. Here is how.

Open Nexus in your browser and click **Team Sign-In**. Microsoft asks for your work email, your password and your usual sign-in check, such as an approval on your phone. **Partner Sign-In** is for people outside Greens, who get a code by email. Never use it with your work email.

> [!WARNING]
> Never share your Microsoft password, even with a coworker who offers to "punch you in." Your sign-in is how Nexus knows every punch, signature and request is really yours.

## The Policy Notice

The first time you sign in, and each time the company publishes a new version of its policies, Nexus shows the company policies before anything else, including the monitoring disclosure (what Nexus records, such as where a punch was made). Read it, tick the box that says you read and agree to comply, and click **Accept & Continue**. You cannot skip it, and seeing it again weeks later is normal: the policies changed.

**The left menu** moves you between modules, in four groups: My Desk (Dashboard, Workday, Team), Work (Tasks, Tickets, Documents and more, by grant), Modules (Item Management for everyone, other department tools by grant) and System (Help Center, and Settings by tier or grant). You only see what your tier and grants allow.

- Click the **N** at the top to go back to the Dashboard from anywhere.
- A small **dot** beside a module means something there is waiting on you. Open it; the tab with the red number is where it is.
- The small **clock** at the foot of the menu shows your shift. Play punches in, Pause starts a break, Stop punches out, and clicking the dial opens the full Time Clock.

**The header** names the module you are in and carries its tabs, so you switch between a module's parts there. On the right:

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Search Nexus** | Finds tasks, people, items, requests and pages. Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) or / jumps to it. |
| **+** | Starts something new from anywhere: New Note, New Ticket, Request an Item, Fill a Form and more. |
| **The bell** | Your notifications (Your Dashboard and the Bell). |
| **?** | The Help panel: Search for Help, What's New, Help with This Page and Take the Tour. |
| **Your photo or initials** | Your profile menu: Profile, What's New, Legal and **Sign Out**. |

## Search, Tours and Help

Search is the fastest way around. Press Ctrl+K, type a few letters and pick a result with the arrow keys and Enter. A coworker's name opens their contact card; "time off" can take you straight to the right screen.

Every screen has a guided tour. Open **?** and click **Take the Tour** to have Nexus point out each part, or **Help with This Page** for the written guide to that screen.

## On a Phone

Everything in this course works on a phone too. What moves:

| On a computer | On a phone |
|---|---|
| Left menu on the side | A menu button opens it as a drawer |
| Search bar in the header | A search button opens a search sheet |
| Clock at the foot of the menu | A small clock pill on the screen |
| Popups slide in from the right | Popups rise from the bottom, full screen |
| Paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V | Take a photo with the camera |

## Sign Out of a Shared Computer

Your profile menu ends with **Sign Out**. On a shared office computer, sign out at the end of every shift. Signing out also clears the copy of your Nexus data saved on that device, which is exactly what you want on a shared machine.

Luis signs in, accepts the notice, presses Ctrl+K, types the Fairfield manager's first name and has the contact card in five seconds. At the end of his shift he clicks his initials and **Sign Out**.

## Common Mistakes

- **Choosing Partner Sign-In.** Greens employees always use Team Sign-In.
- **Clicking past the policy notice.** It tells you what Nexus records about your work.
- **Staying signed in on a shared computer.** The next person could act as you.
- **Hunting through menus.** Ctrl+K is faster.

## Key Points

- **Team Sign-In** with your Microsoft work account. Read the policy notice and **Accept & Continue**.
- The left menu moves between modules; the header carries the module's tabs, Search, +, the bell, ? and your profile.
- **Take the Tour** and **Help with This Page** are one click away on every screen.
- Sign out of a shared computer at the end of every shift.
