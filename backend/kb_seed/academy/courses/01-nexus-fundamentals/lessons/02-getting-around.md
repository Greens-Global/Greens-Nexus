Luis is on day three in the HR office. His manager asks for the phone number of the superintendent on a construction job, and Luis has never opened the Directory. He has it in five seconds without touching a menu. Here is how.

**The left menu** moves you between modules, in four groups: My Desk (Dashboard, Workday, Team), Work (Tasks, Tickets, Documents and more, by grant), Modules (Item Management for everyone, other department tools by grant) and System (Help Center, and Settings by tier or grant). You only see what your tier and grants allow.

- Click the **N** at the top to go back to the Dashboard from anywhere.
- A small **dot** beside a module means something there is waiting on you. Open it; the tab with the red number is where it is.
- If you are hourly, the small **clock** at the foot of the menu shows your shift. Play punches in, Pause starts a break, Stop punches out, and clicking the dial opens the full Time Clock.

**The header** names the module you are in and carries its tabs, so you switch between a module's parts there. On the right:

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Search Nexus** | Finds tasks, people, items, requests and pages. Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) or / jumps to it. |
| **+** | Starts something new from anywhere: New Note, New Ticket, Request an Item, Fill a Form and more. |
| **The bell** | Your notifications (Your Dashboard and the Bell). |
| **?** | The Help panel: Search for Help, What's New, Help with This Page and Take the Tour. |
| **Your photo or initials** | Your profile menu: Profile, What's New, Legal and **Sign Out**. |

## Search First

Search is the fastest way around. Press Ctrl+K, type a few letters and pick a result with the arrow keys and Enter. A coworker's name opens their contact card; "time off" can take you straight to the right screen.

Use it before you hunt through menus. You do not need to remember which module holds a screen, only what you are looking for.

## The + Menu

The **+** in the header starts the common things from wherever you are, so you never leave the page you are on to find the right module first. A **New Note** for something you want to remember, a **New Ticket** for something broken, **Request an Item** to borrow equipment, **Fill a Form** when your team uses forms. What it lists depends on your grants, like the left menu.

## Tours and the Help Panel

Every screen has a guided tour. Open **?** and click **Take the Tour** to have Nexus point out each part, or **Help with This Page** for the written guide to that screen. **Search for Help** looks through the guides, and **What's New** lists recent changes to Nexus, so a button that moved since last week is explained there.

> [!TIP]
> Lost on a screen? **?**, then **Help with This Page**, is faster than asking a coworker, and it is always up to date.

## Your Profile Menu

Your photo or initials at the top right opens your profile menu:

- **Profile** holds your own settings: phone notifications and appearance (Nexus on Your Phone covers both).
- **What's New** is the same list as in the Help panel.
- **Legal** holds the Privacy Policy and the Terms & Conditions.
- **Sign Out** ends your session. On a shared computer, sign out at the end of every shift; it also clears the copy of your Nexus data saved on that machine.

## At Greens

Luis presses Ctrl+K, types the superintendent's first name and has the contact card on screen in five seconds. Later he opens a screen he has never used, clicks **?**, then **Take the Tour**, and Nexus walks him through it part by part.

## Common Mistakes

- **Hunting through menus.** Ctrl+K is faster.
- **Asking a coworker how a screen works.** **Take the Tour** and **Help with This Page** are one click away.
- **Thinking a missing menu item is an error.** It is a grant you do not have (Welcome to Nexus).
- **Staying signed in on a shared computer.** The next person could act as you.

## Key Points

- The left menu moves between modules; the header carries the module's tabs, Search, +, the bell, ? and your profile.
- Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) opens Search from any screen.
- The **+** menu starts a note, a ticket, an item request or a form from anywhere.
- **Take the Tour** and **Help with This Page** are under **?** on every screen.
