Monday morning at Temecula. Marco opens Nexus and sees a yellow bar across the top, a 1 on a tile, and a red number on the bell. Each one is asking him for something different. This lesson is how to read them in under a minute.

Nexus opens on the **Dashboard**. The designed layout, **Home**, shows from top to bottom: a greeting with your clock status, **tiles** that count what is with you or waiting on you, **Needs Your Attention** (or **You're All Caught Up**), **My Hours** for the last 7 days, **Quick Actions** and your **Favorites** links.

| Tile | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Open Tasks** | Tasks due soon or in progress (if you use Tasks). |
| **Checked-Out Items** | Equipment with you right now. |
| **My Equipment** | Items assigned to you to keep. |
| **Signatures Needed** | Documents waiting for your signature. |

Click a tile to jump to its list. Signatures Needed matters most: an HR form or a Time Sheet waiting on you can hold up your pay or your onboarding.

The Dashboard's other tabs are **Approvals** (decisions waiting on you; an Employee sees it when something waits, under **Yours To Do**), **Notes** and **Links** (company and personal web links).

## Your Own View and the Announcements Widget

Click **Customize**, then **Add Widget**, pick a widget, drag it where you want it, click **Save**, then **Done**. Your view joins the picker beside Home; the starred one opens first. **Make Home My Default** in the view menu brings the designed layout back.

Company announcements reach your Dashboard **only through the Announcements widget**, and it is not on Home by default. Add it on day one: **Customize**, **Add Widget**, **Announcements** (under Live), **Save**, **Done**. When a post asks you to confirm you read it, read it and click **Acknowledge**. Administrators post announcements; you read and acknowledge them.

The bell holds every alert meant for you: approvals, replies on your tickets, a schedule to confirm, a document to sign, reminders. A red number means unread alerts.

- **Click a notice** to open the exact thing it is about.
- **Mark All Read** clears the red number without removing anything.
- **Clear All** moves your updates to **Closed**. They stay there for 30 days, and you can restore one cleared by mistake.

Notices that need action also pop up briefly as a toast in the corner. Repeats are grouped into one notice that updates in place. Nexus notifies you about real workflow events, not every small change; if something matters and you got no notice, check the module itself.

## The Yellow Priority Bar

When a notice is holding something up, such as a Time Sheet to sign or a punch fix waiting for your answer, Nexus pins it to the top of every screen as a **yellow bar**. A manager can also raise one for you.

- Tap the text to read the full message.
- Click **Open** to go straight to the item. Acting on it clears the bar.
- With several waiting, **Show All** lists them.

The yellow bar is the one thing in Nexus you never ignore.

## At Greens

Marco taps the yellow bar, clicks **Open** and signs his Time Sheet; the bar goes and Signatures Needed drops to 0. In the bell he reads three updates on his light-out ticket and clicks **Clear All**. Then he realizes he has never seen an announcement, adds the **Announcements** widget, and finds one asking him to acknowledge the new gate hours. He reads it and clicks **Acknowledge**.

## Common Mistakes

- **Never adding the Announcements widget.** It is not on Home by default.
- **Ignoring the yellow bar.** It is always holding up pay, a request or a teammate.
- **Using Clear All as a to-do list.** Clearing hides a notice; it does not do the work.
- **Panicking after Clear All.** Everything is under Closed for 30 days.

## Key Points

- Home shows tiles, Needs Your Attention, My Hours and Quick Actions. Click a tile to jump to it.
- **Customize**, **Add Widget**, **Save**, **Done**. Add **Announcements** on day one and **Acknowledge** posts that ask.
- The bell: click to open, **Mark All Read** clears the count, **Clear All** moves updates to Closed for 30 days.
- The yellow bar means something needs you now. **Open** it and act.
