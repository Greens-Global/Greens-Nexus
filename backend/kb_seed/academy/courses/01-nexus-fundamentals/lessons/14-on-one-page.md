Print this page or keep it open on your phone for your first weeks. Every line is taught in full earlier in this course.

## Every Shift, If You Are Hourly

- [ ] Punch in yourself, with location allowed. Write your Beginning of Day message.
- [ ] Press Start Break and End Break for every break. A Still on Break? notice means you forgot End Break.
- [ ] Punch out with your End of Day message. Never punch again to fix a punch: use Fix My Punch Out or your Time Sheet.

## Every Day

- [ ] Answer the yellow priority bar the moment you see it, then check the bell.

## Every Week

- [ ] If you are hourly, check your Time Sheet. Fix a Missing cell or a wrong break with a request and a real reason.
- [ ] Confirm every newly shared schedule in My Schedule.
- [ ] Add approved sick or vacation time to your Time Sheet yourself.

## Whenever It Comes Up

- [ ] Ask in Nexus, not by text: time off in Workday, HR questions in Ask HR, money back in Reimbursements, new things in Purchase Request.
- [ ] Something broken? A ticket from Help Center or a QR tag, one problem each, with a photo or screenshot.
- [ ] Borrowed equipment: a photo at handover and at return, and always Start Return.
- [ ] Sign your Time Sheet only when it is right. Only you sign it.
- [ ] Lost on a screen? Ctrl+K to search, or ? and Take the Tour.

## Your First Week

- [ ] Add the Announcements widget, install Nexus on your phone with Push to This Device, and fill in Contact & Emergency.
- [ ] Open My Learning and note the due date of every course assigned to you.
