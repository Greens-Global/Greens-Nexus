## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Punch in, start and end a break, and punch out from Workday, the left menu clock or your phone.
- Write the Beginning of Day, break and End of Day messages, and know when you can skip them.
- Understand what Nexus records about your location and what the location words mean.
- Handle the early clock-in question, the break allowance, the long shift banner and a punch that did not record.
- Know how meal and rest breaks work at signing, at a California site and at a Texas site.

## The Four Buttons

The Time Clock is on the **Workday** Overview, and the same clock sits at the foot of the left menu on a computer and in a small pill on a phone. It only ever shows the buttons that make sense right now:

| You are | You see | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Off the clock | **Punch In** | Starts your shift. |
| On the clock | **Start Break** and **Punch Out** | Start Break pauses your paid time. Punch Out ends your day. |
| On a break | **End Break** | Puts you back on the clock. |

After a punch, the clock tells you what happened, for example "Punched in at 8:02 AM." Beside the dial, your hours show with a **Today / This Week / Pay Period** switch.

On the left menu clock, the buttons are icons: Play punches in (or ends a break), Pause starts a break, and Stop punches out.

> [!WARNING]
> Punch for yourself only, and only when you are actually starting, pausing or ending work. Never punch for a coworker and never let anyone punch for you. Every punch is timestamped and tagged with where it was made, and your Time Sheet is a pay record you sign.

## The Message Boxes

At most Greens sites, each punch asks for a short message that posts to your team's Teams chat as you, marked "Sent by Nexus." This keeps your manager and teammates in the loop without a separate text. The four boxes are:

| Punch | Box | What to write | Button |
|---|---|---|---|
| Punch In | **Beginning of Day** | What you plan to work on today. | **Send & start the day** |
| Start Break | **Going on break** | A short reason (required), such as Lunch. | **Start break & notify** |
| End Break | **Resuming work** | An optional note. | **End break & notify** |
| Punch Out | **End of Day** | A summary, tasks completed and pending tasks. | **Send & clock out** |

Two things to know:

- **If you already sent your message another way**, tick "I already sent my login (BOD) message" at the start of the day or "I already sent my logout (EOD) message" at the end, and the punch goes through without posting again.
- **Some roles skip the boxes.** Many field roles are set up so the punch goes straight through with no message. If you never see a box, your role is one of them.

Unless your role skips them, Nexus does not punch you in or out without the message, so write it. One or two lines is plenty. You can also dictate it instead of typing.

## Location at the Punch

When you punch, Nexus checks where you are, **at that moment only**. It does not track you between punches. A punch never waits for your location and is never blocked by it.

What your Time Sheet shows for each punch:

| Shows | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The site name | The punch was made at one of your company's locations. |
| **Out of Location** | The punch was made away from every company location. It is flagged for review. |
| **Approx. Location** | The device gave only a rough location (common on a computer). Punch from your phone for a precise one. |
| **Location Off** | Location was turned off when you punched. |
| **No Location** | No location came through with the punch. |

If you clock in with location turned off, a red **Needs Attention** bar asks you to turn it on, with **Turn On** and **Skip**. **Turn On** asks your phone or browser for your location and adds it to that clock-in. **Skip** keeps the clock-in as Location Off and lets your manager know.

> [!TIP]
> Punch from your phone, on site, with location allowed. A phone gives a precise location, so your punch shows the site name instead of Approx. Location or Out of Location, and nobody has to ask you about it later.

The first time you punch, you may be asked to read and acknowledge a monitoring notice. Read it; it explains what Nexus records.

## Clocking In Early

If you press **Punch In** well before your scheduled shift (the window is set by your company), Nexus asks first with an **Early Clock-In** box: "You're clocking in early - your shift starts at 9:00 AM, about 25 minutes from now. Clock in anyway?"

- **Not Yet** cancels. Come back closer to your start time.
- **Clock In Anyway** records the punch. Nexus never blocks an early punch, but your manager gets a notice about early punches.

Only clock in early when your manager has asked you to start early. Otherwise, choose **Not Yet**.

## Breaks

Press **Start Break** when you step away and **End Break** when you are back. While you are on a break, the clock counts down your allowance, for example "25 min left of your 1h daily break." If you go over, it says so, for example "Break over by 10 min - over your 1h daily allowance."

If a break stays open for a long time (60 minutes by default), you get a **Still on Break?** notice in your bell. If it stays open for twice that, your manager is told. Usually it means you forgot to press **End Break**. Press it now, and if the time is wrong, fix it on your Time Sheet (lesson 8).

## Long Shifts and a Forgotten Punch Out

If you have been clocked in for a long stretch, an amber banner asks: "You've been clocked in for 11 hours - still working, or did you forget to punch out?"

- **I'm Still Working** hides the banner. Use it only when you really are still working.
- **Fix My Punch Out** takes you to your Time Sheet to ask for the right clock-out time.

If a clock-in is never closed and becomes more than 16 hours old, that shift shows as **Missing**. A red bar explains it, with a **Fix My Punch Out** button. Submit a missing punch request with the real clock-out time (lesson 8), then punch in normally for today.

> [!WARNING]
> If you forgot to punch out yesterday, do **not** punch in and out now to "fix" it. That creates a second, wrong shift. Use **Fix My Punch Out** and ask for the real time. Your manager approves it.

## When a Punch Does Not Record

At the back of a lot or in a steel building, your phone may lose signal right as you punch. Nexus is built for this:

- If Nexus could not reach the server but saved your punch on the device, a box titled **Your Punch Did Not Record** explains that the punch is saved on this device at the time you pressed the button, and Nexus keeps trying on its own. It goes in at that time, not whenever it finally gets through. You do not need to do anything. Tap **OK, Keep Trying**, or **Retry Now** once you have signal.
- If the browser could not save it either, retry while you are on that screen. If it still will not go, ask for the punch from your Time Sheet.

In both cases a red line on the clock reminds you the punch is still owed until it lands, and says "Don't punch out to fix it." Punching out would end your day instead.

## Meal and Rest Breaks at Signing

When you sign your Time Sheet (lesson 8), Nexus also handles meal and rest breaks:

| | California site (hourly) | Texas site |
|---|---|---|
| At clock-in | No break question. | No break question. |
| On the signing screen | The statement "I was provided every meal and rest break California law requires." Signing confirms it. | The same statement appears for everyone whose country is the US. Signing confirms it. |
| If you missed a break | Click **Report a Missed Break** instead of signing, pick the days and **Meal** or **Rest**, and click **Send Back to Manager**. Your manager adds the premium pay and sends it back for you to sign. | Report a Missed Break appears only for hourly staff on the California rule. If a break statement on your Time Sheet is not true for you, do not sign it. Tell your manager first; if it is still not true, decline it in Nexus Sign with a reason. |

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

Never sign a statement that is not true. Reporting a missed break is not getting anyone in trouble; it is how the break rules are supposed to work.

## A Greens Scenario

Sam opens the Fairfield office at 7:45 AM. His shift starts at 8:00 AM. He taps **Punch In** on his phone and sees **Early Clock-In**. His manager did not ask him to start early, so he taps **Not Yet**, unlocks the office and walks the gate. At 8:00 AM he taps **Punch In**, writes "Opening, gate check, two move-ins at 10" in **Beginning of Day** and taps **Send & start the day**. The clock reads "Punched in at 8:00 AM," and the punch shows Fairfield.

At noon he taps **Start Break**, types "Lunch" and taps **Start break & notify**. Back at 12:30 PM, he taps **End Break**, then **End break & notify**.

At 4:45 PM he is in the far RV row with no signal and taps **Punch Out**. **Your Punch Did Not Record** appears, saying the punch is saved on the phone. He taps **OK, Keep Trying** and does not punch again. When he walks back to the office, the punch goes in at 4:45 PM by itself.

## Common Mistakes

- **Punching out to fix a missing punch.** It ends your day or creates a wrong shift. Fix it on the Time Sheet.
- **Clocking in early without being asked.** Choose **Not Yet**.
- **Forgetting End Break.** Watch for **Still on Break?** in the bell.
- **Punching from the office computer.** You get Approx. Location. Punch from your phone on site.
- **Signing a break statement that is not true.** Report the missed break, or ask your manager first.

## Key Points

- Only the valid buttons show: **Punch In**, **Start Break**, **End Break**, **Punch Out**.
- Most roles write a short message at each punch: **Send & start the day**, **Start break & notify**, **End break & notify**, **Send & clock out**. Tick the box if you already sent it.
- Location is checked only at the punch and never blocks it. Punch from your phone for a precise location.
- **Clock In Anyway** or **Not Yet** answers the early clock-in question; only start early when asked.
- **Fix My Punch Out** fixes a forgotten clock-out. Never punch again to fix a punch.
- A punch saved on the device sends itself at the time you pressed the button.
- Signing your Time Sheet confirms the meal and rest break statement. Hourly California staff use **Report a Missed Break** instead of signing when a break was missed.
