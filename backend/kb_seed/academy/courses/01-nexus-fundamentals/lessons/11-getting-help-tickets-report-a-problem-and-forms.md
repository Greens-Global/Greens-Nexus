Tasha in HR opens her laptop on Monday and it will not connect to the office Wi-Fi. A text to whoever sits nearest could get lost in a busy day. A ticket with a screenshot reaches the IT team that fixes it, shows when to expect a reply, and tells her when it is done.

## Help Center Is for Everyone

Open **Help Center** from the bottom of the left menu. You do not need the Tickets module; that is the desk side, where teams work tickets. Help Center is your side: a search bar, then these tiles:

| Tile | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Submit a Ticket** | A request to the right team: IT, HR, facilities, maintenance and more. |
| **Documentation** | The written guide to every Nexus screen. |
| **Knowledge Base & Training** | Company SOPs and policies, and your courses. |
| **How Do I...** | Shortcuts that go straight to a screen, such as Request Time Off, with no ticket. |

## Submitting a Ticket

A ticket is the right tool whenever something is broken, missing or needs another team.

1. Open **Help Center** and click **Submit a Ticket** (or **New Ticket** under the bolt in the header).
2. Write a short, specific title: what and where, such as "Laptop will not join office Wi-Fi."
3. Under **What Is Happening?**, say where, since when and what you already tried.
4. Add evidence: **Attach File**, **Record Screen**, Ctrl+V to paste a screenshot, or photos from your phone's camera.
5. Pick the department that needs to help, then what you need help with. If **Which One?** appears, pick the item it is about.
6. Leave the type as **Incident** for something broken, and set the **Priority** honestly.
7. Click **Create Ticket**.

As you type, **Suggested Articles** may appear; if one solves it, click **This Solved My Problem** and nothing is filed. After you submit, **What Happens Next** shows the ticket number, which team has it and when to expect a first reply.

> [!TIP]
> One problem per ticket: a broken laptop and a jammed printer may go to two different people. And always add a screenshot or photo; it saves a phone call.

Your tickets sit in **Open Tickets**, **Tickets I'm Following** and **Closed Tickets**. Until someone replies, a row says **Expect a reply by** with the date and time. Click a row to read the thread and reply.

- **Waiting for User** means the team is stuck until you answer. Reply.
- When it is **Resolved**, check the fix yourself. Then click **Confirm Resolution** and **Done**, and answer the short survey. Not fixed? Click **Reopen** and say why.
- If the problem went away on its own, click **Mark Resolved** and say what fixed it.

## Report a Problem at a Place

Some places and pieces of equipment carry a QR tag. Scan it with your phone's camera and Nexus opens that place: **Open Tickets** shows what is already reported there, and **Report a Problem** opens a short form (**Where**, **Details**, **Photos**, then **Send Report**). It becomes a ticket for the team that looks after that place. If you see **You Do Not Have Access to This Place**, use Submit a Ticket instead.

## Forms

Inspections, incident reports, vehicle checks and checklists are **forms**, used by the teams whose work needs them. When a form is yours to fill, open **Fill a Form** from the Workday action row, the bolt in the header (under **Request**) or your shift card. Click **Send**, then **Fill Another** or **Done**. Someone else reviews your entry; nobody reviews their own.

> [!NOTE]
> Photos are evidence. Take them at the time, of the actual thing, in good light, with something in the shot that shows where it is.

A form sent with no signal shows **Saved on This Phone** and sends itself when signal returns; sending it twice never creates two. The bar at the top says what is waiting:

| The bar says | What to do |
|---|---|
| Offline, or **Waiting to send** | Nothing. **Sync Now** tries right away. |
| Something **not sent** | Tap **Review**: the server says why. Fix it and send again. |
| Something **needs your choice** | Someone changed the same record. Pick **Keep Mine** or **Use Theirs**. |

## At Greens

Tasha clicks **Submit a Ticket**, titles it "Laptop will not join office Wi-Fi," pastes a screenshot of the error with Ctrl+V, picks IT and clicks **Create Ticket**. The next day it reads **Waiting for User**: IT asks whether her other devices connect. She replies. Two days later it is **Resolved**; she checks that the laptop connects, then clicks **Confirm Resolution**.

## Common Mistakes

- **A text instead of a ticket.** Texts get lost; tickets get tracked.
- **No screenshot or photo.**
- **Ignoring Waiting for User.**
- **Confirming before checking the fix.**

## Key Points

- **Help Center** is for everyone: **Submit a Ticket** with a clear title, evidence, the right department and an honest priority.
- Reply when a ticket is **Waiting for User**; **Confirm Resolution** only after you check the fix.
- Scan a QR tag to see **Open Tickets** there and **Report a Problem**.
- Forms send with **Send**; with no signal they wait as **Saved on This Phone** and send themselves.
