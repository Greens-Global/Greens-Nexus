During her afternoon walk at Murrieta, Tasha finds the roll-up door of a vacant unit bent at the bottom. A text to her manager could get lost in a busy day. A ticket with two photos reaches the team that fixes doors, already tied to the building, and tells her when it is done.

## Help Center Is for Everyone

Open **Help Center** from the bottom of the left menu. You do not need the Tickets module; that is the desk side, where teams work tickets. Help Center is your side: a search bar, then these tiles:

| Tile | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Submit a Ticket** | A request to the right team: IT, HR, facilities, maintenance and more. |
| **Documentation** | The written guide to every Nexus screen. |
| **Knowledge Base & Training** | Company SOPs and policies, and your courses. |
| **How Do I...** | Shortcuts that go straight to a screen, such as Request Time Off, with no ticket. |

Below the tiles is the list of your tickets.

## Submitting a Ticket

A ticket is the right tool whenever something is broken, missing or needs another team.

1. Open **Help Center** and click **Submit a Ticket** (or **New Ticket** in the + menu).
2. Write a short, specific title: what and where, such as "Gate 2 keypad not responding."
3. Under **What Is Happening?**, say where, since when and what you already tried.
4. Add evidence: **Attach File**, **Record Screen**, Ctrl+V to paste a screenshot, or photos from your phone's camera.
5. Pick the department that needs to help, then what you need help with. If **Which One?** appears, pick the item it is about.
6. Leave the type as **Incident** for something broken, and set the **Priority** honestly.
7. Click **Create Ticket**.

As you type, **Suggested Articles** may appear; if one solves it, click **This Solved My Problem** and nothing is filed. After you submit, **What Happens Next** shows the ticket number, which team has it and when to expect a first reply.

> [!TIP]
> One problem per ticket: a broken gate and a burned-out light may go to two different people. And always add a photo; it saves a trip and a phone call.

Raising it for a coworker? Pick them as the **Requester**. They get the updates, and you follow it under **Tickets I'm Following**.

Your tickets sit in **Open Tickets**, **Tickets I'm Following** and **Closed Tickets**. Until someone replies, a row says **Expect a reply by** with the date and time. Click a row to read the thread and reply; email and bell updates open the same place.

- **Waiting for User** means the team is stuck until you answer. Reply.
- **Waiting for Vendor** means an outside company, such as the gate vendor, has the next step.
- When it is **Resolved**, check the fix yourself. Then click **Confirm Resolution** and **Done**, and answer the short survey. Not fixed? Click **Reopen** and say why.
- If the problem went away on its own, click **Mark Resolved** and say what fixed it.

A **Closed** ticket stays closed. If the problem comes back later, submit a new one.

## Report a Problem at a Place

Some sites and pieces of equipment carry a QR tag. Scan it with your phone's camera and Nexus opens that place: **Open Tickets** shows what is already reported there, and **Report a Problem** opens a short form (**Where**, **Details**, **Photos**, then **Send Report**). It becomes a ticket for the team that looks after that place, already linked to the property. If you see **You Do Not Have Access to This Place**, use Submit a Ticket instead.

## Forms

Inspections, incident reports, vehicle checks and checklists are **forms**. Open **Fill a Form** from the Workday action row (when a form is yours to fill), the + menu or your shift card. A form can ask for photos, **Stamp My Location**, a QR scan, a rating, yes or no, or a signature drawn with your finger. Click **Send**, then **Fill Another** or **Done**. Someone else reviews your entry; nobody reviews their own.

> [!NOTE]
> Photos are evidence. Take them at the time, of the actual thing, in good light, with the unit number in the shot.

A form sent with no signal shows **Saved on This Phone** and sends itself when signal returns; sending it twice never creates two. The bar at the top says what is waiting:

| The bar says | What to do |
|---|---|
| Offline, or **Waiting to send** | Nothing. **Sync Now** tries right away. |
| Something **not sent** | Tap **Review**: the server says why. Fix it and send again. |
| Something **needs your choice** | Someone changed the same record. Pick **Keep Mine** or **Use Theirs**. |
| Signed out | Tap **Sign In** once you have signal. |

## At Greens

Tasha scans the building's tag. Nothing is reported for that door, so she taps **Report a Problem**, picks the spot, writes "Roll-up door bent at bottom left, will not close fully, vacant unit," adds two photos and taps **Send Report**. Her inspection form, sent from the back fence, shows **Saved on This Phone** and goes through when she reaches the office. Two days later the ticket is **Resolved**; she checks the door, then clicks **Confirm Resolution**.

## Common Mistakes

- **A text instead of a ticket.** Texts get lost; tickets get tracked.
- **A vague title like "Problem."**
- **No photo.**
- **Ignoring Waiting for User.**
- **Confirming before checking the fix.**

## Key Points

- **Help Center** is for everyone: **Submit a Ticket** with a clear title, photos, the right department and an honest priority.
- Reply when a ticket is **Waiting for User**; **Confirm Resolution** only after you check the fix.
- Scan a site tag to see **Open Tickets** there and **Report a Problem**.
- Forms send with **Send**; with no signal they wait as **Saved on This Phone** and send themselves.
