Keisha is on day two in the accounting office. Two hiring forms are waiting for her signature, her emergency contact is blank, and her last name is misspelled on her record. All three are fixed from one page: Workday.

## Workday Is About You

Open **Workday** from the left menu. It holds your own time and your own HR record, and only you see it. Your manager sees your hours and requests in their own screens, never by opening your Workday.

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Your clock, your requests, your profile, your schedule and your documents. |
| **Time Sheet** | Your hours for the pay period, and signing them (Your Time Sheet). |
| **Time Off** | Requesting time off and following your requests (Schedules and Time Off). |
| **Performance** | My Numbers, My Goals, My Reviews and My 1:1s. |

If your job is salaried and exempt from time tracking, Workday shows no Time Clock and usually no Time Sheet tab. If that seems wrong for your job, raise a ticket.

**Documents to Sign** sits at the very top whenever a Nexus Sign document waits for you: an offer letter, hiring packet forms, a policy acknowledgment, an HR letter. Click **Review & Sign**, read it and sign it there. In every HR document **you sign first**; the company never signs before you. Time Sheets are the exception: you sign those on the Time Sheet tab.

**The Time Clock** comes next if you are hourly, with only the buttons that make sense right now and your hours with a **Today / This Week / Pay Period** switch. Punching In, Breaks and Punching Out covers it.

**The action row** under the clock opens the Workday panels:

| Row | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **My Tickets** | Only when maintenance work is assigned to you. |
| **Ask HR** | The confidential HR desk. A red dot means HR updated one of your requests. |
| **Reimbursements** | A claim for something you paid for on the company's behalf. |
| **Purchase Request** | Asking the company to buy something it does not have. |
| **Fill a Form** | Only when there is a form you may fill. |

These panels slide in from the right. A click outside does not close them; it nudges the panel and lights up its close button, so a half-written request is never lost. Close with **Close**, **Cancel** or Escape. Ask HR, Reimbursements and Purchases covers all three.

**Your profile card** shows who you report to, your start date and **Contact & Emergency**.

**My Equipment** lists what is assigned or checked out to you.

**My Schedule** sums up your week (shifts, paid hours, your time off this year, your next shift), then your days. Schedules and Time Off covers it.

**My Documents** holds your signed copies, the files HR filed for you and your **paystubs**, in folders. There is no separate paystubs screen.

## What You Change and What HR Changes

Under **Contact & Emergency**, click **Edit**, change what you need and click **Save**. Keep it current: if something happens to you at work, this is who the company calls.

| You change it yourself | Through Ask HR, Profile Change |
|---|---|
| Personal email and phone | Your name |
| Emergency contact name, relationship and phone | Your job title and department |
| | Your bank details |

Those four affect payroll or your HR record, so open **Ask HR**, **New Request**, pick **Profile Change**, describe it and click **Send to HR**.

## The Performance Tab, Briefly

**Performance** shows My Numbers (tiles Nexus works out from what it already records, such as on-time arrival; time off, sick days, pay and HR requests are never used), My Goals, My Reviews and My 1:1s, and how long you have been with the company. Only you, the people you report to and HR see it. Nothing to do here in your first week.

## At Greens

Keisha clicks **Review & Sign** on both hiring forms; the signed copies appear in **My Documents**. Under Contact & Emergency she clicks **Edit**, adds her sister and clicks **Save**. Her name she cannot edit, so she sends **Ask HR**, **Profile Change**: "My last name is spelled with two Ns."

## Common Mistakes

- **Looking for a Time Sheet in Documents to Sign.** It is signed on the Time Sheet tab.
- **Leaving the emergency contact blank.**
- **Asking a manager to change your bank details.** That is Ask HR, Profile Change.
- **Clicking outside a panel to close it.** Use Close, Cancel or Escape.

## Key Points

- Workday is only yours: **Overview**, **Time Sheet**, **Time Off**, **Performance**.
- **Documents to Sign** waits at the top of Overview. You always sign first.
- The action row opens Ask HR, Reimbursements and Purchase Request as panels.
- You edit Contact & Emergency yourself; name, job, department and bank go through Ask HR.
- **My Documents** holds your signed copies and paystubs.
