Sunday night, Jordan's phone buzzes: next week's schedule was shared, and he now opens on Wednesday instead of closing. He also has a dentist appointment on 11/20/2026 from 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM. Two minutes in Workday covers both, and keeps a record a text never would.

## Your Schedule

Your week is on the **Workday** Overview under **My Schedule**: a line that sums up the week (shifts, paid hours, time off, your next shift), then your days.

| You see | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A shift with times | A shift your manager placed and shared. |
| **Usual Hours** | Your regular hours, on a day with no shared shift. |
| **Request Pending** | You asked to swap, offer or release this shift. |
| Time off | Approved time off that day. |
| On Time, Late, Left Early, No-Show | On a past shift, how your punches matched it. |

The **My Week | Team** switch shows your group's whole week; the same view is **Team**, **Shifts**. You can look but not change anything. Your manager builds the schedule in the Shift Planner.

Your schedule is also how Nexus knows when you are expected, which is why Late and No-Show notices exist. One more reason to punch every shift.

### Confirm a New Schedule

When your manager shares a new or changed schedule, you get a bell and an email, and a banner at the top of My Schedule asks you to have a look and **Confirm**. Read the days that changed and click **Confirm**. Confirm means "I have seen it." If a day does not work, confirm it and then talk to your manager. Your manager can see who has not confirmed.

**Subscribe in another calendar** puts your shifts in your phone's calendar app, and **My Availability** tells your manager about times you cannot work.

## Shift Requests

Swapping, offering and releasing shifts and asking for open shifts are **off by default**; each company decides. If yours has not turned them on, there is no **Request** button under your shifts, and you talk to your manager instead.

When they are on, click **Request** under a shift and pick **Swap**, **Offer** or **Release**, then the teammate. Your requests are under **Workday**, **Time Off**. A request goes to the teammate first (**Waiting On Teammate**), then to a manager (**Waiting On Manager**). Until it is approved, the original shift stands: work the shift you are scheduled for.

Open **Workday**, then **Time Off**. Pick the **Reason** (Personal, Sick, Vacation or Medical Appointment; your company may add others), the **From** and **To** days, turn **All day** off for part of a day, add an optional **Note**, and click **Request**. The form shows what it counts as and, where your company tracks balances, what it would leave you. **Keep this confidential** means your team sees only that you are out, never the note.

Past dates are grayed out. Sick yesterday and could not ask in time? Click **Request for a Past Date**.

Your requests are under **My Requests**: Pending, Approved, Rejected or Canceled. You get a bell when your manager decides. If your company requires HR's second approval, an approved request reads **Awaiting HR Approval** until HR decides. You can cancel a pending or approved request. The tab also shows **My Paid Time Off** (your balances, where set up) and the **Team Leave Calendar**.

## Time Off and Your Pay

Two rules surprise new hourly team members:

1. **Approved time off is not added to your Time Sheet.** Add it yourself: **Add** on the day, Type **Sick** or **Vacation** (Your Time Sheet).
2. **Company holidays are unpaid for hourly staff.** The office is closed and there is no pay; hours you do work on a holiday are paid as usual. Salaried staff follow a separate rule.

## At Greens

Jordan clicks **Confirm** on the new week. For the dentist he picks **Medical Appointment**, sets 11/20/2026, turns **All day** off, sets 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM and clicks **Request**. It comes back Approved. After the appointment he adds the two hours on his Time Sheet so they are paid. A coworker asks to swap Saturday, but there is no Request button at his site, so he asks his manager.

## Common Mistakes

- **Ignoring the Confirm banner.**
- **Treating a swap as done when the teammate agrees.** A manager still approves it.
- **Texting a manager for time off.**
- **Forgetting to add approved leave to the Time Sheet.**

## Key Points

- **My Schedule** on the Overview shows your week. **Confirm** every newly shared schedule.
- Shift requests are off by default; they wait on the teammate, then a manager.
- **Request Time Off**: Reason, From, To, All day, Note, then **Request**. **Request for a Past Date** for a day already gone.
- Approved time off is not added for you. Hourly staff are not paid for company holidays.
