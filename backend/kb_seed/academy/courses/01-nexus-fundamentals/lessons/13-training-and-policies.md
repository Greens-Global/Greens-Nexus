Chris joins the payroll team and gets a bell in his first week: courses for his role are assigned, each with a due date, and a policy is waiting for his signature. He is already partway through, because this course is one of them. Here is how the rest works.

## Where Training Lives

Open **Help Center** and click **Knowledge Base & Training**. Two tabs run across the top: **Library** and **Training** (managers and course authors also see **Manage**). A number on the Knowledge Base & Training card means training or a signature is waiting on you.

**The Library** is how Greens does things, written down: SOPs, policies, guides and reference pages, on shelves called collections, each with an owner who keeps it current. Use **Report a Problem** on a page when something is wrong or out of date. **Needs your signature** at the top lists policies waiting on you.

**Training** has three screens:

| Screen | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **My Learning** | Where you left off, then one to-do list (overdue first, then due soonest, policies to sign included), your paths with their stages, what you completed and your certificates. |
| **Catalog** | Every course and path, by role and department, including ones you may start on your own. |
| **Compliance** | Your own requirements and whether each is done, due or valid. |

A course opens on its home page: what you will learn, the lessons, the checkpoints and the final quiz with its rules.

**Lessons.** Read to the end, then click **Mark Lesson Complete** and **Next**. You can leave and come back; finished lessons stay finished.

**Checkpoints** ask a few questions about what you just read. Click **Check My Answers**. A checkpoint is practice and never uses a quiz try; it tells you what is worth rereading.

**The Final Quiz** card states its rules, for example "20 of 48 questions · 80% to pass · 3 tries." The questions are drawn at random from a bank, so yours differ from a coworker's and from your own last try. Click **Start the Quiz**, answer every question (Submit waits until all are answered) and click **Submit Answers**.

| Result | What happens |
|---|---|
| You passed | Your certificate is issued. **See Your Certificate** or **Download Certificate**. |
| Not quite | **Reread these before you try again** lists the lessons your misses came from. Then **Try Again**. |
| No tries left | Your manager can unlock another round. |

> [!TIP]
> Do not rush to Try Again. The next try draws different questions, so guessing does not work; rereading does.

Some training is proved in person, not by quiz: a manager or senior team member watches you do the task and signs you off. The course shows **In-Person Sign-Off** until then, and **Completed In Person** after.

## Due Dates

Every assigned course has a due date, usually counted from your start date, sometimes one date for everyone. If a course is still not done some days after its due date, Nexus tells your manager, once and then weekly. Keep an eye on My Learning so it never gets that far.

## Read and Sign

Some Library pages are policies you must read and sign. Open one from **Needs your signature**, My Learning or the bell, read it to the end, then under **Read, Then Sign Below** type your full name and sign that version (the button names it, such as Sign v2). Some policies are signed in Nexus Sign instead, and the page says so. A real change to a policy brings it back for a new signature; a typo fix does not.

## What Is Assigned to You

**Training** holds the courses assigned to your role: some for everyone at Greens, like this one, and some for your job and department. Every course issues a certificate when you pass it, and a passed quiz is never taken again. You can reread any lesson whenever you want a refresher.

## At Greens

Chris finishes three lessons on day one and misses a checkpoint question on the bell, so he rereads that part of Your Dashboard and the Bell. At the end of the week he scores 76% on the quiz, reads the lessons listed under **Reread these before you try again**, and passes the next day. He signs the waiting policy under **Read, Then Sign Below** after reading it to the end.

## Common Mistakes

- **Clicking Mark Lesson Complete without reading.** The quiz will find out.
- **Skipping checkpoints.** They are free practice.
- **Retrying right away after a miss.** Reread first.
- **Signing a policy you did not read.**

## Key Points

- Training lives in **Help Center**, **Knowledge Base & Training**: **My Learning**, **Catalog** and **Compliance**.
- Lessons end with **Mark Lesson Complete**; checkpoints are practice and never use a try.
- The final quiz: 80% to pass, three tries, different questions each try. Passing issues a certificate.
- **Read, Then Sign Below**: read to the end, type your name and sign. A real change asks again.
- Training holds the courses assigned to your role, each with its due date.
