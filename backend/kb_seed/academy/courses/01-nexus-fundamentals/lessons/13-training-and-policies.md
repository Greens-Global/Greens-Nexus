## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Find your assigned courses and policies in **My Training**.
- Work through a course: lessons, **Mark Lesson Complete**, checkpoints and the final quiz.
- Explain the difference between a checkpoint and a quiz try, and what happens if you do not pass.
- Get and download your certificate.
- Read and Sign a policy, and know when you will be asked to sign again.
- Describe the six courses of the Greens Storage Academy and the order you take them in.

## Where Training Lives

Open **Help Center** and click **Knowledge Base & Training**. You land on the **Library**. Across the top are the tabs **Library** and **Training** (writers and course owners also see **Manage**). A number on the Knowledge Base & Training card in Help Center means sign-offs or training are waiting on you.

### The Library

The Library is how Greens does things, written down: SOPs, policies, guides and reference pages, on shelves called collections. Each page has an owner who keeps it current and a review date.

- Type in the search box and press Enter to search the pages.
- Pages apply to one or more departments, and you can filter by department.
- At the top, **Needs your signature** lists the policies waiting on you.
- On a page, **Report a Problem** sends a note to the page's owner when something is wrong or out of date.

### My Training

Click the **Training** tab. **My Training** lists everything assigned to you:

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Courses** | Each course with its status (Not Started, In Progress, Completed or Overdue) and due date. |
| **Read and Sign** | Policies you must read and sign. |
| **Compliance** | What you have completed and whether it is valid (Valid Through, or No expiry). |
| **Certificate** | Your certificates, ready to open and download. |

**Certificate Check** confirms whether a certificate is valid.

## Taking a Course

Open a course from **My Training**. The first screen, **What You'll Learn**, lists the lessons, the checkpoints and the final quiz with its rules.

### Lessons

Read each lesson to the end, then click **Mark Lesson Complete** and **Next**. If a lesson has a video, watch it to the end first; until you do, the button waits ("Watch the video to the end to finish this lesson."). Some lessons are Library pages; they open right inside the course.

You can leave and come back at any time. Lessons you marked complete stay complete.

### Checkpoints

Along the way, a **checkpoint** asks a few questions about what you just read. Answer them and click **Check My Answers**. A checkpoint is practice; it does not count as a quiz try. If you miss something, it tells you what is worth rereading. Use checkpoints to find your gaps before the quiz.

### The Final Quiz

At the end is the **Final Quiz**. Its card tells you the rules, for example "25 of 50 questions · 80% to pass · 3 tries." That means:

- The quiz draws 25 questions at random from a bank of 50, so your questions differ from a coworker's and from your own last try.
- You need 80% to pass.
- You have 3 tries.

Click **Start the Quiz**, answer every question (Submit waits until all are answered: "Answer every question to submit."), and click **Submit Answers**.

| Result | What happens |
|---|---|
| You passed | "You passed." Your certificate is issued. Click **See Your Certificate** or **Download Certificate**. |
| Not quite | "Not quite. You need 80%." **Reread these before you try again** lists the lessons to go over. Then click **Try Again**. |
| No tries left | Ask your manager to unlock another round. They get a bell from the course report. |

> [!TIP]
> Do not rush to Try Again. Read the lessons listed under **Reread these before you try again** first. The next try draws different questions, so guessing does not work; understanding does.

### In-Person Sign-Off

Some training is proved in person, not by a quiz. In those courses, a manager or senior team member watches you do the task and signs you off in Nexus. Until they do, the course shows **Waiting for Review**.

### Rating the Course

When you finish, **How Was This Course?** asks for a star rating and an optional comment. Click **Send Feedback**. The owner sees the rating and comments without your name, and uses them to fix what was unclear.

## Due Dates and Reminders

Every assigned course has a due date. For a new hire, it is usually counted from your start date. Sometimes everyone at the company gets one fixed date instead. If a course is still not done some days after its due date, Nexus tells your manager, once and then weekly, until it is done. Keep an eye on the due dates in **My Training** so it never gets that far.

## Read and Sign

Some Library pages are policies you must read and sign, for example a policy on site hours or on handling tenant information.

1. Open the page from **Needs your signature** in the Library, from **Read and Sign** in My Training, or from the bell.
2. Read it to the end.
3. Under **Read, Then Sign Below**, type your full name and click to sign that version (the button shows the version, such as Sign v2).

Some policies are signed in Nexus Sign instead, and the page tells you so.

When a policy changes in a real way, the company publishes a new version and asks everyone to sign again, so you will see it back in Needs your signature. A small fix (a typo, clearer wording) does not ask you to sign again.

## How the Greens Storage Academy Works

You are taking the first course of the **Greens Storage Academy**, the training every new Greens facility team member completes. It is a training plan of six courses, in this order:

| # | Course | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nexus Fundamentals | The portal you use for everything else (this course). |
| 2 | Self-Storage Fundamentals | The vocabulary and rules of the self-storage business. |
| 3 | RV & Boat Storage Fundamentals | Storing RVs, boats and other vehicles. |
| 4 | Cubby Fundamentals | The software for tenants, units and payments. |
| 5 | Customer Service & Sales Fundamentals | Phone calls, tours, move-ins and tough conversations. |
| 6 | Hands-On Check | In person: a mock sales call, a guided site walk and a supervised move-in in Cubby, signed off by a manager or senior team member. |

How it works:

- New facility hires have **30 days from their start date** to finish the plan.
- **Every course issues a certificate when you pass it, and that is the end of it.** The courses do not expire, and a passed quiz is not taken again.
- You can reread any lesson whenever you want a refresher.
- Office and support staff take Nexus Fundamentals too, so the whole company uses Nexus the same way.

## A Greens Scenario

Chris starts at a Texas site and gets a bell: the New Hire: Facility Team plan was assigned, due in 30 days. He opens **Help Center**, **Knowledge Base & Training**, **Training**, and starts Nexus Fundamentals. He finishes three lessons on his first day, clicking **Mark Lesson Complete** after each, and does the Time Clock checkpoint; he misses one question about the early clock-in, so he rereads that part of lesson 6.

At the end of the week he clicks **Start the Quiz**, answers all 25 questions and clicks **Submit Answers**. He scores 76%: "Not quite. You need 80%." He reads the lessons listed under **Reread these before you try again**, comes back the next day, clicks **Try Again** and passes. He clicks **Download Certificate** and moves on to Self-Storage Fundamentals.

He also sees a policy under **Needs your signature**. He reads it to the end, types his full name under **Read, Then Sign Below**, and signs.

## Common Mistakes

- **Clicking Mark Lesson Complete without reading.** The quiz will find out.
- **Skipping checkpoints.** They are free practice and do not use a try.
- **Retrying right away after a fail.** Reread first.
- **Signing a policy you did not read.** Your signature says you read it.
- **Letting a due date pass.** Your manager is told.

## Key Points

- Training lives in **Help Center**, **Knowledge Base & Training**: the **Library** and **My Training**.
- Lessons end with **Mark Lesson Complete**. Checkpoints (**Check My Answers**) are practice and never use a quiz try.
- The final quiz draws questions from a bank: 80% to pass, 3 tries. **Start the Quiz**, **Submit Answers**; if you miss, **Reread these before you try again**, then **Try Again**.
- Passing issues a certificate (**Download Certificate**). Academy certificates do not expire.
- Read and Sign: read to the end, type your full name under **Read, Then Sign Below**, and sign. A real policy change asks you to sign again.
- The Greens Storage Academy is six courses in order, due within 30 days of your start, ending with the in-person Hands-On Check.
