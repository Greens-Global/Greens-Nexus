Your first morning at Escondido. A coworker says, "Check the Tickets tab for the gate repair." You open Nexus and there is no Tickets in your menu. Nothing is broken, and by the end of this lesson you will know why, and where to go instead.

## What Nexus Is

Nexus is the Greens company portal: the one place for your own work life. Your time clock, your Time Sheet, your schedule and time off, your HR requests, the equipment you borrow, the tickets you raise when something breaks, the forms you fill on a walk, and this training. You sign in with your Microsoft work account, the same one you use for Outlook and Teams.

Nexus is not Cubby. Cubby is where tenants, units, payments and gate codes live. Nexus is where you, the employee, live.

| Question | Where you go |
|---|---|
| Is unit 214 paid up? | Cubby |
| What is the gate code for a new tenant? | Cubby |
| Did I punch in this morning? | Nexus |
| Who fixes a broken gate motor? | Nexus (a ticket) |
| When is my next shift? | Nexus |

Nexus does three things a paper sheet and a text message cannot:

1. **One sign-in** opens everything.
2. **The work comes to you.** A document to sign, a schedule to confirm or a reply on your ticket shows up in your bell.
3. **Everything leaves a record.** Every punch is timestamped and every request shows who has it. That record protects you as much as the company.

## Tiers: How Far Your View Reaches

Every person in Nexus has a tier. It sets what you can approve and how far your view reaches.

| Level | Tier | In plain words |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Employee | Your own work: punches, Time Sheet, requests, tickets, training. |
| 2 | Supervisor | Your own work, plus some team screens a grant opens. |
| 3 | Manager | Approves for their team and builds its schedule. |
| 4 | Administrator | Runs one company inside Nexus. |
| 5 | Super Administrator | Everything, across every company. |

Most site team members are Employees. That says nothing about how important your job is. It means Nexus shows you your own work and keeps other people's private information out of your way.

## Grants: Why Menus Differ

On top of your tier, your job role and any Access Groups you belong to open extra modules. Each one is a **grant**. The desk side of Tickets, where the teams work tickets, is a grant. Tasks is a grant. So two Employees at the same site can have different menus.

> [!TIP]
> A module a coworker talks about and you cannot see is a grant you do not have. If you need it for your job, ask your manager or raise a ticket asking for access.

Some modules are open to everyone:

- **Dashboard**: your home page.
- **Workday**: your clock, Time Sheet, time off, schedule and HR requests.
- **Team**: your group's shifts and the Directory.
- **Item Management**: borrow equipment and see what is assigned to you.
- **Help Center**: raise tickets and reach Knowledge Base & Training.

## The Mark Beside a Name

Wherever Nexus shows a person, it shows their **job title** under the name, such as "Site Manager." It never prints the tier as words. A small mark beside the name shows the higher tiers instead:

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A filled shield | Super Administrator |
| An outline shield | Administrator |
| A small people icon | Manager |
| No mark | Employee or Supervisor |

## What Nexus Asks of You

From your first day, Nexus expects six habits. Each has its own lesson in this course:

- Punch in and out yourself, every shift (Punching In, Breaks and Punching Out).
- Check your Time Sheet weekly and sign it when it is approved (Your Time Sheet).
- Confirm your schedule when a new one is shared (Schedules and Time Off).
- Ask for things in Nexus, not by text (Ask HR, Reimbursements and Purchases).
- Report problems with a ticket or a form, with photos (Getting Help: Tickets and Forms).
- Finish your training by its due date (Training and Policies).

## At Greens

Back to Escondido. The gate ticket your coworker mentioned lives on the desk side of Tickets, which you do not have. You do not need it: **Help Center**, which everyone has, lets you raise your own ticket and follow the ones raised for you. You also notice an outline shield beside the name of the person who approves your Time Sheet, so you know they are an Administrator.

## Common Mistakes

- **Thinking a missing module means Nexus is broken.** It is a grant you do not have.
- **Asking by text for things Nexus handles.** A text leaves no record and can be missed.
- **Looking in Nexus for a tenant's balance.** Tenants and units are Cubby.
- **Calling a tier by an old name.** The words are Employee, Supervisor, Manager, Administrator and Super Administrator.

## Key Points

- Nexus is the Greens portal for your own work. Cubby is for tenants and units.
- Five tiers set how far your view reaches. Grants open extra modules on top.
- A missing module is a missing grant: ask your manager or raise a ticket.
- Under a name Nexus shows the job title; a small mark shows a Manager, Administrator or Super Administrator.
