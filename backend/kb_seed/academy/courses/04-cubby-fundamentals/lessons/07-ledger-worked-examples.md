## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Work out a prorated first month at a first-of-month site, and check it against Cubby's Preview Charges.
- Work out what a tenant pays at move-in on or after jump day 26.
- Explain why Greens' first bill is on the 1st, not on the move-in anniversary.
- Follow a partial payment through the ledger, oldest charge first.
- Follow a prepayment month by month, with the paid-through date.
- Work out Greens' late fee for any rent, and what a waive does to the balance.
- Show how a discount appears on the ledger.
- Put open charges in the right aging bucket and count days past due.

## Before You Start

Every rent in this lesson is **made up** for practice. None of it is tenant data. The Greens settings used are real: first-of-month billing, jump day 26, the $25.00 Administration Fee, Greens' protection plan prices and Greens' late fee rule (all From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026).

> [!WARNING]
> The proration formula in these examples (monthly rent times days used, divided by days in the month, rounded to the cent) is our best reading of how Cubby works. Check against a real Preview Charges before you quote a tenant. Whether the protection plan is prorated at move-in the same way is also something to check on a real Preview Charges.

The proration formula, written out:

**Prorated rent = monthly rent x days from move-in to the end of the month (counting both days) / days in the month.**

November 2026 has 30 days, December 31, January 31 and February 2027 has 28.

## Example 1: A Prorated Move-In

**Escondido.** A customer rents a 10x10 at a made-up rate of **$150.00** a month and takes the **$2,500 protection plan at $9.00** a month. Move-in is **11/18/2026**.

**Days used in November:** 11/18 through 11/30 = 13 days.

| Line | Arithmetic | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Prorated rent | 150.00 x 13 / 30 | $65.00 |
| Prorated protection (check on Preview Charges) | 9.00 x 13 / 30 | $3.90 |
| Administration Fee | one time | $25.00 |
| **Due at move-in** | 65.00 + 3.90 + 25.00 | **$93.90** |

- **Paid-through date after the payment:** 11/30/2026.
- **First regular bill:** 12/01/2026, for 150.00 + 9.00 = **$159.00**, charged to the autopay card.

What to tell the tenant: "Today you pay for the 13 days left in November plus the one-time $25 admin fee. From December 1st it's $159 on the 1st of every month."

## Example 2: A Jump-Day Move-In

Same unit, same plan, but move-in is **11/27/2026**, which is on or after jump day 26.

**Days used in November:** 11/27 through 11/30 = 4 days.

| Line | Arithmetic | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Prorated rent, November | 150.00 x 4 / 30 | $20.00 |
| Prorated protection, November (check on Preview Charges) | 9.00 x 4 / 30 | $1.20 |
| December rent | full month | $150.00 |
| December protection | full month | $9.00 |
| Administration Fee | one time | $25.00 |
| **Due at move-in** | 20.00 + 1.20 + 150.00 + 9.00 + 25.00 | **$205.20** |

- **Paid-through date:** 12/31/2026.
- **First regular bill:** 01/01/2027.

Without jump day 26 the tenant would have paid $46.20 today and then $159.00 four days later. Jump day avoids that. If a tenant is surprised by a large move-in total near the end of the month, show them the line for next month: they are paying December now, not twice.

## Example 3: Anniversary Billing, for Contrast (Not Greens)

Some storage companies bill on the tenant's **move-in anniversary**. Greens does not, but customers who rented elsewhere may expect it.

Same $150.00 unit, move-in **11/18/2026**, at an anniversary-billing company:

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| First month's rent, in full | $150.00 |
| Paid-through date | 12/17/2026 |
| Next bill | 12/18/2026, $150.00 |

At Greens, the same customer pays $65.00 of rent at move-in (Example 1) and then $150.00 on 12/01. If a new tenant says "I thought my rent was due on the 18th," explain first-of-month billing: every tenant pays on the 1st, and the first month was shortened to match.

## Example 4: A Partial Payment

**Valley Center.** A tenant rents at a made-up **$120.00** with the **$2,500 plan at $14.00**. On 12/01/2026 rent and protection post: **$134.00**. Autopay is declined, so nothing posts for the payment.

On **12/03** the tenant pays **$100.00** at the counter.

Cubby applies the payment oldest charge first. Rent and protection posted at the same time, so rent comes first:

| Charge | Amount | Paid by the $100 | Still open |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rent, December | $120.00 | $100.00 | $20.00 |
| Protection, December | $14.00 | $0.00 | $14.00 |
| **Total** | **$134.00** | **$100.00** | **$34.00** |

- **Paid-through date:** stays 11/30/2026, because December is not fully paid.
- On **12/11** the tenant still owes $34.00, so the late fee posts. Rent is $120.00, which is $100 or more, so the fee is the greater of $20.00 or 15% of $120.00 ($18.00): **$20.00**.
- New balance: 34.00 + 20.00 = **$54.00**.

The lesson: tell a tenant making a partial payment exactly what is still open and what happens on the 11th. "You've paid $100 today; $34 is still open, and if it isn't paid by the 10th a $20 late fee posts on the 11th."

## Example 5: A Prepayment

**Temecula.** A parking tenant pays a made-up **$180.00** a month and carries their own insurance (no Greens plan). They are paid through **11/30/2026**. On **11/15** they pay **$540.00** to cover three months.

There are no open charges on 11/15, so the whole $540.00 becomes **prepaid**. The paid-through date stays **11/30**: prepaid money does not move it until charges post.

| Date | What happens | Prepaid balance | Paid-through date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11/15/2026 | $540.00 payment, no open charges, all to prepaid | $540.00 | 11/30/2026 |
| 12/01/2026 | Rent $180.00 posts, paid from prepaid | $360.00 | 12/31/2026 |
| 01/01/2027 | Rent $180.00 posts, paid from prepaid | $180.00 | 01/31/2027 |
| 02/01/2027 | Rent $180.00 posts, paid from prepaid | $0.00 | 02/28/2027 |
| 03/01/2027 | Rent $180.00 posts, normal billing resumes | $0.00 | 02/28/2027 until paid |

Two things to tell the tenant:

1. "Your account will show paid through November 30th until December's rent posts. That's normal; the money is there."
2. "If your rent changes while you're prepaid, the prepaid money runs out sooner." With a $10.00 increase from 01/01 (rent $190.00), the prepaid goes $540.00, then $360.00 after December, then $170.00 after January, and February leaves $190.00 - $170.00 = $20.00 due on 02/01.

## Example 6: A Late Fee and a Waive

**Murrieta.** A parking tenant pays a made-up **$160.00** a month, own insurance. On 12/01/2026 rent posts and autopay is declined.

| Date | What happens | Balance |
|---|---|---|
| 12/01 | Rent $160.00 posts; autopay declined (nothing posts for it) | $160.00 |
| 12/02 | Task "2nd - Call Failed Autopay Tenants." You call; voicemail. You note it | $160.00 |
| 12/06 | Task "6th - Call Past Due Tenants." You call; no answer. You note it | $160.00 |
| 12/11 | CA Late Fee posts: the greater of $20.00 or 15% of $160.00 ($24.00) = **$24.00**. Cubby also changes the gate code that night (what Cubby does today; under review with counsel) | $184.00 |
| 12/12 | The tenant calls. It is their first late payment. The waive is inside your limit: you waive the $24.00 with the reason "first late payment, goodwill" | $160.00 |
| 12/12 | The tenant pays $160.00 by card | $0.00 |

- **Paid-through date:** 12/31/2026.
- Before they hang up, check that their gate code works again.
- If the tenant had paid on 12/16 instead, the CA Preliminary Lien Notice fee of $25.00 (posted on the 15th) would also be on the balance: 160.00 + 24.00 + 25.00 = $209.00 before any waive.

Whether you may waive, and how much, is on **Greens Waivers, Credits and Refunds**. If the waive is above your limit, say "Let me ask my manager today and call you back," and do not promise it.

## Example 7: A Discount

**Escondido.** A new tenant rents a unit at a made-up **$140.00** and carries their own insurance. Move-in is **11/18/2026**. They get an example promotion, **"First 2 Months 50% Off,"** which starts the month after move-in (the prorated first days are full price).

**At move-in:**

| Line | Arithmetic | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Prorated rent | 140.00 x 13 / 30 = 60.666..., rounded | $60.67 |
| Administration Fee | one time | $25.00 |
| **Due at move-in** | 60.67 + 25.00 | **$85.67** |

**The next three bills:**

| Bill | Rent line | Discount line | Net due |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12/01/2026 | $140.00 | -$70.00 | $70.00 |
| 01/01/2027 | $140.00 | -$70.00 | $70.00 |
| 02/01/2027 | $140.00 | none | $140.00 |

Notice that the discount is **its own line**. The rent is still $140.00 on the ledger. When the tenant asks in February why the bill doubled, show them the two discount lines and the end of the promotion. Check the rounding of $60.67 against a real Preview Charges.

Which promotions are live at your site, and who may give one, is set by your manager.

## Example 8: Aging Buckets and Days Past Due

**Fairfield.** A parking tenant pays a made-up **$130.00** a month, own insurance. Nothing has been paid since September. **Today is 11/02/2026.** The paid-through date is **09/30/2026**.

**Days past due:** from 09/30 to 10/31 is 31 days, plus 2 more to 11/02 = 33. Minus 1 = **32 days past due**.

**Open charges, and how old each is:**

| Charge | Posted | Age on 11/02 | Aging bucket | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| October rent | 10/01 | 32 days | 30-60 | $130.00 |
| CA Late Fee (greater of $20.00 or $19.50) | 10/11 | 22 days | 0-30 | $20.00 |
| CA Preliminary Lien Notice | 10/15 | 18 days | 0-30 | $25.00 |
| November rent | 11/01 | 1 day | 0-30 | $130.00 |
| **Total** | | | | **$305.00** |

**By bucket:** 0-30 = 20.00 + 25.00 + 130.00 = **$175.00**; 30-60 = **$130.00**. Together: **$305.00**.

What this tells you:

- On the per-charge Aged Receivables report this tenant appears in **two** buckets.
- On a collections view by days late, the whole lease is 32 days past due.
- The 30th - Final Collection Call has already been due. Next, around day 35, Cubby's "35th - CA Lien Notice" task appears and the $35.00 CA Lien Notice fee posts (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). The full ladder is on **Greens Delinquency Timeline**.
- A payment of $175.00 today would pay the oldest charges first: October rent ($130.00), then the late fee ($20.00), then the notice fee ($25.00). November rent would still be open, so the lease would still be past due.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Quoting a proration from memory.** Run the move-in charges preview and read the number.
2. **Forgetting next month on a jump-day move-in.** On or after the 26th, the next full month is due today.
3. **Saying a partial payment "stops" the late fee.** Only payment in full does.
4. **Telling a prepaid tenant they are behind** because their paid-through date has not moved yet.
5. **Calling a discount a "lower rent."** The rent stays the same; the discount is its own line.
6. **Counting days past due from the 1st without the grace day.** Use today minus the paid-through date, minus 1.

## Key Points

- Prorated rent = monthly rent x days used / days in the month. Check against a real Preview Charges.
- On or after the 26th, the tenant pays the rest of this month plus all of next month at move-in.
- Greens bills on the 1st, not on the move-in anniversary.
- Payments go to the oldest charge first; rent before protection when they posted together.
- Prepaid money moves the paid-through date one month at a time, as charges post.
- Greens' late fee for rent of $100 or more is the greater of $20.00 or 15% (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026).
- Discounts are their own line. Aging sorts each open charge by its age.
