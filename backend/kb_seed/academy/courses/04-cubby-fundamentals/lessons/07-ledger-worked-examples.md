A customer at the Escondido counter is about to pay $210.87 for a unit she was quoted at $150 a month, and she is frowning. You can explain every cent before she asks, because you know how Cubby builds a move-in total. Every rent in this lesson is made up; the rules are Greens' real settings.

## How Cubby Prorates

Cubby prorates rent **and** the protection plan the same way, rounded to the cent:

**Prorated amount = monthly amount x days from move-in to month end (counting both days) / days in the month.**

November 2026 has 30 days; December and January have 31. Cubby shows the move-in total on the right of the move-in screen before you create the rental. Always read that total to the customer rather than working it from memory; the arithmetic here is so you can check it and explain it.

## Example 1: A Prorated Move-In

**Escondido.** A 10x10 at **$150.00** a month with a **$14.00** protection plan. Move-in **11/18/2026**: 11/18 through 11/30 is 13 days.

| Line | Arithmetic | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rent, November | 150.00 x 13 / 30 | $65.00 |
| Protection, November | 14.00 x 13 / 30 = 6.066... | $6.07 |
| Administration Fee | one time | $25.00 |
| **Due at move-in** | | **$96.07** |

- **Paid through:** 11/30/2026.
- **First regular bill:** 12/01/2026, $150.00 + $14.00 = **$164.00**.

Say it plainly: "Today you pay for the 13 days left in November plus the one-time $25 admin fee. From December 1st it's $164 on the 1st of every month."

## Example 2: A Jump-Day Move-In

Same unit and plan, move-in **11/27/2026**, on or after jump day 26: 11/27 through 11/30 is 4 days.

| Line | Arithmetic | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rent, November | 150.00 x 4 / 30 | $20.00 |
| Protection, November | 14.00 x 4 / 30 = 1.866... | $1.87 |
| Rent, December | full month | $150.00 |
| Protection, December | full month | $14.00 |
| Administration Fee | one time | $25.00 |
| **Due at move-in** | | **$210.87** |

- **Paid through:** 12/31/2026. **First regular bill:** 01/01/2027.

Without jump day, she would pay $46.87 today and $164.00 four days later. If a move-in total near month end surprises someone, point to the December lines: she is paying December now, not twice.

A customer who rented elsewhere may expect to pay on her move-in day each month ("anniversary" billing). At Greens everyone pays on the 1st; the first month is shortened to match.

## Example 3: A Promotion

**Escondido.** A unit at **$140.00**, the tenant's own insurance, move-in **11/18/2026**, with a promotion that in this example takes 50% off the next two full months.

**At move-in:**

| Line | Arithmetic | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rent, November | 140.00 x 13 / 30 = 60.666... | $60.67 |
| Administration Fee | one time | $25.00 |
| **Due at move-in** | | **$85.67** |

**The next three bills:**

| Bill | Rent line | Discount line | Net due |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12/01/2026 | $140.00 | -$70.00 | $70.00 |
| 01/01/2027 | $140.00 | -$70.00 | $70.00 |
| 02/01/2027 | $140.00 | none | $140.00 |

The discount is **its own line**, named after the program; the rent stays $140.00 on the ledger. When the tenant asks in February why the bill doubled, show her the two discount lines and where the promotion ended. Which promotions are live at your site, and who may give one, your manager sets.

## Check It Yourself

| Move-in | What to look for in Cubby's total |
|---|---|
| Before the 26th | This month's days only, plus the Administration Fee |
| On or after the 26th | This month's days **and** all of next month |
| With a Greens plan | A protection line beside each rent line, prorated the same way |
| With a promotion | A discount line of its own |
| A mailbox | Mailbox Administration Fee instead of the Administration Fee |

The current fee amounts are on Greens Fees and Charges.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Quoting a proration from memory.** Read Cubby's move-in total.
2. **Forgetting next month on a jump-day move-in.**
3. **Leaving protection out of the proration.** It is prorated like rent.
4. **Calling a discount a lower rent.**

## Key Points

- Prorated rent and protection = monthly amount x days used / days in the month, to the cent.
- On or after the 26th, the tenant pays the rest of this month plus all of next month.
- Everyone pays on the 1st; the first month is shortened, not discounted.
- A discount is its own line; the rent on the ledger does not change.
