It is your first morning at the Escondido counter. A tenant asks why her first bill was only $41, a caller wants a trailer space, and the screen in front of you shows a task called "2nd - Call Failed Autopay Tenants." All three answers live in Cubby. This lesson gives you the map before the How-To pages give you the clicks.

## What Cubby Is

Cubby is the software Greens runs its storage sites on. Every unit and parking space, every tenant, lease, charge and payment lives in it. When a customer rents online, Cubby takes the payment. On the 1st it posts rent and runs autopay. When a tenant falls behind, it posts the late fee and puts the collection calls on the task list.

Cubby runs in a web browser (app.cubbystorage.com) and in Cubby's phone app. It updates itself, so a button can move or be renamed from one month to the next; this course teaches the ideas first and keeps the clicks on short Cubby How-To pages.

Greens moved to Cubby in 09/2026. Leases brought over from the old system show "imported" or "old system" lines on their ledgers. That is normal.

## The Words Cubby Uses

| Cubby word | What it means at Greens |
|---|---|
| **Unit** | One rentable space: a storage unit, a parking space or a mailbox |
| **Pricing group** | All units of one kind and size, sharing one set of rates, such as "10' x 10' Storage - Interior, Lower" |
| **Contact** | A person, tenant or lead. One contact can hold several leases |
| **Lease** (a "rental" on some screens) | One rental of one unit by one contact. Charges, payments, gate code, documents and coverage belong to it |
| **Coverage** | Tenant protection or the tenant's own insurance on a lease |
| **Ledger** | The running record of every charge and payment on a lease |

Greens' five Cubby sites are Escondido, Fairfield, Murrieta, Temecula and Valley Center, all in California. Cubby counts storage units and parking spaces separately, and counts mailboxes as units; the current counts per site are on Greens by the Numbers. The Texas sites are run by CubeSmart on CubeSmart's own system and never appear in Cubby.

## First-of-Month Billing and Jump Day 26

Every Greens site bills on the **1st of the month** with a **jump day of 26**.

- **First of the month.** Rent is due on the 1st for every tenant, whatever day they moved in. A tenant who moves in on the 18th pays only for the days left in this month, then pays on the 1st like everyone else.
- **Jump day 26.** A tenant who moves in **on or after the 26th** pays the few days left in this month **plus all of next month** at move-in. Their first regular bill is the 1st of the month after next.

| Move-in date | Rent paid at move-in | First regular bill |
|---|---|---|
| 11/18 | 11/18 to 11/30 | 12/01 |
| 11/26 | 11/26 to 11/30, plus all of December | 01/01 |

Cubby prorates the protection plan the same way it prorates rent. The arithmetic is in Ledger Worked Examples: Move-Ins. Some companies bill on each tenant's move-in "anniversary" instead; Greens does not, so a small first payment is the proration, not a discount.

## What Cubby Does, and What You Do

| Cubby does by itself | You do |
|---|---|
| Posts rent and coverage on the 1st and runs autopay | Answer questions about the bill |
| Makes the "2nd" task for every declined autopay | Make the call and note it |
| Posts the late fee and the lien notice fees on their days | Decide, within the waiver policy, whether a waive is right |
| Flags units that need an overlock | Put the overlock on and record it |
| Logs every change on the lease, with who and when | Write the note that explains why |

The days Cubby runs each collection step are on Greens Delinquency Timeline.

## Who Can Do What

Each person has a role, and the role's permissions decide which buttons you see. For example, "Can waive charges" lets you waive, and Cubby asks nobody to approve it. That is why Greens has its own limits on Greens Waivers, Credits and Refunds: the software lets you; the policy decides whether you should. If a button on a How-To page is missing for you, the likely reason is your role. Ask your manager; never borrow someone's sign-in, because every action carries the name of the person signed in. Every lease's **Activity** record shows each change, who made it and when.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Trusting memory over the How-To page.** Cubby changes.
2. **Explaining a small first payment as a discount.** It is the proration.
3. **Doing by hand what Cubby does by itself**, such as typing a late fee.

## Key Points

- Cubby holds every unit, lease, charge, payment and task; it runs in a browser and a phone app.
- Help comes from the Cubby How-To pages, Cubby's help articles, then your manager.
- Every Greens site bills on the 1st with jump day 26; rent and protection are prorated.
- Cubby posts charges and raises tasks; you make the calls, apply overlocks and write notes.
- Your role's permissions decide what you can press; Greens' policy decides what you should.
