## What Cubby Is

Cubby is the software Greens uses to run its storage sites. Every unit and parking space, every tenant, every lease, every charge and every payment lives in it. When a customer rents online, Cubby takes the payment. When rent is due on the 1st, Cubby posts the charge and runs autopay. When a tenant falls behind, Cubby posts the late fee and puts the collection calls on your task list.

Cubby is a **cloud system**. Nothing is installed on the office computer. You sign in through a web browser at app.cubbystorage.com, and the same account works on a phone through Cubby's app, which can also send push notifications. Because it is cloud software, Cubby changes over time without anyone at Greens installing an update. A button can move or get a new name between one month and the next. That is why this course teaches the ideas first and keeps the click-by-click steps on short Cubby How-To pages, which are easy to update.

Cubby describes itself as an operating system for self-storage. In plain words: it is the office, the filing cabinet, the cash register and the to-do list in one place.

> [!NOTE]
> Greens moved to Cubby in 09/2026. The first lease made in Cubby itself started on 09/14/2026; every lease before that was brought over from the old system (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). You will see "imported" balances and paperwork on older leases. That is normal.

## Getting Help With Cubby

Cubby has **no public help center**. There is no online manual you can search. Help comes from three places, in this order:

1. **The Cubby How-To pages** in the Greens Storage Academy collection. Each page is one task, with numbered steps and space for screenshots from Greens' own Cubby.
2. **Your manager or Area Manager**, who knows how Greens has set Cubby up.
3. **Cubby support**, through Greens' Cubby customer success manager (CSM) or support@cubbystorage.com. Your manager decides who contacts Cubby support for your site.

> [!TIP]
> When you take a screenshot to ask for help, crop out tenant names, phone numbers and card details. A screenshot of a tenant's account is a tenant record.

## Cubby's Product Areas

Cubby is sold as a set of product areas. You will use some every hour and some never.

| Area | What it does | How often you use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Facility Operations** | Unit tracking (occupied, vacant, needs attention), the visual site map, the shared task list, walkthroughs from a phone, gate access, lock management | Every shift |
| **Tenant Management** | Leads, reservations, move-ins, move-outs, payments, autopay, payment links, documents and e-signatures, messages, collections, liens, auctions, the tenant portal | Every shift |
| **E-Commerce** | Online rentals on Greens' website, and following up on online rentals that were started and not finished | Every day, as leads |
| **Revenue Management** | Pricing recommendations, competitor tracking and rate increases for existing tenants | Managers |
| **Calls and Comms** | Call routing, recording and an AI voice agent that can answer calls | As Greens sets it up |
| **Reporting** | Reports on demand or on a schedule: General Ledger, Aged Receivables, Rent Roll, Merchant Services Disbursements | Open, close and month end |
| **Workflows** | Rules that run by themselves: when something happens, Cubby sends a message, creates a task or sets an exemption | You see the tasks they create |

The tenant has their own side of Cubby too: a **self-service tenant portal** with passwordless sign-in, where tenants pay, view their lease and manage their account.

## The Words Cubby Uses

Cubby has its own vocabulary. Learn these and every screen makes more sense.

| Cubby word | What it means at Greens |
|---|---|
| **Unit** | One rentable space: a storage unit, a parking space or a mailbox. Cubby calls a parking space a unit too |
| **Pricing group** | The "unit type": all units of the same kind and size, sharing one set of rates. Greens' names look like "10' x 10' Storage - Interior, Lower" or "12' x 32' Parking - Gravel, Drive-In" |
| **Street rate** | The standard rate for a pricing group. A pricing group can also have a web rate and a walk-in rate |
| **Contact** | A person: a tenant or a lead. One contact can have several leases |
| **Lead** | A person who asked about renting and has not rented yet. Cubby's activity log calls a lead an "Interest" |
| **Reservation** | A unit held for a future move-in |
| **Lease** | One rental of one unit by one contact. Charges, payments, the gate code, the documents and the coverage all belong to the lease |
| **Move-in** | Starting a lease. In Cubby's developer documents a move-in is called a "checkout" |
| **Coverage** | Cubby's word for tenant protection or insurance on a lease |
| **Ledger** | The running record of every charge and payment on a lease |
| **Paid-through date** | The last day the lease is paid up to |
| **Task** | A to-do on the shared task list. Some you create; most collection tasks are created by workflows |
| **Workflow** | A rule that runs by itself, such as "on the 2nd, create a call task for every failed autopay" |

## First-of-Month Billing and Jump Day 26

This is the single most important setting to understand, because it shapes every move-in and every bill.

All five Greens sites bill on the **first of the month** with a **jump day of 26** (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026).

**First of the month.** Rent is due on the 1st for every tenant, whatever day they moved in. A tenant who moves in on the 18th does not pay a full month; they pay only for the days left in this month (a "prorated" first month), and from then on rent is due on the 1st like everyone else.

**Jump day 26.** A tenant who moves in **on or after the 26th** pays for the few days left in this month **plus the whole next month** at move-in. Without this, someone moving in on the 29th would pay for three days and then get a full bill two days later. With it, their first regular bill comes on the 1st of the month after next.

| Move-in date | What the tenant pays at move-in (rent part) | First regular bill |
|---|---|---|
| 11/03 | Rent for 11/03 to 11/30 | 12/01 |
| 11/18 | Rent for 11/18 to 11/30 | 12/01 |
| 11/26 | Rent for 11/26 to 11/30, plus all of December | 01/01 |
| 11/29 | Rent for 11/29 to 11/30, plus all of December | 01/01 |

How Cubby works out the days (the exact formula and rounding) is in the Ledger Worked Examples lesson, with worked numbers. Always check the formula against a real Preview Charges in Cubby, which shows the exact amounts before the tenant pays.

> [!NOTE]
> Some storage companies bill on each tenant's move-in "anniversary" instead (move in on the 18th, pay on the 18th every month). Greens does not. If a new tenant asks why their first payment was small, the answer is first-of-month billing.

## What Cubby Does by Itself, and What It Hands to You

Cubby does a lot on its own. Knowing what it does keeps you from doing it twice, and knowing what it hands you keeps things from falling through.

| Cubby does by itself | You do |
|---|---|
| Posts rent and coverage on the 1st | Answer questions about the bill |
| Runs autopay on the saved card or bank account | Call the tenants whose autopay failed (the "2nd" task) |
| Posts the late fee on the 11th (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026) | Decide, within the waiver policy, whether a waive is right |
| Posts the lien notice fees and raises the collection tasks | Make the calls, mail or check the notices, record what happened |
| Flags a unit as needing an overlock | Put the overlock on and mark it applied |
| Turns abandoned online rentals into leads | Call the lead back |
| Keeps an activity log of every change, with who and when | Write notes that explain why |

The calendar of collection steps Cubby runs for Greens is on **Greens Delinquency Timeline**. You will meet it again in the delinquency lessons.

## Who Can Do What

Cubby gives each person a role, by site and by portfolio. What you can see and press depends on your role. For example, waiving a charge or issuing a credit needs the **"Credits and Waives"** permission, and Cubby does not ask anyone to approve it once you have it. That is why Greens has its own waiver limits (on **Greens Waivers, Credits and Refunds**): the software will let you, but the policy decides whether you should.

If you cannot find a button a How-To page describes, the most likely reason is your role. Ask your manager rather than borrowing someone else's sign-in. Every action in Cubby is recorded under the name of the person signed in.

## The Activity Log

Every lease has a timeline of what happened to it: lease created, gate code changed, autopay changed, late fee charged, overlock needed, rent change scheduled, payment taken. Each entry shows who did it and when, or that Cubby did it by itself.

Use the activity log when a tenant says "I never got charged that" or "someone changed my code." It answers most "what happened?" questions in a minute.

## A Day at Greens, Through Cubby

**Temecula, 8:00 AM.** You sign in. The Home page shows the tasks due today, the documents with issues and the liens to initiate. Two tasks are "2nd - Call Failed Autopay Tenants," because today is the 2nd.

**9:15 AM.** A caller wants a 30 ft space for a trailer. You create a lead, choose the real source ("Call"), quote the rate and set a follow-up task.

**11:00 AM.** A walk-in rents a space. You run the move-in, check the move-in charges preview, take the payment, enable autopay, and get the lease signed and the vehicle details entered.

**2:00 PM.** A tenant whose autopay failed calls back. You send a payment link by text. The payment arrives a few minutes later, and Cubby closes the open charge.

**5:30 PM.** Before you leave, you check that every task due today is completed with a note, that the cash and checks you took match Cubby, and that nothing is waiting on you.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Trusting memory over the How-To page.** Cubby changes. Check the page.
2. **Choosing "Other" as a lead source.** It is the most common source at Greens today (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026), and it tells the company nothing about where customers come from.
3. **Explaining first-of-month billing wrong.** A small first payment is the proration, not a discount.
4. **Doing by hand what Cubby does by itself**, such as typing a late fee.
5. **Sharing a sign-in.** Every action carries the name of the person signed in.

## Key Points

- Cubby is Greens' cloud system for units, leases, charges, payments, tasks and reports.
- There is no public help center: use the Cubby How-To pages, your manager, then Cubby support.
- Greens' five Cubby sites are all in California: Escondido, Fairfield, Murrieta, Temecula and Valley Center. Texas sites join later.
- Every site bills on the 1st with jump day 26 (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026).
- Cubby posts charges, runs autopay and raises tasks by itself; you make the calls, apply the overlocks and write the notes.
- Waiving needs the Credits and Waives permission; Greens' waiver policy decides when you should.
