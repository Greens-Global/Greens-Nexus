## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Walk a vehicle lot with the site-walk checklist and know what each item looks like.
- Match every occupied space to its Cubby record.
- Record an exception with dated photos and a note, in Cubby and in Nexus.
- Handle a damage incident in the right order, without admitting fault.
- Plan and run a weather walk after wind, heat, rain and hail.

## Why the Walk Matters

A vehicle lot changes every day. Rigs come and go, covers loosen, tags expire, tires go flat, something leaks, someone parks in the wrong space. Most problems are small and cheap when you find them early and big and expensive when you find them late.

The walk is also how you know your lot. After a few weeks you will spot what is new or wrong at a glance: a trailer you have never seen, a cover gone, a stain that was not there yesterday.

> [!NOTE]
> The Hands-On Check at the end of the Academy includes a guided vehicle lot walk with your trainer. This lesson is the preparation.

## Before You Start

- Bring your phone, charged, with Nexus and Cubby open.
- Know your route. A lot walked in the same order every time is a lot nobody forgets a row of. Cubby can sort spaces in the site's walkthrough order.
- Drive the lot only in a cart or company vehicle at walking speed, or walk it. Never drive or move a tenant's vehicle.
- Watch for people backing up. A driver backing a 40 ft rig cannot see you in the mirrors.

## The Site-Walk Checklist

Walk every row. At every space, check:

- [ ] The vehicle matches the Cubby record for the space: type, plate, and the vehicle on the lease.
- [ ] No unauthorized vehicle: a rig in the wrong space, an extra vehicle, or a car parked in an RV space while the RV is out.
- [ ] Nothing sticks past the line, into the aisle or into a fire lane.
- [ ] Registration tags are current (check the month and year on the sticker).
- [ ] No flat or low tires, sinking jacks or a vehicle leaning to one side.
- [ ] No leaks or new stains under the vehicle.
- [ ] Covers are tied down and whole; no tarps holding water.
- [ ] Windows, hatches and doors closed; slides and awnings as Greens Vehicle Storage Rules require.
- [ ] No extension cords to outlets that are not part of the rental; no generators running.
- [ ] No signs of anyone living there: lights at night, pets, trash, people sleeping.
- [ ] Nothing stored beside or under the vehicle: tires, fuel cans, furniture, canopies.
- [ ] Building and lot damage: hit posts, bent canopy beams, damaged doors, faded lines, missing space numbers.
- [ ] Clear height signs present and readable at covered rows and enclosed doors.

And at the gate:

- [ ] The gate opens, closes and holds long enough for a long rig.
- [ ] The keypad and lights work, and cameras where the site has them.
- [ ] Nothing blocks the stacking area outside the gate.

## Recording What You Find

Every exception gets **a dated photo and a note**. A problem with no photo did not happen, as far as a later dispute is concerned.

| What you found | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| Something about a tenant's vehicle or account (a leak, a flat, expired tags, wrong space) | A note on the lease in Cubby, with the photo, and a call or message to the tenant |
| Something broken at the site (a gate, a light, a post, a canopy panel) | Nexus: scan the site's QR tag and use **Report a Problem**, or raise a ticket |
| A daily site inspection or a vehicle check form your manager uses | Nexus: **Fill a Form**, with photos. It works with no signal and sends itself later. |
| An incident: damage, injury, theft | Incident report the same day (below), and tell your manager |

Nexus Fundamentals showed you Report a Problem and Fill a Form. Use them; they put the photo, the place and the time together where your manager will see them.

## A Damage Incident, Step by Step

Incidents happen: a tenant backs into a post, a trailer swings into a neighbor, wind throws a canopy panel, hail dents a row of roofs, someone steals a battery.

1. **Safety first.** Is anyone hurt? Is anything leaking, smoking or unstable? Call 911 if needed.
2. **Photograph everything**: both vehicles, their plates, the space, the post or building, the ground, from far and close.
3. **Get names and insurance information** from the people involved.
4. **Do not admit fault or promise payment.** Say: "I'm documenting everything and passing it to my manager."
5. **Write the incident report the same day**, with the photos (your manager tells you which Nexus form to use).
6. **Tell your manager**, who decides whether to notify Greens' insurer.

The tenant's own insurance handles their vehicle. If Greens ever offered services like parking, fueling or washing tenants' vehicles, it would take on "care, custody and control" of them and need matching insurance (Inside Self Storage, 2009). That is one more reason staff never move tenant vehicles.

> [!WARNING]
> Never tell a tenant "we'll pay for that" or "that's our fault," even to be kind. Your manager and the insurers decide. Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

## Weather Walks

Walk the lot after any weather event, before tenants call you about it.

| Weather | Where | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| **Wind** | All sites; strong wind events in Southern California and across the open Fairfield lot | Loose or torn covers, awnings out, canopy roofing panels lifted, trash and debris in aisles |
| **Heat** | All sites, and Texas summers | Soft pavement with jacks and tongue jacks sinking (pads help); tires; batteries; seals |
| **Rain and flooding** | Low spaces on gravel and paved lots | Drainage, standing water, boat covers pooling water, open bilges |
| **Hail** | Texas sites especially | Dented roofs and fiberglass, canopy damage. Photograph the site and canopy damage. |
| **Freezing nights** | Can happen at some sites | Burst water lines in RVs not winterized; tell tenants to winterize |

After a weather walk:

- Photograph every problem with the date and time.
- Call or message the affected tenants. Tell them what you saw, not who is responsible.
- Report site damage in Nexus.
- Tell your manager about anything big.

## Walk Times and Busy Times

Vehicle tenants come and go at odd hours: before dawn for a lake launch, Sunday evening coming back. Ask your manager when your site's walks happen and what to check at the gate on busy days. Fairfield's gate reports entries and refused codes to Cubby (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026); at other sites, ask how gate activity is checked.

## Greens Scenarios

**Temecula.** On your walk, space 214's Cubby record shows a travel trailer, but a pickup is parked there. You photograph it, check Cubby for the tenant, and call. It is the tenant's truck, left while the trailer is out. You explain that the space is for the vehicle on the lease and note the call.

**Fairfield.** You notice five rigs in one row with tags expired over four months. You note each in Cubby with a photo, and tell your manager. Two of the accounts are already late.

**Murrieta.** A tenant clips a bollard backing out and cracks his taillight. Nobody is hurt. You photograph the rig, the bollard and the space, get his insurance details, say you are passing it to your manager, and write the incident report that day.

**Escondido.** Your walk finds an extension cord running from a light pole to a trailer. You photograph it, unplug nothing, and call your manager, who decides how to handle it.

**Valley Center.** A garage door does not close fully after a storm. You report it in Nexus with a photo and call the tenant.

**A Texas site.** Hail at 4 PM. At 5 PM you walk every row, photographing damage, then list the affected spaces for your manager.

## Key Points

- Walk the same route every time, every row, every space.
- Match each vehicle to Cubby; flag wrong-space and extra vehicles.
- Check tags, tires, leaks, covers, hatches, slides, cords, signs of living, items beside vehicles and site damage.
- Every exception gets a dated photo and a note: Cubby for the tenant, Nexus for the site.
- Incident: safety, photos, names and insurance, no admission of fault, report the same day, tell your manager.
- Walk the lot after every weather event.
