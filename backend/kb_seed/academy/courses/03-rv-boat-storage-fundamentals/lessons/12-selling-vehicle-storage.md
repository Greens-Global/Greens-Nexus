## The Moment

A retired couple at Murrieta have toured two other lots this week. They ask about price, but what they really say, twice, is that backing their 42 ft fifth wheel is the worst part of every trip. Quote the cheapest space and you lose them. Hear the backing and lead with a pull-through, and you have a tenant for years.

## Vehicle Customers Buy Differently

The full sales call is taught in Customer Service & Sales Fundamentals. What is different here:

- They **know exactly what they own** and what it is worth.
- They **shop several sites**, comparing aisles, hours and surface, not just price.
- **Fit is a dealbreaker.** A space that does not fit is no sale at any price.
- **Access matters:** pre-dawn launches, Friday departures, Sunday-night returns.
- Once a site works for them, **they tend to stay.**

## Ask Before You Quote

| Ask | It tells you |
|---|---|
| "What are you storing? How long, hitch to bumper?" (and the height for covered) | The fit (see Measuring for the Right Fit) |
| "How do you usually park it?" | Whether to lead with a pull-through |
| "How often do you take it out, and when?" | Access hours and gate habits |
| "Where is it now?" | The real reason: an HOA letter, a theft, a rate increase |
| "What matters most: price, protection, security, access?" | What to lead with |
| "Anything else to store?" | A second space, or a household unit too |

> [!TIP]
> Listen for the trigger. "My HOA gave me 10 days" means speed beats price. "My battery was stolen" means security is the sale.

## What Drives the Price

Quote today's rate from Cubby, never from memory or a page. Typical rates by site are on Greens by the Numbers. What moves a price:

- **Length.** A longer space uses more land and more aisle.
- **Cover.** A covered space costs more than open space of the same length.
- **Pull-through.** It uses two aisle frontages, so it costs more than a drive-in.
- **The site.** Fairfield is usually the lowest; every market differs.

Prepay discounts, specials and rate increases are set by Greens; ask your manager what applies today.

## Present the Space, Not the Price

| Feature | What it means for them |
|---|---|
| Gated access with a personal code | "Only people with a code get in." |
| Regular lot walks | "We walk the lot; if something's wrong with your rig, we call you." |
| Pull-through | "You drive straight in and straight out. No backing, no spotter." |
| Covered | "Your roof, seals and tires stay out of the sun and the hail." |
| A space sized to their rig | "It fits with room to walk around. You won't have to move it." |

Only promise what your site has. Amenities such as a dump station, air, propane or power are on each Site Profile; a site that lacks one says so.

> [!WARNING]
> Never promise "nothing will happen to it," "we're responsible for it" or "you don't need insurance." The tenant's own insurance covers their vehicle.

## Objections

**"Why is the bigger space so much more?"** "A longer rig needs a longer space and more room to turn in, and that land is what you're paying for. The right size means it fits the first time."

**"It's too expensive for a parking spot."** Compare it with what it prevents: HOA fines, a sun-cooked roof, a stolen battery or catalytic converter. Then offer a lower option: open instead of covered, a shorter space if the rig really fits, or a site with lower rates.

**"Is it secure?"** Describe what is real: the gate code, lighting, the walks, cameras where the site has them. Never promise nothing will happen.

**"The place down the road is cheaper."** Ask what they get there: aisle width, hours, surface, a dump station. Then show what Greens offers. Never criticize a competitor.

**"Will my 13 ft 6 in fifth wheel fit under your roof?"** Never guess. Compare it with the covered rows' measured clear height (your manager has it until the site profile is filled in). Not clearly enough? Open space now and the covered waitlist.

**"I only use it in summer. Can I pause?"** The rental is month to month, so they can leave, but the space may not be there when they return (see Waitlists and Upgrades).

**"Do you cover damage?"** "No. Your own vehicle insurance covers your RV, and we need proof of it at move-in."

## At Greens

**Murrieta.** The fifth wheel couple: you check Cubby for an open 12x50 pull-through, walk them to it, and quote it.

**Fairfield.** A caller says it is "just a gravel lot." You agree, explain that gravel drains well and keeps the rate low, and describe the gate and the walks.

**Temecula.** A wake boat owner wants covered space but balks at the price. You offer open space now at the lower rate and the covered waitlist.

**Escondido.** A competitor is $40 cheaper. You ask about their aisles and hours, then describe Escondido without a word against them.

## Common Mistakes

- **Quoting before the fit.** The price of a space that does not fit is irrelevant.
- **Quoting a rate from a page or memory.** Cubby has today's rate.
- **Promising an amenity the site does not have.**

## Key Points

- Ask what, how long, how tall, how they park, how often and why now, before you quote.
- Quote from Cubby; length, cover, pull-through and site drive the price.
- Sell what the space does: fit, access, protection, security.
- Never promise "nothing will happen" or that Greens covers damage.
