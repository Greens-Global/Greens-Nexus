## Keep This Page Open

You are reading a report, answering a variance question or taking a call from a vendor. This page is the course in one place. Each line points to the lesson that teaches it.

## Where Things Land

| What happened | In the books |
|---|---|
| Rent for this month | Revenue in the month of storage, paid or not |
| Rent paid for next month | Prepaid rent, a liability, until the 1st |
| A security deposit | A liability: owed back |
| A free month or a waiver | A reduction of revenue, with the reason |
| Third-party insurance collected | A liability until remitted |
| Lien sale proceeds | Applied to the tenant's balance owed; any surplus is the tenant's, a liability; never revenue |
| A vendor invoice | Expense (or asset) now, payable until paid |
| A repair | Expense this period |
| A replacement, a new roof, a repave | A fixed asset, depreciated: cost divided by life, monthly |
| Wages worked this period | Expense this period, even if paid next |

Lessons: The Accounting Equation and the Accounts; Debits and Credits; Revenue at a Storage Company; Fixed Assets and Depreciation.

## What the Site Owes Accounting

- **A ticket number on every vendor invoice.** The three-way match: ordered (the approved scope and quote), received (the inspection and photo), billed (the invoice).
- **On every card charge:** a receipt, a memo that says what and why, the right property, on time.
- **A reason on every waiver and credit.**
- **Deposits intact;** refunds only through Cubby and Accounting; no vendor ever paid from the site.
- **A W-9 before a vendor's first payment.**
- **Every tax notice and bill forwarded the day it arrives.**
- **Same-day answers** to match, reconciliation and variance questions.

Lessons: Payables, Cards and the Bank; Bills, Payments and Aging; Bank Reconciliation; What Accounting Needs From You: 1099s and Taxes.

## Reading a Month

1. Wait for the close. Nothing is final before it.
2. Read earned rent, NOI and the line that moved.
3. Click the figure. Read the lines; open the entry.
4. Decide: **timing** (reverses next month) or **real** (does not).
5. Write the comment: what moved, why, timing or real, what next, with ticket numbers.

Lessons: Cash and Accrual, and the Period; The Close and the Three Statements; Budgets, Controls and the Audit.

## Your Site's Numbers

| Number | How |
|---|---|
| NOI | Revenue less operating expenses |
| NOI margin | NOI divided by revenue |
| Expense ratio | Operating expenses divided by revenue |
| Economic occupancy | Earned rent divided by gross potential rent |
| Delinquency rate | Past-due balances divided by rent billed |
