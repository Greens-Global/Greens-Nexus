## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Use the accounting words correctly with Accounting, leadership, a lender or an auditor.
- Explain each one in plain words to a coworker.

## How to Use This Lesson

Read it once now and come back when a report or a question uses a word you do not know. The words of the storage business itself (occupancy, street rate, concession as a selling tool) are in the Self-Storage Fundamentals glossary; here they appear only where accounting gives them a second meaning.

## The Equation and the Accounts

| Word | What it means | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **Asset** | Something of value the business controls | Cash, receivables, prepaids, the property |
| **Liability** | What the business owes | Payables, prepaid rent, deposits held, the loan |
| **Equity** | The owners' share: what they put in plus profits kept | Assets = Liabilities + Equity, always |
| **Revenue** | What was earned in the period | Rent, fees, protection plan, merchandise |
| **Expense** | What it cost to earn it in the period | Payroll, repairs, utilities, insurance, depreciation |
| **Chart of accounts** | The numbered list of every account | The number tells you the kind before the name |
| **Dimension** | Intacct's tags on a transaction: entity, department, vendor, customer, project | How one repairs account is read per site |
| **Contra account** | An account that sits against another to net it | Accumulated depreciation against assets; the allowance against receivables |
| **Debit, credit** | The left and right side of an entry | Debit increases assets and expenses; credit increases liabilities, equity and revenue |
| **Journal entry** | A recorded transaction: accounts, debits, credits, memo | Debits always equal credits |
| **General ledger** | Every account with every entry posted to it | What every report drills down to |

## Timing

| Word | What it means | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash basis** | Counts when money moves | The bank view; lies about a month |
| **Accrual basis** | Counts when earned or incurred | Greens' statements; the truth about a month |
| **Matching** | Revenue and the expenses that earned it in the same period | Why October's late bill is October's expense |
| **Adjusting entry** | A month-end entry that moves things to the right period | Prepaids, accruals, depreciation, the allowance |
| **Prepaid expense** | Something paid ahead that is used up over time | A year of insurance expensed a twelfth a month |
| **Accrued expense** | Something incurred but not yet billed or paid | Wages earned; the utility bill not yet in |
| **Prepaid rent (unearned revenue)** | Rent a tenant paid for a month not yet arrived | A liability until the 1st |
| **Cut-off** | The line between one period's transactions and the next | Why an invoice dated in the wrong month is a timing variance |
| **Period, close** | The month or year, and the work that makes its statements final | Nothing is true until the close is done |

## Revenue and Receivables

| Word | What it means | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **Gross potential rent** | Every unit at street rate, full | The ceiling |
| **Billed rent** | What was charged on the 1st | Vacancy and in-place rates take the first gap |
| **Earned rent** | Billed less concessions, for the period | What the income statement reports |
| **Collected rent** | What arrived | Delinquency takes the last gap |
| **Concession** | A revenue reduction to win a rental | Recorded as a reduction, never an expense |
| **Proration** | A partial month's rent for a partial month | Cubby's rule; earned over its days |
| **Receivable** | Rent owed to the company | A promise, not cash |
| **Aging report** | Receivables sorted by how long unpaid | The lien timeline as money |
| **Allowance for doubtful accounts** | The expected loss on receivables, recorded now | Bad debt expense in the period of the revenue |
| **Write-off** | Removing an uncollectible balance against the allowance | No new expense at write-off |
| **Recovery** | A payment on a balance already written off | Never new rent |
| **Pass-through** | Money collected for someone else | Third-party insurance; a liability until remitted |

## Assets, Payables and Cash

| Word | What it means | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed asset (property, plant and equipment)** | A long-lived asset the business uses | The building, the lot, the canopies, the gate operator |
| **Capitalize** | Record a spend as an asset rather than an expense | Adds value or life; the capitalization policy decides |
| **Capitalization policy** | The threshold and the test for capitalizing | Accounting's call, from an accurate scope |
| **Depreciation** | Spreading an asset's cost over its life | Straight-line: cost divided by life, monthly |
| **Accumulated depreciation** | Depreciation taken so far, against the asset | Cost less this is net book value |
| **Net book value** | What is left of an asset's cost on the books | Near zero means a replacement case |
| **Construction in progress** | A project's costs before it is placed in service | Not depreciating yet |
| **Placed in service** | The day an asset starts being used | Depreciation starts |
| **Fixed asset register** | Every capitalized asset with its cost, life and depreciation | The accounting twin of Asset Management's records |
| **Accounts payable** | Invoices received, not yet paid | The running list of what is owed |
| **Three-way match** | Ordered, received and billed agree | Ticket number, scope, inspection |
| **Coding** | The account and dimensions on a transaction | Why the cost lands on the right site |
| **Suspense account** | Where an uncoded or unexplained item waits | A card charge with no receipt |
| **Bank reconciliation** | Matching the books to the bank, item by item | Timing, bank items, errors, fraud |
| **Segregation of duties** | No one person takes, records and reconciles | The first cash control |

## Statements and Measures

| Word | What it means | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **Balance sheet** | Assets, liabilities and equity at a moment | What is owned and owed |
| **Income statement (profit and loss)** | Revenue and expenses over a period | Did it make money |
| **Cash flow statement** | The change in cash: operating, investing, financing | Where the cash went |
| **Net operating income (NOI)** | Revenue less operating expenses, before depreciation and interest | The number a storage site is valued on; the number a manager moves |
| **NOI margin** | NOI divided by revenue | Compare sites |
| **Expense ratio** | Operating expenses divided by revenue | Costs outrunning rent |
| **Economic occupancy** | Earned rent divided by gross potential rent | Potential actually collected |
| **Budget, budget-to-actual, variance** | The plan, the plan beside what happened, the gap | Read through the comments |
| **Favorable, unfavorable** | A variance that helps or hurts | Over on revenue is favorable; over on expense is not |
| **Internal control** | A rule or check that keeps the books honest and cash safe | Each one asks something of the site |
| **Audit** | An independent test that the statements are fair | Trails and controls; records that already exist |
| **Retention** | How long records are kept | Long; never cleaned up |

## Key Points

- The equation and the five kinds of account; debit and credit as left and right.
- Timing words: cash and accrual, matching, adjusting entries, prepaids and accruals, cut-off and the close.
- Revenue words: the four rents, concessions, receivables, the aging, the allowance and the write-off.
- Asset and cash words: capitalize, depreciation, net book value, payables, matching, coding, the reconciliation, segregation of duties.
- Statement words: the three statements, NOI and its margin, variance and controls.
