On a final collection call, a tired coworker leans over: "Tell him we're selling Monday. That'll get him moving." The sale is weeks away. You do not say it, because it is false, and a false statement to a tenant who owes money is a legal problem, not just bad manners. This lesson is the conversation. The lien process is in Self-Storage Fundamentals, Delinquency and the Lien Process, and the days are on Greens Delinquency Timeline.

## The Law in Plain Words

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

The federal Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA) mainly covers collection agencies. But California's Rosenthal Act and Texas Finance Code chapter 392 reach a business collecting its own consumer debts, and both forbid false or misleading statements, threats and harassment. So **Greens follows FDCPA-style conduct on every collections contact.** It is the right way to treat people, and it keeps everyone safe.

## The Greens Conduct Rules

| Rule | In practice |
|---|---|
| Talk only to the tenant | Or a verified authorized person or their attorney. Confirm identity before you name the balance |
| Call between 8:00 AM and 9:00 PM | The tenant's local time. Texts too |
| Not at work, if they say no | Stop calling them there |
| Never call to wear them down | Seven calls in seven days is an absolute ceiling; the Greens ladder is a handful a month |
| No threats, no shaming | Never threaten what Greens does not intend or may not do: arrest, a sale "tomorrow," telling their employer |
| No false statements | About the amount, the fees, the dates or the law. No fake urgency |
| Never "blame the system" | Not "the system won't let me" when it is a policy. Say "Greens' policy is..." or "That's my manager's decision." |
| Third parties get nothing | Your first name, "Greens Storage" and a number |
| Log every contact | In Cubby notes: date, time, who, what was said and promised |

## The Calls on the Ladder

Cubby raises the call tasks; you make the calls.

| Call | The conversation |
|---|---|
| Failed autopay | A service call: the card did not go through. Help them update it |
| Past due | A helpful reminder before the late fee posts |
| After the preliminary lien notice | The tenant may call worried. Explain calmly and factually |
| Final collection call | The amount, the next date, the ways to pay |

A payment in full stops the ladder. **A partial payment does not:** Cubby applies money to the oldest charge first, so the lease stays past due. Say so before the tenant decides how much to pay.

> [!NOTE]
> "Hi Sam, it's Lee at Greens Storage Fairfield. The balance is [amount from Cubby], and the next step is [step and date from the timeline]. I'd really like to help you keep your unit. Can we take care of it today, or set a date you can pay?"

## The Wrong Person Answers

> [!NOTE]
> "Hi, this is Lee from Greens Storage. Is Sam available? ... Could you ask Sam to call me at [number]? Thank you."

If they ask what it is about: "It's a personal matter for Sam." **Never** the reason, the balance, "past due" or "lien," not even to a spouse unless the spouse is on the account and verified. A voicemail carries the same three things: your name, Greens, a number. Your own words about a debt never go in a text, an email, a review reply or a note on a door.

## When a Tenant Cannot Pay

Empathy does not mean waiving everything. It means dignity while you work the problem.

- **Expect emotion**, and do not take it personally. "That sounds really hard."
- **Offer real solutions:** part now, a split across two cards, a payment link.
- **Get a firm commitment with a date:** "So you'll pay [amount] on Friday the 14th. I'll note that."
- **Schedule the follow-up** in Cubby before you hang up.

A payment plan, or a waive for a tenant in the lien process, is your manager's decision: "Let me talk to my manager today and call you back by 4:00 PM."

## Stop and Escalate

Stop and call your manager the same day for active military duty, a bankruptcy notice, a tenant who has died, a written dispute or request to stop, a California Declaration in Opposition, or anyone who mentions a lawyer. The rules for each are in Self-Storage Fundamentals, Lines You Never Cross.

## At Greens

**Temecula.** A boat tenant is behind and lost his job. You confirm it is him, then give the amount and the next date. He pays half across two cards; you tell him honestly that it does not stop the ladder, and ask your manager about a plan for the rest.

## Key Points

- Greens follows FDCPA-style conduct on every collections contact.
- The tenant only, after confirming identity; 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM their time; never to wear them down.
- No threats, no false statements, no fake urgency, never "blame the system."
- Third parties and voicemail get your name, Greens and a number.
- A partial payment does not stop the ladder; say so. Get a commitment with a date and log it.

Sources: Fair Debt Collection Practices Act and Regulation F; California Civil Code 1788 and 1788.17; Texas Finance Code 392.301 to 392.306.
