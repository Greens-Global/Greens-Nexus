## The Sales Call

1. **Greeting:** "Thank you for calling Greens Storage [Site], this is [name]. How can I help?" By the third ring.
2. **Why:** answer the question, then "Are you looking to rent, or is this about an account?"
3. **Discovery:** rented before? What are you storing? When? How long? Anything heat-sensitive? Name and number early.
4. **Recommend:** never "What size do you need?" A size and a reason.
5. **Value:** two features tied to their words, plus one thing that makes the site different.
6. **Price:** rent from Cubby, any special and its end, the one-time admin fee, protection at today's price, a lock, rates can change.
7. **Close:** "Does Saturday work?" or "11:00 AM or 3:00 PM?" or a free reservation.
8. **Objections:** listen, find what is underneath, answer honestly, ask again.
9. **Capture:** name, phone, email, move date, unit and price quoted, a set callback time.

## Response Times

| What | When |
|---|---|
| Phone | By the third ring |
| Web lead | Within 5 minutes during open hours; 30 at most |
| Missed call | Within the hour while open |
| Second follow-up | 24 to 48 hours |
| Move-in | Confirm the day before |
| Move-out notice | A live call within 24 hours |
| Escalation | An answer within 24 hours |
| Review | A reply within 48 hours |

## At the Move-In

- Photo ID, an alternate contact, military status.
- Explain: due date, late fee day, what happens if rent goes unpaid, rates can change, no flammables or living in units, "whoever has your code and key can get in," move-out steps.
- Protection: the myth, a real situation, the choice, recorded in Cubby. Never "insurance."
- Orientation: code tried, lock on, autopay, how to pay, hours, unit number.
- Thank-you that day; a call a few days later; ask for a review once settled.

## Hard Conversations

| Moment | Remember |
|---|---|
| Something went wrong | Hear, Own, Solve, Thank, Fix, in that order |
| "Why did my rent go up?" | Hear, real reasons, below street only if Cubby shows it, options in your authority, never a promised reversal |
| Collections | The tenant only, 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM their time, no threats, no false dates, never "blame the system" |
| Wrong person answers | First name, Greens Storage, a number. Nothing else |
| Partial payment | Does not stop the ladder. Say so |
| Break-in or damage | Empathy, document, claim path, never promise coverage, never admit fault, follow up |
| Lockout | Photo ID against the lease first |
| "Let me into that unit" | No. Do not confirm they rent here. A court order is the path |
| A death | Condolence, documents before access, steps in writing, manager the same day |
| Review | Within 48 hours, about 70 words, no account details, take it offline |

## Lines You Never Cross

- Never enter a unit with anyone. Never say you are alone.
- Never promise a waive, a match or a concession you do not have.
- Never put a balance or lien detail on a voicemail, in a text or in a public reply.
- Never offer anything for a review.
- Never use a child to interpret lease or lien terms.
- Never criticize a competitor or invent urgency.
