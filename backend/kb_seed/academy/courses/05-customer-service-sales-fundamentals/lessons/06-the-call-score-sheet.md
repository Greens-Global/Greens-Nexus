You just finished a call at Escondido that felt great. The caller laughed, thanked you and said she would "let you know." Score it and the truth shows: you never asked for the rental, and you have no email to follow up with. The score sheet turns "be better on the phone" into 22 things you either did or did not do.

## The 22 Items

| # | Item | Part of the call | What a "yes" looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Answered within 3 rings | Greeting | Picked up by the third ring, or called back within the hour |
| 2 | Greeting with property name and own name | Greeting | "Thank you for calling Greens Storage Fairfield, this is Lee." |
| 3 | Asked why they were calling or offered help | Why they called | "How can I help?" plus a "why" after a simple question |
| 4 | Asked whether they had rented before | Discovery | Asked, not assumed |
| 5 | Asked what they are storing | Discovery | Asked before giving a size |
| 6 | Asked move-in date | Discovery | A real date or week |
| 7 | Asked length of stay | Discovery | "How long do you think you'll need it?" |
| 8 | Asked about climate-sensitive items | Discovery | For vehicles: length, height and how often it goes out |
| 9 | Recommended a specific size and explained why | Recommend | "A 10x15, because..." |
| 10 | Named 2 or more features tied to stated needs | Build value | Each feature linked to the caller's words |
| 11 | Stated what makes this property different | Build value | One clear thing |
| 12 | Quoted price, special and fees accurately | Price | Rent from Cubby, any special with its end date, the admin fee |
| 13 | Mentioned the protection plan | Price | Greens' plan or own policy, at today's price, never called insurance |
| 14 | Mentioned supplies or locks | Price | Offered, not pushed |
| 15 | Asked for the rental or reservation | Close | A direct ask |
| 16 | Offered an appointment with a set time | Close | "11:00 AM or 3:00 PM?" |
| 17 | Captured name, phone and email | Capture | All three |
| 18 | Created urgency honestly | Close | Their move date, a unit held for them; never a fake "only one left" |
| 19 | Closed with the property name and thanks | Capture | "Thank you for calling Greens Storage Fairfield." |
| 20 | Tone: warm, unhurried, not scripted | Throughout | Sounded like a person who cared |
| 21 | Took control of the call | Throughout | Guided the conversation |
| 22 | Rapport | Throughout | Used the caller's name and their words |

Items 1 to 19 are things you did or did not do. Items 20 to 22 are judgments: the scorer rates each from 1 to 5, and a 4 or 5 counts as a yes.

## How to Score a Call

1. Mark each item **Yes**, **No** or **N/A**.
2. Use N/A only when an item could not apply: the caller said "I rented with you before" in the first sentence (item 4), or hung up before a close was possible. **Not asking is a No, not an N/A.**
3. Items that applied = 22 minus the N/A items.
4. Score = Yes divided by items that applied.

| Score | What it means |
|---|---|
| Below 70% | Not yet. Pick the two items that would have changed the call most, practice, try again |
| 70% to 89% | Pass |
| 90% and above | Excellent |

> [!WARNING]
> A call that quotes a wrong price or fee, calls the protection plan "insurance," or promises something outside your authority is not a pass, whatever the percentage.

### Worked Example

A Murrieta fifth-wheel call. Item 4 is N/A (he opened with "I've had my RV at another place for years"); item 14 is N/A (a vehicle space needs no supplies). The staff member never said what makes Murrieta different (item 11: No) and got a name and phone but no email (item 17: No). The judgments came out 4, 3 and 4, so item 21 is a No. Everything else was a Yes.

Items that applied: 22 minus 2 = 20. Yes: 17. Score: 17 / 20 = **85%. Pass.** The two things to work on: say what makes the site different, and always get the email.

## Reviewing Your Own Calls

1. **Review five of your calls each week.** If your line records calls the way Greens allows, listen back. If not, score yourself right after you hang up, or have a coworker listen to a live call.
2. **Track your results per 10 new-rental calls**: how many rented or reserved. Write it down weekly.
3. **Practice 5 to 10 minutes a week** with a coworker, using one of the two Practice: Twenty Scenarios lessons (Parts 1 and 2).

Pick the two items you miss most, work only on them for a week, then pick the next two.

## At Greens

**Escondido.** You did everything until the close, then said "Let me know if you have any questions." Items 15 and 16: No. You scored 74%, a pass, but you lost the rental. Next week you practice the assumptive close.

## Key Points

- 22 items: the call's nine parts plus tone, control and rapport.
- Yes, No or N/A; not asking is a No. Score = Yes over items that applied.
- 70% passes, 90% is excellent; a wrong price, "insurance" or an unauthorized promise never passes.
- Review five calls a week and track results per 10 calls.
- The Hands-On Check scores your mock call with this sheet.
