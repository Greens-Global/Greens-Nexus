---
title: The Nine Parts of a Sales Call
summary: The nine parts of a storage sales call, from the greeting to the follow-up, with sample words you can adapt, the four discovery questions, quoting the whole price, the closes that work, and what to capture before you hang up.
---

A caller at Escondido opens with "How much is a 10x10?" Ten minutes later she has reserved a climate-controlled 10x20 for the day before her closing, and you have a set time to call her son. Nothing about that call was luck. It followed nine parts, in order. Learn the structure by heart and make the words your own: a script you read sounds like a script; a structure you know lets you listen.

Greeting, why they called, discovery, recommend, build value, price, close, objections, capture: answer what they ask, know where you are, and steer back.

## 1. Greeting

> [!NOTE]
> "Thank you for calling Greens Storage Valley Center, this is Maria. How can I help you today?"

Short, warm, clear. Pick up by the third ring.

## 2. Why They Called

Answer the question, then open the conversation: "Sure, I can help with that. Are you looking at renting, or is this about an account with us?" A tenant gets help (Existing Tenants: Payments and Move-Outs). A shopper goes to discovery.

## 3. Discovery

| Question | Why you ask it |
|---|---|
| "Have you rented storage before?" | A first-timer needs the gate, locks and paperwork explained |
| "What are you planning to store?" | Decides the size and type, and screens for what cannot be stored |
| "When do you need to move in?" | Honest urgency, and which unit to hold |
| "How long do you think you'll need it?" | Shapes the unit choice and what you say about rates |

Add: "Is any of it sensitive to heat?" For vehicles: the overall length with the tongue, the height, and how often it goes out. **Get the name and number early:** "Who am I speaking with? And in case we get cut off, what's the best number?"

## 4. Recommend

**Never ask "What size do you need?"** Recommend, and say why (Recommending a Size).

> [!NOTE]
> "With a two-bedroom, a sectional and about 30 boxes, I'd put you in a 10x15. That leaves a walkway so you can reach the boxes you'll need before spring."

## 5. Build Value

Value is a feature tied to something they said. Pick two or three.

| Feature | Tied to their words |
|---|---|
| Drive-up unit | "You're doing this yourself with a rental truck, so you can back right up to the door." |
| Climate control | "You said the dresser is solid wood. Climate control keeps it out of the summer heat." |

Then say **one clear thing that makes this site different.** Your Site Profile is a good place to start.

## 6. Price

Give the price after value, and give all of it. Surprise fees are the complaint you are preventing.

1. **Monthly rent**, read from Cubby, never from memory.
2. **Any special**, exactly as Cubby shows it, and **when it ends**.
3. **The one-time admin fee** (Greens Fees and Charges).
4. **Protection:** Greens' plan or their own policy (Greens Tenant Protection). New tenants pay today's prices; never quote a lower price you saw on an older lease.
5. **A lock**, if they need one.
6. **Rates can change:** "Rates are reviewed over time, and you'll always get written notice before a change."

> [!NOTE]
> "That 10x15 is [rent] a month. There's a one-time $25 admin fee, and you'll choose protection for your things: our plan is $14 a month for $2,500 of coverage, or you can use your own insurance if it covers storage. There's no deposit and no charge to reserve it."

The first payment depends on the move-in date. Do not guess it: run Preview Charges in Cubby and quote that number.

## 7. Close

Ask. Most storage staff never do.

- **Assumptive:** "Let's get you set up in the 10x15. Does Saturday work?"
- **Option:** "I have a 10x15 at [rent] or a 10x20 at [rent] if you'd like more room. Which feels right?"
- **Reservation:** "I can reserve it now at no charge so it's held for the 15th. What's the best email for the confirmation?"
- **Visit:** "I have 11:00 AM or 3:00 PM tomorrow. Which is better?" Never "come by anytime."

## 8. Objections

Listen, find what is underneath, answer honestly, then ask again (Objections, Honestly).

## 9. Capture and Follow Up

Before you hang up:

- [ ] Name, phone and email
- [ ] Move-in date
- [ ] The unit and price you quoted
- [ ] How they heard about Greens
- [ ] A set time for the next contact

Put it in Cubby as a lead or a reservation, close with the property name and thanks, and call exactly when you said.

## At Greens

**Escondido.** Daniel gives the 10x10 range, then asks what Karen is storing: a three-bedroom house with a piano, for about six months. "A piano won't fit in a 10x10. I'd put you in a 10x20, and climate control will protect the piano." He quotes everything, offers to reserve it for the 27th, hears "I need to talk to my son," emails the quote, and sets a call for Thursday at 6:00 PM when they are together. On Thursday at 6:00 PM, he calls.

## Key Points

- Greeting, why, discovery, recommend, value, price, close, objections, capture.
- Never ask "What size do you need?"
- Value is a feature tied to their words, plus one thing that makes the site different.
- Quote rent, special and its end, admin fee, protection at today's price, and that rates can change.
- Ask for the rental, a reservation or a set visit time, then capture everything.

Sources: storage sales training and mystery-shop standards in Inside Self-Storage (2010 to 2026).
