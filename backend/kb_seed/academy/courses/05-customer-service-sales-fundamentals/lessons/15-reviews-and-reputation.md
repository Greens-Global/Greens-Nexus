A one-star review appears for Murrieta: "They auctioned my RV." You know the account went through every step the law requires. You want to say so, in public, with dates. You will not, because every future shopper reads that reply, and a balance or a lien date in a public reply is a privacy problem. For many shoppers your reviews are the first conversation they have with Greens, before you ever get a chance to help.

## Why Reviews Matter

Two things shape what a shopper sees: **the reviews** (how many, how recent, how good) and **your replies.** A calm, kind reply to a one-star review often does more for the next shopper than another five stars. It shows how Greens treats people when something goes wrong.

## Asking for Reviews

**When:** once the customer is happy and settled. At the follow-up call after a move-in, after you solved a problem, or at a happy move-out.

**How:** ask **every** customer the **same neutral way**, with the direct review link by text or email.

> [!NOTE]
> "Thanks again for choosing Greens Storage Valley Center. If you have a minute, we'd appreciate a review about your experience. Here's the link. Good or bad, it helps us get better."

| Never | Why |
|---|---|
| Offer anything for a review: a discount, a free month, a gift card, a drawing | Federal rules ban buying reviews conditioned on being positive, and **Greens offers no incentives at all** |
| Ask only the happy customers | It skews the picture and breaks review-site policies. Ask everyone |
| Hide or bury negative reviews | Suppressing reviews is banned too |
| Review Greens yourself, or ask family or friends to | That is a fake review |
| Review a competitor | Never, good or bad |

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

## Replying to Reviews

- **Reply to every review within 48 hours.**
- **Say who you are and your role.**
- **Keep it short:** about 70 words.
- **Thank positive reviewers by name** and mention one real detail.
- **On a negative review**, stay calm, own real mistakes, and take it offline with a direct number.
- **Never disclose account details:** no balance, lien status, unit number, visit dates, not even whether the person still rents.
- **Fix what the review is about.** The reply is the start, not the end.

Ask your manager who replies for your site; many companies keep one voice per site.

### Template 1: Positive

> [!NOTE]
> "Thank you, [Name]! We're so glad the move went smoothly and that [detail] helped. We're here whenever you need us. - [Your name], Greens Storage [Site]"

### Template 2: Negative, About Service

> [!NOTE]
> "Hi [Name], I'm [Your name], the manager at Greens Storage [Site]. I'm sorry your visit didn't go the way it should have. I'd like to make it right. Please call me directly at [number], and I'll take care of it personally."

### Template 3: Negative, About a Rate Change or a Fee

> [!NOTE]
> "Hi [Name], thank you for the feedback. I understand how frustrating a rate change is. I'd like to go over your options with you, including other unit sizes. Please call me at [number]. - [Your name], Greens Storage [Site]"

### Template 4: Negative, About a Lien or Auction

> [!NOTE]
> "Hi [Name], I'm sorry for how difficult this situation has been. I'm not able to discuss account details here, but I'd welcome a call at [number] to talk it through. - [Your name], Greens Storage [Site]"

## The "They Sold My Stuff" Review

What you feel: defensive. What you do:

1. **Breathe. Do not reply in the first hour.**
2. **Tell your manager** the same day.
3. **Reply within 48 hours** with Template 4. Calm, short, no details.
4. **Take it offline.** If they call, listen; your manager handles the conversation.
5. **Fix the process** if there is something to fix: was the lien process explained at move-in, were the calls made and logged?

> [!WARNING]
> Never write "You were sent multiple notices," "Your account was 90 days past due," or anything else about the account in a public reply, however unfair the review feels.

A review about a break-in, an injury or a police matter is the same: no details of the incident, Template 2 or 4, and your manager before you reply.

## At Greens

**Escondido.** A tenant who stored her late mother's furniture posts five stars and names you. Same day: "Thank you, Linda! We're so glad we could help with your mom's things. - Daniel, Greens Storage Escondido."

**Temecula.** A one-star review: "Gate was broken and nobody answered the phone." You reply with Template 2, find the keypad was faulty that weekend, and report it. Your manager gets it fixed. That is the Fix step.

## Key Points

- Reviews are often the first conversation; your replies matter as much as the stars.
- Ask every customer the same neutral way. No incentives, no fake reviews, no review of a competitor.
- Reply within 48 hours, about 70 words, your name and role, negatives taken offline.
- Never disclose an account, lien or incident detail in a public reply.
- After a bad review, fix the cause.

Sources: the Federal Trade Commission's rule on consumer reviews and testimonials (2024); storage reputation columns in Inside Self-Storage (2026).
