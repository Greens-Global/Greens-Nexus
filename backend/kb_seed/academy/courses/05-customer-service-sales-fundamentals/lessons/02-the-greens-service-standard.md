It is 4:45 PM at Valley Center. A tenant walks in angry: a late fee posted and he says he paid on the 10th. You could open his ledger and prove him wrong in ten seconds. You will not, not yet. This lesson gives you the five steps Greens uses when something goes wrong, the habits that come before every conversation, and the response times every site works to.

## What Great Service Means Here

1. **We listen before we fix.** A fix for the wrong problem is not a fix.
2. **We are honest** about prices, fees, rate changes, and what we can and cannot do.
3. **We follow up.** When we say we will call at 3:00 PM, we call at 3:00 PM.

## Hear, Own, Solve, Thank, Fix

When something goes wrong (a code that will not work, a surprise fee, a wet box) use these five steps, in order.

| Step | What you do | What it sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| **Hear** | Let them finish. Do not defend or explain yet. Take notes | "So you drove out after work and your code didn't open the gate. Did I get that right?" |
| **Own** | Name the feeling and apologize for the experience | "I'm sorry. That's a frustrating way to end a long day." |
| **Solve** | Fix what you can inside your authority. If you cannot, say what happens next and when | "Let me check your code in Cubby right now." |
| **Thank** | Thank them for telling you | "Thanks for coming in about it." |
| **Fix** | Afterward, fix the cause so the next person is spared | You note it in Cubby and tell your manager the exit sign is confusing |

**Order matters.** The common mistake is jumping to Solve. You reset a code when the real problem was that nobody told them the gate hours. Fix is about the process, never blame: ask "what made this happen?", not "whose fault is it?"

## Listening Like You Mean It

1. **Let them finish**, even when you know where they are going.
2. **Play it back.** "So the movers come on the 15th and the house closes in spring?"
3. **Name the feeling.** "That sounds like a stressful week."
4. **Write it down** in Cubby notes, so the next person knows.
5. **Use their words.** If they said "my mom's dresser," say "your mom's dresser," not "the furniture."

## Before They Arrive

**On the phone.** Finish chewing, stop typing and smile before you pick up; callers hear attitude. Pick up by the third ring. Slow down.

**At the counter.** Stand up and greet within seconds. Look up from the screen. Put your phone away: the person in front of you comes first.

**The site.** Customers judge security and care by what they see.

- [ ] Office clean, counter clear, lights on
- [ ] Rent due date and late-fee day posted where tenants can read them
- [ ] Gate instructions posted at the entrance and the exit
- [ ] Keypads wiped and working; drive aisles swept
- [ ] A clean vacant unit ready to show

## The Second Mile

Give every customer one thing beyond what they expected. It costs little: carry the boxes to their car, write a thank-you card, call a few days after the move-in, remember the name of a tenant's dog. Pick two habits, practice them for a week, then add two more.

## Your Authority

A customer who hears "Let me fix that" from you is happier than one who waits two days for someone else. A customer who hears "Yes, I'll waive it" and then gets the bill anyway is angrier than if you had said nothing. Your limits are on Greens Waivers, Credits and Refunds; know them before you need them. Above your limit: "That's my manager's call. I'll ask today and call you by 4:00 PM."

## The Greens Response Times

| Channel | Greens target |
|---|---|
| Phone | Answer by the third ring |
| Web lead or online reservation | First live contact within 5 minutes during open hours; 30 at the most |
| Voicemail or missed call | Within one hour while open; first thing next business morning after hours |
| Second follow-up on a lead | 24 to 48 hours after first contact |
| Before a move-in | Confirm the arrival time the day before |
| Move-out notice | A live call within 24 hours |
| Escalated complaint | The customer hears back within 24 hours |
| Online review | A reply within 48 hours |
| A callback you promised | At the time you said, or call before it and say when |

## At Greens

**Valley Center.** You let the tenant finish (Hear). "I'd be upset too" (Own). In his ledger you see no payment on the 10th: his saved card expired, so nothing posted. You show him gently and take the payment (Solve), thank him (Thank), and update his card with him (Fix).

**Murrieta.** You promised a fifth-wheel owner an answer by 2:00 PM. At 1:45 PM you still do not have it, so you call: "I'll have it by 4:00 PM." He trusts you more, not less.

## Key Points

- Greens service is listening, honesty and follow-up.
- Recover with Hear, Own, Solve, Thank, Fix, in that order.
- Sorry for the experience is never an admission of fault.
- Know your limits on Greens Waivers, Credits and Refunds.
- Phone by the third ring, web leads in 5 minutes, reviews in 48 hours, promised callbacks on time.
