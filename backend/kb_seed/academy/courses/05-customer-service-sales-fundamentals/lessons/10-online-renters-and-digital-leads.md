At 10:02 AM a web reservation for a 5x10 lands at Valley Center. At 10:05 AM you call. She is storing a one-bedroom apartment, which will not fit in a 5x10. You move her to a 10x10 and confirm Saturday. Call at 2:00 PM instead and she has rented somewhere else, or she arrives Saturday with a truck full of things that will not fit. A web lead is a person at a screen with three storage sites open. Whoever reaches them first, helpfully, usually wins.

## Minutes Matter

**Greens' standard: first live contact within 5 minutes during open hours, never more than 30.** During open hours this comes before anything except a customer at the counter. A lead that arrives after hours is the first call of the next business morning, before the task list.

## Where Web Leads Come From

The online sources (Leads and Reservations in Cubby) and your first move:

| Cubby source | What happened | Your first move |
|---|---|---|
| Website Checkout | They rented or reserved online | Call to confirm the size fits and the move-in date |
| Abandoned Checkout | They started and stopped | Call the same day: they were minutes from renting |
| Failed Checkout | The payment did not go through | Call and offer to finish it with them |
| Contact Form | They asked a question | Answer it, then do discovery |

## The First Contact

1. **Call within 5 minutes.**
2. **Confirm the size fits.** Online renters pick their own size and often pick too small. Ask what they are storing.
3. **Confirm the move-in date and time.**
4. **No answer?** Leave a short voicemail, then send a text and an email right away.
5. **Call again** later that day.
6. **Second follow-up 24 to 48 hours later**, by the channel they prefer.

> [!NOTE]
> "Hi, this is Ana with Greens Storage Temecula. I saw you just reserved a 10x20 parking space online, and I want to make sure it fits. What are you parking? ... A 22-foot boat on a trailer, so about 26 feet overall? Then let's put you in a 10x30, so you're not squeezed. Same day, the 1st?"

For an abandoned checkout: "I saw you started a rental on our website and wanted to see if I could help you finish it, or answer anything that came up."

## Texts and Emails

- **Say who you are and the site** in the first line. Nobody replies to an unknown number.
- **Keep texts short:** one or two sentences, one question.
- **Personalize** with one detail from the conversation.
- **Never put a balance, a late fee or a lien detail in writing** (Collections With Respect).

> [!NOTE]
> "Hi Joe, it's Ana at Greens Storage Temecula. Thanks for your reservation! I'd like to make sure your space fits your boat. Is now a good time for a quick call?"

An email confirming a reservation names the unit, the site and the date in the subject ("Your 10x10 Reservation at Greens Storage Valley Center, 11/15/2026"), then lists the unit, the rent, the one-time admin fee and the move-in time, mentions one thing they told you ("we'll have a cart ready for the piano"), and ends with your name and direct number.

## Chat

If your site answers website chat, answer as fast as you would at the counter, ask one discovery question, then offer a call: "Would it be easier if I called you?"

## Online Renters Still Need You

Some customers rent without ever speaking to you, and they skip the orientation every counter customer gets. You still owe them:

1. **The unit checked** before they arrive: clean, swept, ready.
2. **Their gate code checked** in Cubby.
3. **A welcome message** with the hours, the key rules and how to reach the office.
4. **A welcome call within a day of move-in.** That call is their orientation: the due date, the late fee day, autopay, protection, and that nobody may live in a unit.

## Texting and Consent

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

- **Marketing texts need the customer's prior written consent** under the federal Telephone Consumer Protection Act. A text about a special is marketing.
- **A customer who replies STOP gets no more texts**, right away. Note it in Cubby.
- **Text only between 8:00 AM and 9:00 PM** in the customer's local time.
- A text that answers the customer's own question or confirms their reservation is service, not marketing, but it follows the hours rule too. Ask your manager which messages need which consent.

## At Greens

**Temecula.** Four minutes after a reservation, you call: no answer. Voicemail with your name, the site and a callback time; a text and an email; a second call at 2:00 PM reaches him. You confirm his arrival time.

**Escondido.** An abandoned checkout shows a climate-controlled 10x10. You call that afternoon. Her card was declined and she gave up. You finish the rental with her on the phone.

## Key Points

- First live contact within 5 minutes during open hours, 30 at most; after-hours leads first thing next morning.
- Confirm the size fits and the move-in date; voicemail, text and email if you miss them; follow up in 24 to 48 hours.
- Abandoned and failed checkouts are warm leads. Call the same day.
- Online renters still get a checked unit and code, a welcome message and a welcome call.
- Marketing texts need written consent; honor STOP at once; text only 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

Sources: storage web-lead response guidance in Inside Self-Storage (2021, 2026); the Telephone Consumer Protection Act.
