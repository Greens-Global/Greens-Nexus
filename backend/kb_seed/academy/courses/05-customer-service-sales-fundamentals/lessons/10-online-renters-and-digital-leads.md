## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Explain why a web lead must be contacted within 5 minutes, and never later than 30.
- Work a web reservation, an abandoned checkout and a contact form, from first call to move-in.
- Write a follow-up text and email that a customer will actually read.
- Answer a chat and move it to a reservation or a call.
- Give an online renter the orientation they skipped.
- Know when a text needs the customer's consent.

## Minutes Matter

A web lead is a person sitting at a screen with three storage sites open in three tabs. Whoever reaches them first, helpfully, usually wins (lesson 1).

| Time to first live contact | What the trade sources say |
|---|---|
| 5 minutes | "Impactful" (Garrett Byrd, Inside Self-Storage, 03/2021). DeFazio (06/16/2026): you have about five minutes; "by minute six" they may have moved on |
| 15 minutes | "Professional" (Byrd, 2021) |
| 30 minutes | "Mandatory," the most you should ever take during open hours (Byrd, 2021) |

**Greens' standard: first live contact within 5 minutes during open hours, 30 at most.** A lead that arrives after hours gets a call first thing the next business morning, before anything else on the task list.

You will see claims online that answering within one minute makes you "400% more likely" to win, or that an hour's wait makes you seven times less likely to reach the person. Those numbers come from general sales research we could not check for this course, so we do not use them. The storage sources above are enough: five minutes.

## The First Contact

Byrd's sequence for an online reservation (2021), adapted:

1. **Call within 5 minutes.** During open hours, this comes before anything except a customer at the counter.
2. **Confirm the size fits.** Run the discovery questions (lesson 4). Online renters pick their own size, and often pick too small.
3. **Confirm the move-in date and time.**
4. **If no answer:** leave a short voicemail, then send a text and an email right away.
5. **Call again** later that day.
6. **Second follow-up 24 to 48 hours later** by the channel they prefer, if you still have not reached them.
7. **The day before move-in:** confirm the arrival time (lesson 9).

> [!NOTE]
> "Hi, this is Ana with Greens Storage Temecula. I saw you just reserved a 10x20 parking space online, and I wanted to make sure it fits. What are you parking? ... A 22-foot boat on a trailer, so about 26 feet overall? Then let's put you in a 10x30 instead, so you're not squeezed. Same day, the 1st?"

For an abandoned checkout:

> [!NOTE]
> "Hi, this is Ana with Greens Storage Temecula. I saw you started a rental on our website and wanted to see if I could help you finish it, or answer anything that came up."

## Sample Texts and Emails

### The Rules

- **Identify yourself and the site in the first message.** Nobody replies to a text from an unknown number.
- **Keep texts short.** One or two sentences and one question.
- **Text only with consent, and only at reasonable hours.** See the consent rules below.
- **Never put a balance, a late fee or lien details in a text or email** unless Greens' policy says so (lesson 13).
- **Personalize.** Use one detail from the conversation (DeFazio, 2026).

### A Text After a Missed First Call

> [!NOTE]
> "Hi Joe, it's Ana at Greens Storage Temecula. Thanks for your reservation! I'd like to confirm your space fits your boat. Is now a good time for a quick call?"

### A Text the Day Before

> [!NOTE]
> "Hi Joe, Ana at Greens Storage Temecula. Your space is ready for tomorrow. Still planning on about 9:00 AM? Bring your ID, the boat's registration and your insurance card."

### An Email Confirming a Reservation

A clear subject line names the unit, the site and the date.

> [!NOTE]
> Subject: Your 10x10 Reservation at Greens Storage Valley Center, 11/15/2026
>
> Hi Priya,
>
> Thanks for reserving with us. Here are your details:
>
> Unit: 10x10 drive-up, unit [number]. Monthly rent: [rent]. One-time admin fee: $25. Move-in: Saturday, 11/15/2026, around 10:00 AM.
>
> You mentioned the movers are bringing your grandmother's piano. I've noted that, and we'll have a cart ready at the door.
>
> Please bring a photo ID. If you'd like a lock or boxes, we have them here. Questions? Call me directly at [number].
>
> Maria, Greens Storage Valley Center

### A Follow-Up for a Lead Who Has Not Decided

> [!NOTE]
> "Hi Karen, Daniel at Greens Storage Escondido. I'm still holding the climate-controlled 10x20 for your closing on the 28th, no charge. Want me to keep it for you?"

## Chat

If your site answers website chat:

- **Answer fast**, as fast as you would someone at the counter (DeFazio, 2026).
- **Answer the question, then ask one discovery question.** "Yes, we have 10x10s. What are you planning to store?"
- **Move to a reservation or a phone call.** "Would it be easier if I called you? What's a good number?"
- **Do not paste walls of policy text.** If they need the details, offer to email them.

## Online Renters Still Need You

Online rental and contactless move-in have been standard in storage since 2020. We could not find a reliable recent number for how many U.S. rentals happen online, so we do not quote one. What we know: some Greens customers rent without ever speaking to you, and they skip the orientation every counter customer gets.

What you still owe the online renter:

1. **Check the unit before they arrive.** Clean, swept, and either unlocked or with the right lock arrangement.
2. **Check their gate code works** in Cubby.
3. **Send a welcome message** with the hours, the key rules, and how to reach the office.
4. **Make a welcome call within a day of move-in.** That call is their orientation: the due date on the 1st, the late fee on the 11th (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026), autopay, protection, and that nobody may live in a unit.

> [!NOTE]
> "Hi, this is Lee at Greens Storage Fairfield. I saw you moved in online yesterday. Welcome! Did your gate code work okay? ... Great. Just a couple of quick things, since we didn't get to meet: rent is due on the 1st, and..."

## Texting and Consent

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

- **Marketing texts need prior express written consent** under the federal Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA). A text about a special or a sale is marketing.
- **A customer who replies STOP gets no more texts**, right away. Note it in Cubby.
- **Text only during reasonable hours.** Greens' collections calling window is 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM in the customer's local time (lesson 13); never text outside it.
- Texts that answer a customer's own question, or confirm a reservation they made, are service, not marketing, but they still follow the hours rule. Greens decides which messages need which consent; ask your manager.

## Greens Scenarios

**Valley Center, 10:02 AM.** A web reservation for a 5x10 comes in. At 10:05 AM you call. She is storing a one-bedroom apartment. You explain gently that it will not fit, change the reservation to a 10x10, and confirm Saturday. She was about to rent too small.

**Temecula, 4 minutes after a reservation.** Move-in is tomorrow. You call: no answer. You leave a short voicemail with your name, the site and a callback time, send a text and an email with the space, size and price, call again at 2:00 PM, and reach him. You confirm his arrival time.

**Escondido.** An abandoned checkout shows a climate-controlled 10x10. You call that afternoon. Her card was declined and she gave up. You finish the rental with her over the phone.

**Fairfield, 7:40 PM.** A tenant who rented online calls the on-call phone: she cannot get through the gate, and the office is closed. You verify who she is, check her code in Cubby and walk her through the keypad. You make sure she can get back out. The next day you call her with the orientation she missed.

**Murrieta.** A chat asks whether you have space for "a big RV." You answer that you do, ask the overall length, and offer to call. On the phone, you find out it is a 38-foot motorhome and recommend a pull-through space.

**A Texas site.** A lead from SpareFoot arrives at 8:50 PM. You call at the start of the next business morning, before you open the task list.

## Key Points

- Web leads: first live contact within 5 minutes during open hours, 30 at most (Byrd, 2021; DeFazio, 2026). After-hours leads are the first call of the next business morning.
- Confirm the size fits and the move-in date. Then voicemail, text and email if you cannot reach them; a second follow-up in 24 to 48 hours; a confirmation the day before.
- Abandoned and failed checkouts are warm leads. Call them the same day.
- Texts and emails: say who you are and the site, keep it short, personalize, and never put a balance or lien detail in writing unless policy says so.
- Online renters still get a check of the unit and code, a welcome message and a welcome call that covers the orientation.
- Marketing texts need prior express written consent (TCPA). Honor STOP right away. Text only at reasonable hours.
