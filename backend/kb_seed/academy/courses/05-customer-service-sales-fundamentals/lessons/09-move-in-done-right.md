A year from now, a Valley Center tenant gets a rate-change notice. One version of her calls you furious: "Nobody told me." The other version shrugs, because on move-in day you pointed to that line and said it in one plain sentence. Most storage complaints start at a move-in where something was not explained. Cubby Fundamentals taught you the clicks; this lesson is the conversation that goes with them.

## The Day Before: Confirm

> [!NOTE]
> "Hi Karen, it's Daniel at Greens Storage Escondido. Just confirming your 10x20 for tomorrow. Still planning on about 10:00 AM? ... Great. Bring a photo ID and the card you'd like to use. If you'd like a lock or boxes, I'll have them ready."

## Completing the Lease

Cubby's move-in drives the order: unit, Preview Charges, coverage, payment, autopay, documents, gate code and lock. Alongside the clicks:

1. **Verify identity.** Photo ID; the name matches the lease.
2. **Collect contact details**, including an **alternate contact**: "Someone we can reach if we ever can't reach you. We won't share your account with them."
3. **Ask about military status** and record it (Self-Storage Fundamentals, Lines You Never Cross explains why).
4. **Walk through the terms below**, and have the tenant initial where the agreement asks.

## The Terms That Cause Most Complaints

Do not read the agreement aloud. Point to each section and say what it means.

| Term | What to say |
|---|---|
| Due date | "Rent is due on the 1st of every month." |
| Late fee | "If rent isn't paid by the end of the 10th, a late fee posts on the 11th." Show the amount on Greens Fees and Charges |
| If rent goes unpaid | "Access is suspended and the lien process starts, which can end in a sale. Here's the timeline in your agreement." |
| Rates can change | "Rates are reviewed over time, and you'll get written notice before a change." If there is a special, say exactly when it ends |
| Prohibited items and uses | "No flammables, food or anything living, and nobody can stay in a unit, even for a night." |
| Who can get in | "Whoever has your gate code and key can come and go. If you give them to someone, they have access until you change your code or your lock." |

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

Explain the lien process the same way to everyone. Never soften it into "we'd never actually sell your stuff," and never make it sound like a threat. It is a fact in the agreement, said kindly.

**The gate code and key line is worth saying every time.** When a separating couple, a relative or an ex later asks for access, that rule makes the answer simple: the tenant controls access with their code and their key (Difficult Situations).

## Offering Protection Honestly

1. **Clear up the myth.** "Our property insurance doesn't cover what's inside your unit. Most people don't know that."
2. **Tell a real situation.** "The usual problem at storage sites is water: a roof leak or a neighbor's unit."
3. **Give the choice:** Greens' plan, or their own policy if it covers storage. A new tenant pays today's prices on Greens Tenant Protection. The lower prices you may see on older leases are legacy prices for existing tenants only; never quote them to a new customer.
4. **Record the choice in Cubby:** the plan, or their own policy with the policy number and expiration date.

> [!NOTE]
> "Do you have homeowner's or renter's insurance? Many policies cover things in storage, but check the off-premises limit. If yours does, I'll just need the policy number and the expiration date. If not, our plan is $14 a month for $2,500 of coverage, or $25 a month for $5,000."

**Never** call Greens' plan "insurance," never say it is required unless the agreement says so, and never promise a loss will be covered: the claims administrator decides.

## The Orientation

Before they leave the counter:

- [ ] **Gate code** set in Cubby, written down privately, tried at the keypad if they are moving in now
- [ ] **Lock** on the unit; Greens keeps no key
- [ ] **Autopay** offered, and turned on after the payment succeeds
- [ ] **How to pay** otherwise: the tenant portal, a payment link or the office
- [ ] **Hours**, gate and office, and how to reach the office
- [ ] **Where to load**, the carts, the dumpster rule
- [ ] **Their unit number**, said out loud and written down

## At Greens

**Escondido.** On the confirmation call, Karen says her closing moved to Monday. You move the reservation and thank her. On Monday, the unit is swept and ready.

## Key Points

- Confirm every move-in the day before.
- Photo ID, an alternate contact and military status at every move-in.
- Explain the due date, late fee, what happens if rent goes unpaid, rate changes, prohibited uses and the gate code and key rule before they sign.
- Offer protection at today's price with a real situation; record the choice; never call it insurance or promise coverage.
- Thank them that day, call a few days later, and ask for a review once they are settled.
