## The Certified Envelope

A week after a Notice of Lien Sale went out at Temecula, a certified envelope arrives holding a signed form titled Declaration in Opposition. Does the sale go ahead? It does not, and knowing why is the point of this lesson: the law behind each rung of the ladder, so you recognize the documents and dates that matter.

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

## California, Step by Step

California's law is the Self-Service Storage Facility Act, Bus. & Prof. Code 21700 to 21716.

1. **The lien exists (21702)** on everything stored, for rent, late fees, other charges and the costs of sale.
2. **Preliminary Lien Notice (21703).** Once any charge has been unpaid **14 consecutive days**, the owner may send it to the tenant's last known address **and** the alternate address, by certified mail, first-class mail with a certificate of mailing, or email if the agreement and the tenant's signed consent allow it (and delivery can be shown). It itemizes what is owed and gives a termination date **at least 14 days after mailing**.
3. **The lien attaches (21705(a))** if the balance is still unpaid on that date. The owner may then deny access.
4. **Notice of Lien Sale (21705(b))** to both addresses, with a sale date **at least 14 days after mailing**, the amount, how to pay in full before the sale, and a **blank Declaration in Opposition** enclosed.
5. **Declaration in Opposition (21706).** A form the tenant signs under penalty of perjury and returns by certified mail before the sale. If a valid one arrives, **the owner may not sell**; it must go to court instead.
6. **Advertising (21707):** once a week for two weeks in a general newspaper, or once in a newspaper plus at least 7 days on a public online auction website.
7. **The sale** must be **commercially reasonable**: an in-person auction or a public auction website. A good-faith buyer takes the goods free of the tenant's rights.
8. **The money.** Sale proceeds pay the tenant's balance and the costs of sale. Anything left over is **held one year** for the tenant or a claimant, then paid to the county.

From due date to sale takes at least about six weeks, and in practice longer.

## A Lien Sale Is Not a Profit Center

The proceeds are applied to what the tenant already owed: rent and fees Greens has already billed. A surplus is never Greens' money; it is held for the tenant as the law requires. Greens would always rather the tenant pay and keep their things.

## Texas, Property Code Chapter 59

- **The lien attaches** when the goods are placed in the facility (59.006).
- **No sale without a court** unless the lien is underlined or in conspicuous bold in a written agreement (59.041).
- **Notice of claim (59.042, 59.043):** in person, by verified mail, or by email if the agreement's email language is underlined or bold. It itemizes the claim, says the contents have been seized, gives the tenant until the **14th day after delivery** to pay, and carries a **bold or underlined request that a tenant in military service say so**.
- **Advertising (59.044):** once in each of two consecutive weeks in a county newspaper, or posting at the facility and five other places.
- **Sale (59.045):** public, at or near the facility or on a public website.
- **Excess:** paid on request within **two years**; after that the lessor keeps it.
- **Vehicles and boats** need notice to the owner and every lienholder (59.0445); towing only if the agreement authorizes it (59.052).

## Side by Side

| | California | Texas |
|---|---|---|
| Lien attaches | On the termination date | When goods are placed |
| First notice | Preliminary Lien Notice after 14 days unpaid | Notice of claim |
| Delivery | Certified, first-class with certificate, or consented email; tenant AND alternate | In person, verified mail, or email |
| Time to pay | At least 14 days after mailing | Until the 14th day after delivery |
| Tenant's shield | Declaration in Opposition stops the sale | Pay before the sale |
| Advertising | Two weeks of newspaper, or newspaper plus 7 days online | Two consecutive weeks of newspaper, or posting |
| Excess proceeds | Held one year, then the county | Two years, then the lessor |
| Military | Federal SCRA | Federal SCRA plus Texas Guard members |

## At Greens

**Temecula.** The Declaration goes to your manager that morning. The sale does not happen unless Greens goes to court.

**Escondido.** A tenant whose preliminary notice went out asks whether an email is enough notice. You tell him notices go as the agreement and his signed consent allow, to him and his alternate contact, and that your manager can answer questions about his account.

## Common Mistakes

- **Treating a Declaration in Opposition as "just paperwork."**
- **Sending a California notice to only one address** when an alternate is on file.
- **Calling a lien sale income** for Greens.

## Key Points

- California: Preliminary Lien Notice after 14 days unpaid, to the tenant and the alternate; at least 14 days to the termination date; Notice of Lien Sale with a blank Declaration; at least 14 more days; advertising; a commercially reasonable sale.
- A Declaration in Opposition stops a California sale.
- Proceeds pay the balance owed; a surplus is held for the tenant (one year in California, two in Texas).
- Texas: lien attaches when goods are placed, must be underlined or bold, notice of claim with 14 days to pay.
