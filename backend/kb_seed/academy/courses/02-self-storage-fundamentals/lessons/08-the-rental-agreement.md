## The Signing Desk

A new Escondido tenant is about to sign and asks, "What am I actually agreeing to?" A month from now she may ask "Can you do that?" about a fee, a rate change or an overlock. Every one of those answers starts with the agreement. This lesson teaches what it says, what the law requires it to say, and what you must collect at the desk so the lien process works months later.

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

## What the Agreement Is

1. **Month-to-month.** It renews every month until one side ends it with the notice it requires. That is why rates can change with notice.
2. **A rental of space, not a bailment.** Greens never takes custody of the goods; the tenant keeps the only key.
3. **Not a home lease.** Residential landlord-tenant law does not apply. Storage has its own law: California's Self-Service Storage Facility Act (Bus. & Prof. Code 21700 to 21716) and, in Texas, Property Code chapter 59.

## Key Clauses

| Clause | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|
| **Rent and due date** | At Greens, rent is due on the 1st |
| **Fees** | Only fees in the agreement may be charged |
| **Lien statement** | Required by law; without it the lien process fails |
| **Contact and alternate contact** | Lien notices go to the last known address, and in California to the alternate too |
| **Value limit** | A cap on what the tenant may store unless Greens agrees in writing |
| **Release of liability and protection** | See the lesson Tenant Protection and Insurance |
| **Use rules** | What may and may not be stored or done |
| **Owner's right to enter** | Emergencies, repairs, legal orders, prohibited items, lien enforcement, with the notice it states |
| **Authorized users** | Only the tenant and the people listed get access or information |
| **Military status** | Federal (and Texas) protections in the lien process |

## Lien Language: California and Texas

| | California (21712) | Texas (59.041, 59.043) |
|---|---|---|
| Lien statement | Property may be sold if rent or charges are unpaid **14 consecutive days** | The lien must be **underlined or in conspicuous bold print**, or no sale without a court |
| Alternate contact | The agreement must **request** one; if given, notices go to both | Not required |
| Email notices | Only if the agreement allows it and the tenant signs consent; delivery must be proven | Only if the agreement has underlined or bold email-notice language |
| Towing a vehicle | Its own statutory path | Only if the agreement expressly authorizes it |

At the desk that means: collect address, phone and email every time; in California, ask for an alternate contact and record it; never say "we'll just email you" unless the clause and consent are in place; ask about military status. Missing details at move-in break the lien process months later.

## SB 709: California's First Page

For California agreements signed **on or after 01/01/2026**, the first page, in larger contrasting type, must show:

- the initial term and the renewal term (for example, month-to-month);
- whether the move-in rate is promotional or discounted, and for how long;
- whether the rate can change, and **the maximum rent the owner could charge in the first 12 months**;
- every step needed to end the agreement, including removing all property;
- the owner's contact information.

Point to and explain each one before the tenant signs. Never promise a rate will not rise; point to the disclosed maximum. Never describe a promotion as permanent.

California also limits late fees (amount, timing, one per missed payment); the math is in the lesson How a Storage Site Makes Money. Texas chapter 59 sets no late-fee cap; never quote a Texas amount.

## Prohibited Items and Uses

| Never allowed | Why |
|---|---|
| **Living in a unit**, even one night | No exits, ventilation or sanitation; people have died. Texas forbids it outright |
| **Hazardous materials:** gasoline, propane, solvents, chemicals | Fire, explosion, poisoning |
| **Explosives, ammunition, fireworks** | Fire and explosion |
| **Food, animal feed, perishables** | They bring pests to every unit nearby |
| **Live animals; anything wet or smelly** | Cruelty, mold, odor |
| **Illegal items** | Crime |

Firearms are allowed by some agreements and not others; follow Greens' agreement and ask your manager.

**Store yes, operate no.** Inventory, tools, samples and records are fine. Customers coming to buy, employees working in the unit, repairs, auto work or running equipment off power are not.

If you see a prohibited item or use, do not confront the tenant and never enter the unit. Report it to your manager. A gas or chemical smell, or signs of someone living in a unit, is reported at once, with 911 if anyone may be in danger.

## What Greens Can and Cannot Do

| Greens can | Greens cannot |
|---|---|
| Change rent with proper notice | Promise a rate will never change |
| Charge fees in the agreement, within the law | Charge a fee not in the agreement |
| Deny access or overlock through the lawful process | Lock out outside that process |
| Enter as the agreement allows | Go into a rented unit casually |
| Enforce a lien by the statutory steps | Skip, shorten or back-date any step |
| Refuse to rent, applying the same rules to all | Throw out anything from a unit outside the process, even "obvious junk" |

## At Greens

**Valley Center.** "Can I keep a camping stove and a gas can in the unit?" No: fuel and propane are prohibited because of fire risk.

**Murrieta.** "Can I work on my motorcycle in the unit on weekends?" Storing it may be fine; repairs in the unit are not.

**Temecula.** A move-in gives only a phone number. You collect a mailing address and email and ask for an alternate contact, explaining California asks for one for important notices.

## Common Mistakes

- **Rushing the signing** past the first page.
- **Skipping the alternate contact** or the military question.
- **Letting someone sleep** in a unit "just tonight."

## Key Points

- A storage agreement is a month-to-month rental of space: not a home lease, not a bailment.
- California: 14-day lien statement, alternate contact requested, signed consent for email notices. Texas: lien and email language underlined or bold.
- SB 709: agreements signed on or after 01/01/2026 show the term, any promotion, the 12-month maximum rent, how to end it and the owner's contact on page one.
- No living in units, no hazardous materials, food or explosives; store business goods but never run a business there.
