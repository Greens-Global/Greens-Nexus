## "What Size Do I Need?"

A caller at Valley Center is moving out of a two-bedroom apartment and asks the question you will hear more than any other. By the end of this lesson you can answer it from their largest items, pick the right type of unit, and know when the right answer is a parking space or a mailbox instead.

## How Sizes Work

A size is **width x depth in feet**. A 10x15 is 10 feet wide (the door side) and 15 feet deep. Multiply for square feet: a 10x15 is 150 square feet. Height matters too: most units have 8 to 10 foot ceilings, and a customer who stacks high gets far more in. Check your own site's real heights.

Learn this table by heart. Capacities assume reasonable packing.

| Size | Square feet | What typically fits |
|---|---|---|
| **5x5** | 25 | Boxes, seasonal clothes, files, a small dresser, a twin mattress |
| **5x10** | 50 | A studio: a full mattress set, a dresser, a TV, boxes |
| **10x10** | 100 | A one-bedroom apartment, 20 to 30 boxes plus furniture. The most rented size |
| **10x15** | 150 | A two-bedroom home, about four rooms of furniture |
| **10x20** | 200 | A three-bedroom home with washer, dryer and refrigerator |
| **10x30** | 300 | A four- to five-bedroom home and heavy outdoor gear |

Sites also have odd sizes (5x15, 10x25, 12-foot-wide units and more). Quote sizes and prices from Cubby, never from memory.

## How to Size a Customer

Ask in this order:

1. **"What is the largest thing you are storing?"** A sectional, a king bed or a refrigerator sets the minimum more than boxes do.
2. **"How many rooms are you moving out of?"** Use the guide.
3. **"Any appliances?"** They take floor space.
4. **"Will you need to get to things while they are stored?"** Then they need an aisle: go up one size.
5. **"How long do you expect to store?"** Longer stays make climate control worth it.

A one-bedroom with a queen bed, a couch, a dresser and 25 boxes is the classic 10x10; with an aisle, a 10x15. A three-bedroom house with appliances is a 10x20, or a 10x15 plus a 5x10 if no 10x20 is open.

## Unit Types

| Type | What it is | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Drive-up** | A roll-up door on a drive aisle; park in front of it | Heavy items, contractors, frequent access. Dust and outside heat reach it |
| **Interior** | Off a hallway, sometimes upstairs by elevator | Household goods that need more protection from dust and pests |
| **Climate-controlled** | In a building whose temperature is regulated | Wood furniture, electronics, photos, documents, mattresses, instruments |

Inland California summers are hot, and a closed metal unit in the sun gets far hotter than the air. That is a real reason to recommend climate control. Say "the temperature in this building is regulated"; never promise humidity control or a temperature your manager has not confirmed. Ground-floor and drive-up units usually cost more than upper floors, which makes an upstairs unit a good offer for a price-sensitive customer.

## Parking Spaces

A vehicle space is **uncovered** (open, on gravel or pavement), **covered** (under a canopy) or **enclosed** (a garage with a door). What decides the space is the vehicle's total length including tongue and hitch, its height and the room to turn in. A boat never goes in a 10x10; switch to parking. ## Mailboxes

Valley Center and Escondido rent **mailboxes**: a locking box in the mailbox room that gives a customer a street address for mail and packages. Mailbox customers are often small businesses, people between homes and people who want a private address. A mailbox is a lease in Cubby like a unit, with its own one-time Mailbox Administration Fee instead of the unit admin fee, optional monthly package plans, its own small late fee and its own collection call tasks (amounts on Greens Fees and Charges). The Postal Service sets rules for any business that receives mail for customers, including an application form and identification; your manager has Greens' mailbox move-in steps.

## Common Mistakes

- **Selling the biggest unit.** Over-selling leads to early move-outs; under-selling ruins move-in day.
- **Promising humidity control** or a temperature.
- **Forgetting height** and stacking.
- **Putting a vehicle in a storage unit** where the site does not allow it.

## Key Points

- Width x depth = square feet; a 10x10 is 100 square feet and the most rented size.
- 5x5 closet, 5x10 studio, 10x10 one bedroom, 10x15 two bedrooms, 10x20 three bedrooms, 10x30 four to five.
- Size from the largest item, then rooms, appliances and access.
- Drive-up for easy loading, interior for protection, climate control for things heat damages; never over-promise.
- Greens also rents vehicle spaces and, at two sites, mailboxes.
