## Your First Morning

Your first morning at Escondido, a caller asks, "Are you guys part of Public Storage?" Ten minutes later a boat owner asks whether Temecula has a covered space. By lunch you will have answered a mailbox question too. This lesson gives you the map: what the business is, who runs it, and what Greens rents.

## Where Self-Storage Came From

Modern self-storage grew up in Texas in the 1960s: small steel-and-block buildings where a customer brought their own lock, kept the only key and came and went on their own. That idea, that the tenant alone controls the space, is still the legal heart of the business (see the lesson Access, Locks and Security).

The idea reached California quickly. Public Storage was founded in 1972 in El Cajon, in San Diego County, not far from Greens' Escondido and Valley Center sites, and grew into one of the largest real estate companies in the country.

Demand grew because people own more things and move more often, because life events (deaths, divorces, downsizing, moves) create sudden needs, and because contractors and online sellers found storage cheaper than a warehouse. Reality TV made auctions famous; real lien sales are a strict legal process and a last resort (see the lesson Delinquency and the Lien Process).

## How Big It Is

About **1 in 8 U.S. households** rents storage, and there is a storage site in almost every town. That is the one industry number worth saying out loud: competition is real, so every caller and every tenant you keep matters.

## Who Owns and Runs Storage

You will meet three kinds of operator as competitors.

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| **REIT** | A publicly traded company that owns real estate: Public Storage, Extra Space, CubeSmart. Big marketing budgets, call centers, central pricing |
| **Independent** | Everyone who owns and runs their own sites, from one-site owners to private regional operators. Most facilities in the country are still independent |
| **Third-party manager** | A company that runs a site for its owner, under the manager's brand, systems and staff, for a fee |

**Greens is a private regional operator.** Decisions are close to the sites, policies are Greens' own, and your work shows up directly in how a site does.

Three questions sort out who does what at any site: who owns the building, who employs the staff, and whose rental agreement the tenant signed. The agreement the tenant signed controls fees, notices and the lien.

## Where Greens Fits

Greens runs **five California sites on Cubby**: Escondido, Fairfield, Murrieta, Temecula and Valley Center. Greens' three Texas stores (Austin, Sachse and Georgetown) are run by CubeSmart, on CubeSmart's own rules and system. This course teaches Texas law where it differs, so you can answer a Texas question correctly, but the Greens procedures here are the California ones.

Greens rents three things:

| What | What it is | Where most of them are |
|---|---|---|
| **Enclosed storage units** | A space with walls and a door | Escondido and Valley Center |
| **Vehicle spaces** | Parking for RVs, boats, trailers and cars | Temecula, Fairfield and Murrieta |
| **Mailboxes** | A street address for mail and packages | Valley Center and Escondido |

The counts per site, and how fast they change, are on **Greens by the Numbers**. Two things never change: vehicle parking is a large share of Greens' business (which is why RV & Boat Storage Fundamentals comes next), and each site has its own mix. Fairfield is nearly all parking; Valley Center is units and mailboxes. Learn your site's mix first.

## At Greens

**Escondido.** The caller asks if you are Public Storage. "No, we're Greens Storage, a regional company with five sites in California." Then you ask what they need to store.

**Fairfield.** A caller wants a 10x10. Fairfield has almost no enclosed units. You say so, and offer Escondido or Valley Center, or a parking space if what they have is a trailer.

## Common Mistakes

- **Repeating TV myths.** Auctions are not treasure hunts, and staff never "keep the good stuff."
- **Assuming every site is the same.** Each site's mix of units, parking and mailboxes changes the work.
- **Quoting a number from memory.** Look it up on Greens by the Numbers or in Cubby.
- **Telling a Texas caller what Greens does there.** Texas stores run on CubeSmart's procedures.

## Key Points

- Self-storage rests on one idea: the tenant brings the lock and keeps the only key.
- About 1 in 8 U.S. households rents storage; competition is in every town.
- REITs are public companies, independents run their own sites, third-party managers run sites for owners. Greens is a private regional operator.
- Greens runs five California sites on Cubby; its Texas stores are run by CubeSmart.
- Greens rents enclosed units, vehicle spaces and mailboxes; the counts live on Greens by the Numbers.
