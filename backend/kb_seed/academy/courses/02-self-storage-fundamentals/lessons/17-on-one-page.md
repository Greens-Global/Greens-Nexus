## Keep This at the Counter

A caller, a tenant at the window and a task on the list, all at once. This page is the course on one sheet: the rule, and the lesson that teaches it. Numbers that change (prices, fees, counts) are on Greens by the Numbers.

## At Every Move-In

- [ ] Size from the largest item, then rooms, appliances and access (The Product: Units and Sizes).
- [ ] Walk through page one of the agreement: term, any promotion, the 12-month maximum, how to end it, contact (The Rental Agreement).
- [ ] Address, phone, email, and in California an alternate contact.
- [ ] "Are you or your spouse on active-duty military service?" Record it.
- [ ] Protection: Greens' plan at today's price, or their own policy with number and expiration date (Tenant Protection and Insurance).
- [ ] A disc lock, supplies and autopay offered.
- [ ] Gate hours and office hours both told (Access, Locks and Security).

## Every Day

- [ ] Opening routine; callbacks in the first hour (Running the Site: Open to Close).
- [ ] The walk, reconciled to Cubby: vacant with a lock, rented with no lock, paid with an overlock.
- [ ] Every Cubby task the day it appears.
- [ ] Every payment with a Cubby receipt; deposit daily.
- [ ] Report broken gates, lights, fences, doors and drains the day you see them (The Facility: What a Storage Site Is Made Of).

## The Ladder

Due the 1st. Calls on the 2nd and 6th. Late fee and gate change on the 11th. Preliminary notice on the 15th. Overlock on the 20th. Final call on the 30th. Lien notice on the 35th. Vehicles on the 61st. Sale ad on the 62nd. Payment in full stops it; a partial payment does not (Delinquency and the Lien Process).

## California Late Fee

$60 or less: $10. Over $60 and under $100: $15. $100 or more: the greater of $20 or 15% of rent (How a Storage Site Makes Money).

## Stop and Call Your Manager the Same Day

- A Declaration in Opposition.
- Active military duty, said or found.
- A bankruptcy notice.
- A death report.
- A written dispute of the balance.
- Anything about a notice or date you do not understand.

(Lines You Never Cross.)

## Never

- Hold a tenant's key or go into a rented unit.
- Read a gate code or account detail to an unverified caller.
- Confirm someone rents here to a person not on the account: "I'm not able to share whether that person is a customer." (Privacy and Compliance)
- Overlock by hand, or leave an overlock on a paid unit.
- Cut a lock without authorization, a witness and photos.
- Take, toss or buy anything from a liened unit, or bid at a sale (Auction Day).
- Call the protection plan insurance, or promise a claim.
- Promise a rate will never change, or invent a discount.
- Touch a roll-up door spring.

## When Something Goes Wrong

Anyone in danger: 911 first, then your manager. Never confront a thief, never open a unit to check a smell. Photograph before cleanup. File the incident in Fill a Form the same day (Safety and Emergencies).

## Key Points

- The move-in checklist sets up everything that follows, including the lien process.
- The early calls stop most delinquencies.
- Five events stop the ladder; when in doubt, stop.
- Changing numbers live on Greens by the Numbers.
