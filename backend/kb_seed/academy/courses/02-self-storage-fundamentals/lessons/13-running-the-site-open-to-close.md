## A Lock Where There Should Not Be One

On your morning walk at Escondido you see a tenant's lock on unit 2207. Cubby says the unit is vacant. Is someone storing for free, or is Cubby wrong? A storage site runs on small habits done every day, and this lesson gives you the routines that catch problems like this while they are small. Your site manager's direction comes first where your site differs.

## Opening

- [ ] Disarm the alarm; note overnight alarm or gate events.
- [ ] Check the cameras for anything after hours.
- [ ] Walk the gate and fence: the gate opens and closes fully, the keypad works.
- [ ] Open the office: lights, computer, Cubby, phones.
- [ ] Voicemail, web leads and reservations; plan callbacks for the first hour.
- [ ] Count the cash drawer against the opening bank.
- [ ] Today's list in Cubby: move-ins, move-outs, payments, collection and lien tasks.
- [ ] Face the retail wall, clean the restroom, put signs out.
- [ ] Punch in on Nexus.

> [!TIP]
> A web lead called back in the first hour is far more likely to rent than one called in the afternoon.

## The Walk and the Lock Check

Walk every aisle and hallway, or drive every row of the lot, as often as your site sets, usually daily. Look for missing or cut locks, doors not fully closed, odors or liquids under a door, pests, dumped trash, damaged doors and lights, blocked aisles, and vehicles where they should not be. Signs someone is living in a unit (bedding, cooking smells, voices, an extension cord) are reported to your manager at once, with 911 if anyone may be in danger. Never open a unit to investigate.

The most important part is comparing what you see with Cubby:

| You see | Cubby says | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A tenant lock | Vacant | Possible unauthorized use. Photograph it, check cameras with your manager, follow the process |
| No lock | Rented | Put a facility lock on, record it, call the tenant |
| An overlock | Paid in full | A paid tenant is locked out. Remove it now and record it |
| No overlock | Overlock Needed | A step was missed. Apply it and record it |
| A vehicle | A different or vacant space | Photograph both spaces and call the tenant |

Overlock rules are in the lesson Access, Locks and Security.

## Vacant Unit Prep

1. Confirm the tenant removed their lock and everything inside.
2. Photograph the empty unit.
3. Sweep and clean; remove nails, tape and debris.
4. Check the door rolls, latches and seals; look for damage, pests or stains.
5. Reset the cylinder core where the site uses cylinder locks.
6. Put on the facility lock.
7. Mark the unit rent-ready in Cubby.

Every day a clean unit sits marked "not rentable" is a day of lost rent.

## Abandoned Items and Dumping

| Where | What to do |
|---|---|
| Inside a rented unit | It belongs to the tenant. Only the lien process or your manager's direction disposes of it |
| Inside a vacated unit | Photograph, log, and follow your manager's direction |
| In aisles or at the dumpster | Photograph, check cameras, log it, clean up, report |

When in doubt, treat it as the tenant's property and ask.

## Keys, Codes and Cash

- Master keys (office, gate override, control key) are signed out, tracked and never lent.
- Never let anyone in on your own code; never give out a master code, even to a tenant locked out at 6:00 AM.
- Codes and alarm codes change when staff leave.
- Every payment gets a Cubby receipt; never take cash "off the books." Never cash personal checks, never write down a card number, never keep cash in your car or desk.
- Use the drop safe; count deposits with two people where possible; deposit daily; reconcile the close-out report.

The best cash policy is less cash: offer autopay at every move-in.

## Closing

- [ ] A last walk or drive of the property.
- [ ] Nobody left inside after gate hours.
- [ ] Gate closed and locked.
- [ ] Cash closed out, end-of-day reports run.
- [ ] Alarm set, lights per the routine, office locked.
- [ ] Punch out on Nexus.

## At Greens

**Escondido.** Unit 2207: you do not cut the lock. You photograph it and check the cameras with your manager, who follows the process for unauthorized use.

**Fairfield.** The drawer is $20 short at close. You recount with your manager and check every receipt against Cubby. You never cover it from your own pocket or change the report.

**Valley Center.** A move-out leaves a mattress and three boxes. You photograph and log them and ask your manager how to proceed.

## Common Mistakes

- **Skipping the walk** on a busy day, or walking without checking Cubby.
- **Leaving an overlock** on a paid unit.
- **Keeping or tossing** items left behind.
- **Taking cash** without a Cubby receipt.

## Key Points

- Open with a routine: alarm, cameras, gate, office, callbacks, cash, today's list.
- Reconcile the walk to Cubby: vacant with a lock, rented with no lock, paid with an overlock.
- Turn vacant units fast and mark them rent-ready.
- Left-behind items are the tenant's until the lawful process says otherwise.
- Track keys and codes; every payment gets a receipt; deposit daily.
