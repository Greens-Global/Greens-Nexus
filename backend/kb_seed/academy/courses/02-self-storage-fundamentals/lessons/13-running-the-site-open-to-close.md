## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Open the site with a complete opening routine.
- Do an honest walk-through and lock check, and reconcile it to the management system.
- Turn a vacant unit into a rent-ready unit.
- Handle overlocks, abandoned items and dumping the right way.
- Keep the site clean, pest-free and good-looking.
- Control keys, master codes and cash.
- Close the site safely.

These routines are typical industry practice. Greens' own site procedures, and your site manager's direction, come first where they differ.

## Why Routines Matter

A storage site runs on small habits done every day. A gate left open, a unit marked vacant that has someone's lock on it, a paid tenant who finds an overlock, a cash drawer that does not balance: each one starts as a skipped step. Routines catch problems while they are small, and they protect you, because a completed checklist shows you did your job.

## The Opening Routine

Before the first customer arrives:

- [ ] Disarm the alarm and note any overnight alarm or gate events.
- [ ] Review the cameras for anything overnight: people after hours, vehicles, damage.
- [ ] Walk or drive the gate and fence line. Confirm the gate opens and closes fully and the keypad works.
- [ ] Check outside lights: off for the day, or note any that are out.
- [ ] Open the office: lights, computer, the management system, phones.
- [ ] Check voicemail, web leads and reservations, and plan your callbacks.
- [ ] Count the cash drawer against the opening bank.
- [ ] Check today's list: move-ins, move-outs, reservations due, payments due, and collections and lien tasks.
- [ ] Make the office presentable: merchandise faced, brochures out, restroom clean, signs and flags out.
- [ ] Punch in on Nexus (Nexus Fundamentals covers your Time Clock).

> [!TIP]
> Do the callbacks early. A web lead called back in the first hour is far more likely to rent than one called back in the afternoon.

## Walk-Throughs and Lock Checks

Walk every aisle and hallway (or drive every row of the vehicle lot) as often as your site's routine sets, usually daily.

### What to Check

| Look for | What it can mean | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A rented unit with **no lock** | The tenant forgot, or a break-in | Put on a facility lock, record it, call the tenant to come re-lock |
| A **cut or damaged lock** | A possible break-in | Photos, report to your manager, notify the tenant, check camera and gate logs |
| A door **not fully closed** | Weather, pests or theft can get in | Close it if safe; if rented and unlocked, secure it as above |
| **Signs of someone living in a unit**: bedding, cooking smells, voices, lights, extension cords | Unsafe and prohibited | Do not confront; report to your manager at once; call 911 if anyone may be in danger |
| **Odors, leaks, liquids under a door** | Hazardous materials, food, a leak, a dead animal | Report at once; do not open the unit |
| **Pests** | Food in units, gaps, spills | Report; schedule pest service |
| **Dumped trash** | Someone used the site as a dump | Photos, check cameras, clean up, report |
| **Damaged doors, latches, lights, hallways** | Safety and security risk | Report for repair |
| **Blocked aisles or fire exits** | Safety risk | Clear or report |
| **Vehicles where they should not be** | Unauthorized parking, a tenant in the wrong space | Note and report |

### Reconcile the Walk to the System

The most important part of the walk is comparing what you see with what the management system says:

| You see | The system says | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| A tenant lock on a unit | Vacant | Someone may be using a unit without paying. Overlock per process and report. |
| No lock on a unit | Rented | The tenant may have moved out without telling you, or a break-in. Secure and call the tenant. |
| An overlock | Paid in full | A paid tenant is locked out. Remove it promptly and record it. |
| No overlock | Overlock needed | A step was missed. Apply it per the system. |

At Greens, Cubby flags units **Overlock Needed** at 12:00 AM on the 20th, and staff usually put the overlock on the next day (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). This is what Cubby does today and is under review with counsel. Follow Cubby. Greens has charged a "Collection Fee - Unauthorized Use of Space" in a case of a space used without a lease (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026); unauthorized use is real.

## Vacant Unit Preparation

When a tenant moves out, the unit goes back on the market only after it is ready:

1. **Confirm the tenant removed their lock and everything inside.**
2. **Photograph** the empty unit.
3. **Sweep and clean;** remove debris, nails and tape.
4. **Check the door:** it rolls smoothly, latches and seals.
5. **Check for damage, pests, stains or odors.** Repair or report.
6. **Reset the cylinder core** if the site uses cylinder locks.
7. **Put on the facility lock.**
8. **Mark the unit rent-ready** in the management system so it can be sold.

Every day a clean unit sits marked "not rentable" is a day of lost rent.

> [!WARNING]
> Items left behind in a vacated unit are never just kept or thrown out. Follow the abandoned-property steps below.

## Overlocks, Done Right

You learned overlocks in lesson 4. On a site routine, the rules are:

- Put them on only where the system flags them.
- Record each one the same day.
- Check the unit number twice before you lock.
- Remove them promptly when the tenant pays, record the removal, and check that the gate code works.
- The tenant never removes an overlock; staff do.

## Cleanliness and Curb Appeal

A clean site sells units. Online reviews mention cleanliness more than almost anything else.

Every day:

- [ ] Office and restroom clean.
- [ ] Trash picked up across the property and around dumpsters.
- [ ] Gate, keypad and signage clean.

Every week, or as needed:

- [ ] Cobwebs cleared in hallways and on doors.
- [ ] Graffiti removed fast (the longer it stays, the more appears).
- [ ] Landscaping tidy.
- [ ] Burned-out lights reported.
- [ ] Door paint and signs in good shape.

## Pest Control

- Scheduled professional service, usually with bait stations around the perimeter.
- Enforce the no-food rule.
- Clean spills quickly.
- Report droppings, bites, smells or nests.
- Seal gaps under doors and in walls.

Rodents and roaches are the main threats. In Texas and inland California you may also see scorpions, spiders and snakes. Do not handle them; report them.

## Abandoned Items and Dumping

| Where | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Inside a unit that is still rented** | It belongs to the tenant. Only the lien process, or the tenant's written abandonment or release, lets Greens dispose of it. |
| **Inside a vacated unit** | Photograph, log, and follow your manager's direction. Some agreements include a signed move-out release. |
| **In aisles, at the dumpster, on the lot** | Photograph, check the cameras for who left it, log it, clean up and report |

When in doubt, treat it as the tenant's property and ask your manager.

## Key and Code Control

- **Master keys** (cylinder-lock master, office, elevator, gate override) are tracked, signed out and never lent.
- **Never let a tenant or a vendor in on your own code.** Your code is logged as you.
- **Change codes when staff leave.**
- **Log every gate-code change or override.**
- **Never give out a master code,** even to a tenant locked out at 6:00 AM (lesson 4 and Greens Site Hours and Access).

## Cash Handling

- [ ] Count the opening bank.
- [ ] Give a receipt from the management system for **every** payment. Never take cash "off the books."
- [ ] Never cash personal checks.
- [ ] Use the drop safe.
- [ ] Count and prepare deposits with two people where possible.
- [ ] Deposit daily.
- [ ] Reconcile the close-out report to the system.
- [ ] Never keep cash in your car or desk.
- [ ] Never write down a card number.

The best cash policy is less cash: offer autopay at every move-in. It is the most preferred way for renters to pay (SSA Demand Study, 2023), and it lowers late payments.

## The Closing Routine

Before you leave:

- [ ] Walk or drive the property one last time.
- [ ] Confirm no one is left inside after gate hours (some systems sound a warning).
- [ ] Confirm the gate is closed and locked.
- [ ] Close out cash and run the end-of-day reports.
- [ ] Set the alarm.
- [ ] Lights on or off per the site's routine.
- [ ] Lock the office.
- [ ] Punch out on Nexus.

## Greens Scenarios

**Escondido.** On your morning walk you see a tenant lock on unit 2207, which Cubby shows as vacant. You do not cut it. You take a photo, check the camera footage with your manager, and follow the process for unauthorized use.

**Temecula.** Driving the vehicle rows, you find a boat parked in a space Cubby shows as vacant, and the boat's own space empty. You note both spaces, take photos and call the tenant to move it.

**Valley Center.** A move-out leaves a mattress and three boxes in the unit. You photograph them, log them and ask your manager how to proceed. You do not take the boxes home or toss them.

**Fairfield.** The drawer is $20 short at close. You count again with your manager, check every receipt against the system, and write up what you found. You never cover it from your own pocket or "fix" the report.

**Murrieta.** Your site manager is leaving Greens. On their last day, the gate override code and the office alarm code are changed, and the master keys are signed back in.

**A Texas site.** On a July afternoon you notice a strong chemical smell near a drive-up row. You do not open anything. You move away, call 911 if it seems dangerous, and call your manager.

## Common Mistakes

- **Skipping the walk** on a busy day.
- **Not reconciling** what you see with the system.
- **Leaving an overlock** on a paid unit.
- **Keeping or tossing** items left behind.
- **Letting someone in** on your own code.
- **Taking cash** without a system receipt.
- **Leaving with someone still** on the property after hours.

## Key Points

- Open with a routine: alarm, cameras, gate, office, voicemail and leads, cash drawer, today's list.
- Walk every aisle and reconcile what you see with the system: vacant with a lock, rented with no lock, paid with an overlock.
- Turn vacant units fast and mark them rent-ready; every day "not rentable" is lost rent.
- Overlocks only where Cubby flags them (Overlock Needed on the 20th, under review with counsel), recorded and removed promptly when paid.
- Abandoned items are the tenant's property until the lawful process says otherwise.
- Track keys and codes; never let anyone in on your code; change codes when staff leave.
- Every payment gets a system receipt; deposit daily; push autopay.
- Close with a last walk, an empty property, a locked gate, balanced cash and the alarm set.
