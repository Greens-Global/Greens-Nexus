## The 2nd of the Month

It is the 2nd at Escondido, and Cubby's task list has a call for every tenant whose autopay was declined last night. Most of them will pay today if you call kindly. A few will not, and Cubby will walk their leases up a ladder that ends, two months later, at a lien sale. This lesson is the whole ladder: each step, what Cubby does and what you do.

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

## Why It Matters

A lien sale sells a person's belongings. The law sets strict steps, notices and minimum days, and one mistake can make a sale unlawful. Your part is to do each task the day it appears, record what happened, never skip or shortcut a step, and stop and escalate the moment something unusual happens. Most delinquencies never reach a sale: a tenant reached early, with a kind and clear call, usually pays.

## The Ladder at Greens

Rent is due on the **1st** at every Greens site. Cubby runs the ladder by itself: it posts the fees, raises the tasks and flags the overlock. The fee amounts are on Greens by the Numbers; the exact task names are on Greens Delinquency Timeline.

| Day | Cubby does | You do |
|---|---|---|
| 2nd | Raises the failed autopay call tasks (units, mailboxes) | Call; help update the card; note the result |
| 6th | Raises the past due call task | Call everyone still unpaid; note promises |
| 11th | Posts the late fee and changes the gate code at 12:00 AM | Explain the balance; take payment; check the code works after |
| 15th | Posts the preliminary lien notice fee; the notice goes out | Make sure the address and alternate contact are right |
| 20th | Flags Overlock Needed at 12:00 AM | Put the overlock on, usually the next day; mark it applied |
| 30th | Raises the final collection call | Call; record everything |
| 35th | The lien notice goes out and its fee posts | Escalate anything unusual before the sale steps |
| 61st | Raises the vehicle lien filing or tow notice task | Your manager runs the vehicle path |
| 62nd | Raises the task to publish the lien sale ad | Your manager handles the ad |

Follow Cubby's day-11 gate change and day-20 overlock exactly, but never explain that timing to a tenant as "the law." A tenant who disputes it goes to your manager.

## Payment Stops It, Partly Does Not

A **payment in full** stops the ladder at any step before the sale. A **partial payment does not**: Cubby applies money to the oldest charge first, so the lease stays past due until every charge is paid. Tell the tenant the full amount that stops it, and note the call. When a delinquent tenant pays in full, remove the overlock when Cubby raises Overlock Remove Needed and check the gate code works before they leave.

**Example.** A boat tenant pays $184 by autopay. On the 1st it fails. On the 2nd you leave a voicemail and note it. On the 6th she promises to pay Friday. Nothing comes, so on the 11th Cubby posts a $27.60 late fee (15% of $184 beats $20) and changes her gate code. On the 13th she pays $211.60 in full. The ladder stops; you check her code works before she hangs up.

## The Collection Call

1. **Confirm you are speaking with the tenant** before you mention a balance (see the lesson Privacy and Compliance).
2. **Be kind and direct:** "Hi, this is Sam at Greens Storage. This month's payment didn't go through, and I wanted to help you sort it out before any late fee."
3. **Offer the easy path:** pay now, or update the card for autopay.
4. **Record** what happened: reached or not, what was promised, by when.
5. **Never threaten or mislead,** and never leave a balance or lien detail in a voicemail, text or email unless Greens' policy says so.

## When to Stop

Stop the ladder and call your manager the same day for a Declaration in Opposition, any sign of active military duty, a bankruptcy notice, a death report, or a written dispute of the balance. Why each one stops the sale is in the lesson Lines You Never Cross.

## Vehicles Take Their Own Path

A registered vehicle, boat or trailer is never sold through the normal unit auction. It goes through the vehicle lien path (the DMV process, or a tow where the law allows), which starts at the day-61 task. Those extra steps are taught in the lesson Vehicle and Boat Liens in RV & Boat Storage Fundamentals. The law behind the unit steps is in the lesson Lien Law: California and Texas.

## At Greens

**Escondido.** Autopay fails on the 1st; on the 2nd she updates her card with you and pays. Nothing else posts. This is how most delinquencies should end.

**Valley Center.** A tenant pays half his balance on the 18th, after the preliminary notice went out. You tell him the account is still past due and the full amount, and note the call.

**Murrieta.** A boat tenant reaches day 61. This is the vehicle path, not the unit sale; your manager runs it.

## Common Mistakes

- **Skipping the 2nd and 6th calls** on a busy day.
- **Telling a tenant a partial payment "takes care of it."**
- **Leaving a balance in a voicemail.**
- **Defending Cubby's timing as the law** instead of sending a dispute to your manager.

## Key Points

- Rent due the 1st; calls on the 2nd and 6th; late fee and gate change on the 11th; preliminary notice on the 15th; overlock on the 20th; final call on the 30th; lien notice on the 35th; vehicles on the 61st; sale ad on the 62nd.
- Payment in full stops the ladder; a partial payment does not.
- The early calls prevent most liens.
- Vehicles and boats follow their own path (Vehicle and Boat Liens).
