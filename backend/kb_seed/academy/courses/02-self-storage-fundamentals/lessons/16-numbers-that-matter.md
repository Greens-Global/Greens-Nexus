## "Our Conversion Is Down"

Your manager says Temecula's conversion dropped this month and delinquency is up. You do not run the reports (Cubby Fundamentals covers those), but you should know what each number means and which of your habits moves it. This lesson connects the numbers to the work.

## The Metrics and the Habits

| Metric | How to work it out | The habit that moves it |
|---|---|---|
| **Unit, physical, economic occupancy** | See the lesson How a Storage Site Makes Money | Answer every call, size well, quote real rates, collect early |
| **Net rentals** | Move-ins minus move-outs | Fast callbacks, good service, honest sizing |
| **Rentable units** | Units marked rent-ready | Turn vacant units fast |
| **Delinquency** | Past-due rent / rent billed | The calls on the 2nd and 6th, autopay at move-in |
| **Conversion rate** | Rentals / inquiries | A great first call and a fast follow-up |
| **Average length of stay** | Average months a tenant stays | Service, right sizing, honest rates |
| **Protection penetration** | Plan leases / all leases | Offer protection at every move-in |
| **Autopay share** | Autopay leases / all leases | Offer autopay at every move-in |
| **Online reviews** | Count and star rating | A clean site, kind service, problems solved |

### Worked Examples

**Net rentals.** 18 move-ins and 13 move-outs: 18 - 13 = **+5**. With 21 move-outs it would be **-3**, and the manager would ask why people left.

**Delinquency.** A site bills $50,000 on the 1st; on the 10th, $4,000 is unpaid: $4,000 / $50,000 = **8%**. If the calls on the 2nd and 6th collect half of it before the 11th, delinquency is 4%, and fewer late fees, gate changes and liens follow.

**Conversion.** 60 inquiries in a week and 15 rentals: 15 / 60 = **25%**. Faster callbacks that bring it to 20 rentals make it 33%, with no extra marketing.

**One tenant's value.** At $164 a month, a tenant who stays 18 months pays $164 x 18 = **$2,952** in rent. That is what one missed call can cost.

Protection penetration needs one note: at Greens most vehicle tenants carry their own policy by design, so the habit that matters is simply to offer the plan at every move-in and record the own policy when there is one.

## Ten Myths to Bust

| The myth | The truth |
|---|---|
| "The facility's insurance covers my stuff." | It covers the building. The tenant needs Greens' plan or their own policy |
| "Staff have a key." | The tenant has the only key |
| "Auctions are full of treasure." | Most hold everyday household goods |
| "You can sell as soon as someone is late." | The law sets strict steps and minimum days |
| "A partial payment stops the auction." | At Greens only payment in full stops the ladder |
| "Rent is fixed as long as I rent." | Month-to-month; rates change with notice |
| "Police can always get in." | Only with a warrant or other legal basis, through the manager |
| "Family can get into a relative's unit after a death." | Only with a death certificate and proof of authority |
| "Service doesn't matter; it's just real estate." | Most renters are in a life change; service drives reviews and stays |
| "Bigger is better to sell." | Over-selling leads to quick move-outs |

## At Greens

**Temecula.** The lot has many open spaces. Each vehicle caller you convert moves the numbers, and a fast callback is the cheapest way to do it.

**Escondido.** Enclosed units are nearly full, so every move-out turned rent-ready the same week matters.

## Common Mistakes

- **Treating a metric as someone else's job.** Every number on this page has a counter habit behind it.
- **Chasing "full"** with discounts instead of revenue.

## Key Points

- Know occupancy (three kinds), net rentals, delinquency, conversion, length of stay, protection and autopay share.
- Every metric has a habit behind it: answer and follow up, size well, quote real rates, collect early, offer protection, a lock and autopay.
- Bust the ten myths kindly and accurately.
