Thursday, 7:55 AM. The gate vendor's truck is at the keypad, and the driver asks for "the code, so I don't have to bother you." It is a reasonable-sounding ask from a vendor you trust. The answer is still no, and this lesson is the short list of rules that keeps a vendor's visit safe for the tenants, the property and the vendor.

## The Morning of the Visit

Open the vendor's record in Accounting > Records > Vendors and read the **Compliance** tab the morning of the visit, not the week before. **Expiring Soon** may work. **Expired** or **No Insurance On File** does not unload, however small the job (see the lesson Vendors: Who Does What and How to Qualify Them). Then clear what the vendor needs: the row emptied (the tenant moves a vehicle, never staff), the escort named, the lane to close.

## The Rules on the Property

A vendor on the property is a guest with tools.

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Sign in at the office, every visit** | The site knows who is on the property |
| **No gate codes** | Staff open the gate. A vendor with a standing code can come at 2:00 AM |
| **Escorted to the work area**, with the boundaries set | Which aisle is closed, where the truck parks, which units or spaces are off limits |
| **Never into a rented unit** without the tenant's written consent, or an emergency the manager approved, with a witness and photos | The tenant has exclusive control of the unit |
| **Tenants' vehicles are never moved** by the vendor or staff | If the work needs a space, the tenant moves it |
| **Cones and a closed lane** for anything in an aisle | A tenant's rig and a lift do not share an aisle |
| **No hot work** (torches, grinders) near stored vehicles, or without a permit and a fire watch | A spark in an RV row is a fire in an RV row |
| **Fall protection** for anyone on a roof or in a lift | The vendor brings and uses their own |

## The Field Checklist Is a Form

The visit's checklist is filled on your phone as a **Form**: open **Fill a Form** from Workday (or the bolt in the header, under **Request**), pick the site's vendor-visit form, and tick each line as it happens. Photos go in the form, and it works with no signal, sending when the phone reconnects.

The form is the record of the visit; the **ticket** stays the record of the job. Write the ticket number in the form, and put the finished-work photo on the ticket, where it resolves the job. If your site has no vendor-visit form yet, your manager has one until it is built.

## When Something Goes Wrong

If the vendor damages the property or a tenant's vehicle, photograph it where it stands, get the crew lead's name, write it in the ticket, and tell the manager above you the same day. **Never accept an offer to "just fix it" quietly**: a quiet fix leaves no claim and no record (see the lesson Risk, Insurance and Incidents).

## At Greens

At 7:55 AM the escort checks the Compliance tab (Expiring Soon, fine to work), opens the gate herself, signs the vendor in, cones the entry lane and stays near. She ticks the Vendor on Site form as she goes and adds the ticket number. When the work is done she photographs the new sprocket for the ticket, submits the form, and the gate vendor leaves without ever having had a code.

## Common Mistakes

- **A gate code given to a vendor**, even a trusted one.
- **A rented unit opened for a vendor** without consent or an approved emergency.
- **Moving a tenant's vehicle** to make room for the work.
- **Checking the papers the week before** instead of that morning.
- **The checklist in a text** instead of the Form.

## Key Points

- Check the Compliance tab the morning of the visit; Expired does not unload.
- Signed in, no gate code, escorted, never into a rented unit, no tenant vehicle moved.
- Cones for aisle work; no hot work near stored vehicles; fall protection on roofs and lifts.
- The visit is a Form; the job is the ticket.
