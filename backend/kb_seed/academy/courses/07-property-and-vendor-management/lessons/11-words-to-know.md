## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Use the property management and vendor words correctly with vendors, inspectors, insurers and leadership.
- Explain each one in plain words to a coworker.

## How to Use This Lesson

Read it once, then come back when a vendor, an inspector or an insurer uses a word you do not know. Words the earlier courses already define (lien, overlock, exclusive control, bailment, clear height) are not repeated here.

## Property and Maintenance Words

| Word | What it means | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **Asset** | The property and everything on it that has value and a life | It is what the four jobs protect |
| **AHJ (authority having jurisdiction)** | Any agency with the right to inspect the property and require things of it | Each one is a record with a next visit |
| **Capital (capex)** | Money that replaces or adds something that lasts years | Leadership's plan; at Greens a Construction job |
| **Operating expense (opex)** | Money that keeps today's property running | The site manager's budget |
| **Reserve** | Capital set aside over a system's life to replace it | Your record of age and tickets is what funds it |
| **Service life** | How long a system lasts with its maintenance kept | Where each system is in its life goes in the budget case |
| **Preventive maintenance** | Work on a schedule before failure | The cheapest maintenance there is |
| **Corrective maintenance** | Fixing what failed, with nobody in danger | A normal ticket at a normal rate |
| **Emergency maintenance** | Now, because the site cannot operate or damage is spreading | The most expensive hour; skips quotes, never the record |
| **Preventive schedule** | A calendar, floating or meter schedule on the property that files a ticket when due | Closing the ticket writes the record and moves it on |
| **Work order** | The instruction to do a piece of work; at Greens, a ticket | There is no second queue |
| **Maintenance record** | What was done to the property, when, by whom, with the photo | Written by the closed ticket |
| **Scope of work** | What is to be done, where, to what standard, by when, with what excluded | What the vendor quotes against and you inspect against |
| **Variance** | The gap between a budget line and the actual | A line over in March is a conversation in March |
| **Cost per unit, per space** | The property's cost divided by what it rents | How leadership compares sites |
| **Crack seal, sealcoat, striping** | The yearly, the every-few-years and the with-it steps of the asphalt cycle | Skip one and the next comes sooner |
| **Overlay, repave** | A new surface over a sound base; a rebuilt base | Capital projects |
| **Grading** | Shaping a gravel lot so water runs off it | After the first rain, where water stood |
| **Swale, catch basin, culvert** | A drainage ditch, a drain inlet, a pipe under an aisle | Cleaned before the rains and after storms |
| **Purlin** | A roof beam that carries the panels on a canopy | A bent one is a vendor visit |
| **Bollard** | A crash post; replaced, never straightened | A bent one did its job |
| **Photocell** | The sensor that turns site lights on at dusk | The part that fails first in a dark row |
| **Loop detector, photo eye** | The gate's pavement sensor and its beam sensor | What keeps a gate from closing on a vehicle |
| **Operator** | The gate's motor and gearbox | Staff never open its housing |
| **Backflow device** | The valve that keeps site water from flowing back into the public supply | Tested yearly; a program record |
| **Knox box** | The fire department's key box at the gate | Current keys and codes; never shared |
| **Climate log** | The daily temperature reading in a climate building | The answer to the heat claim |

## Vendor and Money Words

| Word | What it means | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **Vendor** | A company Greens buys work or goods from; at Greens an Intacct vendor record in Accounting | No record in Accounting, no vendor |
| **Trade** | A licensed line of work: electrical, plumbing, roofing, gate, door, HVAC | Call the one that holds the license |
| **General contractor** | A company that coordinates several trades on one job | Right for a project; not a replacement for the licensed trade |
| **License** | The state's permission to practice a trade | Unlicensed work can be illegal and uninsurable |
| **Certificate of insurance (COI)** | A one-page proof of a vendor's liability coverage, its limits and expiry | Must name Greens as additional insured |
| **Additional insured** | A party named on another's policy so it can claim on it directly | Why Greens can claim on the vendor's policy |
| **Workers compensation** | Coverage for a vendor's crew if hurt on the job | Without it, the injury can become Greens' claim |
| **W-9** | The tax form a vendor gives before it can be paid | No W-9, no check |
| **Expiring Soon, Expired, No Insurance On File** | The one state of a vendor's papers on its Compliance tab | Expired does no work on site |
| **Quote** | A written price for a written scope | One, two or three by the amount |
| **Approval limit** | The amount a level may approve on its own | Never split a job to stay under one |
| **Purchase order (PO)** | A written order for goods or work at an agreed price | How larger work and goods are committed |
| **Purchase request** | A Workday request for the company to buy something it does not have | Approved by the manager it names |
| **Commitment** | A subcontract or purchase order on a Construction job | What is committed against the budget |
| **Change order** | A written change to a job's scope or price, with a reason | Explained before, never after |
| **Invoice matching** | Checking an invoice against the ticket or PO, the scope, the quote and the inspection | Accounting pays only what matches |
| **Lien waiver** | A vendor's signed statement that it has been paid and waives its lien on the property | Received on construction jobs; Nexus never writes the wording |
| **Service contract** | A fixed price for scheduled visits and a defined emergency response | For the systems with schedules and emergencies both |
| **On-call** | Work at the vendor's rate on file, as needed | For the trades needed a few times a year |
| **Response time** | How fast a vendor comes for each kind of problem | Known before the emergency |
| **Scorecard** | Response, quality, price, papers, from the tickets, twice a year | Feedback first; replacement with a reason |
| **Conflict of interest** | A personal interest in a vendor or a job | Disclosed in writing or it is a breach |

## Inspection and Insurance Words

| Word | What it means | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **Permit** | The building authority's permission to do work and its right to inspect it | The licensed vendor pulls it; the final goes in Records |
| **Finaled** | A permit whose work passed its last inspection | An open permit is a problem at sale and at a claim |
| **Deficiency** | An item an inspection found wrong | A ticket with the deadline, the day of |
| **Fire lane** | The route a fire engine needs, painted and signed | Never parked in, for any length of time |
| **Deductible** | The part of a claim the policyholder pays | Why small damage is often a ticket, not a claim |
| **Adjuster** | The insurer's person who inspects a claim | Permanent repairs wait for them |
| **Property insurance** | Greens' coverage for damage to the buildings and lot | Storm, fire, a rig into a canopy |
| **Liability insurance** | Greens' coverage for injury to people on site | Why lighting, signs and bollards are insurance too |
| **Loss control survey** | The insurer's inspection of the property with recommendations | Each recommendation has a deadline; a record |
| **Incident report** | The Nexus form that records what happened, with photos and location | The same day; never a text |
| **Hot work** | Welding, torching, grinding | A permit and a fire watch; never near stored vehicles |
| **Fall protection** | The harness and anchor a person on a roof or in a lift must use | Nobody on a roof without it |

## Key Points

- Property words: asset, AHJ, capital and operating, reserve, service life, the three kinds of maintenance, the preventive schedule, the scope.
- Vendor words: the four papers, additional insured, the three paper states, quotes and limits, purchase orders and requests, invoice matching, contracts and on-call, the scorecard, conflict of interest.
- Inspection and insurance words: permit and finaled, deficiency, deductible, adjuster, the two Greens policies, the incident report, hot work, fall protection.
