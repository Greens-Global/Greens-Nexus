7:00 AM after a winter storm at Fairfield. Panels are off Building A's roof, one is lying on a tenant's travel trailer, and rain is still coming. The roofer can be there by nine and offers to replace the panels today. The order you do things in this morning decides whether the claim is paid.

## The Risks and the Insurance

| Risk | Example | Whose insurance |
|---|---|---|
| **Damage to the property** | A storm takes roof panels; a rig hits a canopy | Greens' property policy, with a deductible |
| **Injury to a person on site** | A visitor trips in a dark hallway; a gate closes on a car | Greens' liability policy |
| **Loss of a tenant's goods** | Water in a unit; a break-in | The tenant's own protection plan or policy; the lease limits Greens' liability |
| **A vendor's work** | A roofer's torch starts a fire; a crew member is hurt | The vendor's liability and workers comp, with Greens as additional insured |

The manager's job is not to decide claims. It is to make sure each one lands on the right policy with the evidence it needs: photos, tickets, inspection history, the vendor's papers, the temperature log. Greens' policies, deductibles and the carrier are held by leadership; when a claim is possible, the manager above you tells the carrier.

## Before the Weather

Prepare on the preventive schedule (see the lesson The Maintenance Mindset: Preventive, Corrective, Emergency) for rain (drains cleared in October), wind (loose panels fastened), heat (HVAC serviced in spring) and fire season (weeds cut, evacuation plan known). After each, walk the site.

## After a Storm

The walk after a storm is a claim's first page. In order:

1. **Safety**: downed lines, water near electrical, a leaning canopy. Keep people away; call the utility or 911.
2. **Photograph the whole site** before anyone touches anything.
3. **Tenants' property**: photograph it, do not touch it, call the tenant.
4. **Tickets**: one per damaged thing, with photos, by priority.
5. **The manager above you**, the same day, with the photos. The carrier may send an **adjuster** before repairs start.
6. **Temporary protection only**: tarps, cones, a closed row.

> [!WARNING]
> Never start permanent repairs on storm damage until the manager above you says so. A roof replaced before the adjuster saw it is a roof the carrier may not pay for.

The incident report is a Nexus **Form**, filled on the phone with photos and location, reviewed by someone else, and it works with no signal. Two property additions:

- **The property's own damage is also a ticket**, with the incident photos attached, so the repair and the claim share one record.
- **Footage is preserved the same hour.** Tell your manager which camera and what time, before the recorder overwrites it.

## Damage by a Vendor or a Tenant

A **vendor** who damages the property or a vehicle is why the certificate exists. Photograph the damage and their equipment, get the crew lead's name, write it in the ticket, and tell the manager above you, who takes it to the vendor's insurer. **Do not accept "we'll just fix it"**: a quiet fix leaves no claim and no history.

A **tenant** who backs into a post or damages a fence is responsible under the lease. Follow the incident order, raise the repair ticket, and the manager above you decides how the cost is billed or claimed. It is never negotiated at the counter.

## What a Manager Never Says

| Never | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "We'll cover it." | "I am documenting everything and passing it to my manager." |
| "That was our fault." | "Here is what I saw and photographed." |
| "Your insurance won't need to know." | Nothing. Every incident is recorded. |
| "Just fix it and we're square," to a vendor | "Please send your report; my manager will be in touch." |

## At Greens

The manager clears the row, photographs everything, calls the tenant, raises an emergency ticket for the roof, files the incident form and calls the manager above him, who calls the carrier. The roofer tarps the roof and waits for the adjuster. The trailer goes to the tenant's insurer with the site's photos.

## Common Mistakes

- **Permanent repairs before the adjuster.**
- **Letting a vendor quietly fix what it broke.**
- **Promising a tenant the property will cover a loss.**
- **Footage overwritten** because nobody asked the same hour.

## Key Points

- Four risks, four policies; the manager's job is the evidence, never the decision.
- After a storm: safety, photos, tenants, tickets, your manager, temporary protection only.
- An incident is a Form the same day, plus a ticket for the property's damage; preserve footage the same hour.
- Never admit fault, promise payment, settle at the counter or keep an incident off the record.
