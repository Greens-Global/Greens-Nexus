## The One Path for a Repair

1. **Ticket** with photos, place and priority (Report a Problem from a site tag, or Help Center).
2. **Warranty check** in Records or Item Management before any paid repair.
3. **Scope** in writing: where, what, standard, when, excluded.
4. **Quote and approval**, in writing on the ticket, by the level the amount needs. Never split a job.
5. **Visit**: papers checked that morning, signed in, no gate code, escorted, the Form filled.
6. **Inspect** against the scope, test it, photograph it, **Mark Resolved**.
7. **Invoice** matched three ways: ordered (scope and quote, or PO), received (inspection and photo), billed (invoice with the ticket number). Accounting pays; the site never does.

Emergency: safety, approved vendor, ticket from your phone, the manager above you the same day. Skip the quotes, never the record.

## Vendor Papers

License, certificate of insurance naming Greens as additional insured, workers comp, W-9. **Expiring Soon** may work; **Expired** and **No Insurance On File** may not.

## Lien Waivers

Conditional with the invoice, unconditional once paid; Unconditional Final on every commitment before close-out. Attach the vendor's own signed form; never write one.

## Staff Never

- Work on a gate operator, a door spring, an electrical panel, a sprinkler valve or a roof.
- Reset a breaker twice, silence an alarm panel, or move an extinguisher for an inspection.
- Give a vendor a gate code, open a rented unit for one, or move a tenant's vehicle.
- Start permanent storm repairs before the adjuster, or let a vendor quietly fix its damage.
- Say "we'll cover it," accept a gift, take side work, or pay a vendor from the site.

## Key Points

- The ticket is the file, Records is the memory, the books are the money.
- Check the warranty, then the papers, then approve in writing.
- Match ordered, received and billed before Accounting pays.
