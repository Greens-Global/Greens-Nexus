Your first Monday running Temecula. A tenant at the counter says his door is "heavy again," the gate took two tries this morning, and a vendor you have never met is in the lot asking for the code. None of it is a sales problem. All of it is the property, and from today the property is yours to run.

## The Building Is the Product

A storage site sells **space that is dry, secure and reachable**. There is no other product. A roll-up door that will not open, a gate that sticks, a roof that drips, a lot that floods, a dark light over the RV row: each one is the product failing in a tenant's hands. The person who runs the site runs the building.

## The Four Jobs

| Job | What it looks like on a Tuesday |
|---|---|
| **Protect the asset** | A gutter cleaned before the first rain; a bent bollard replaced before the wall takes the next hit |
| **Keep it rentable** | A vacated unit swept, its door checked and locked the same day |
| **Control the cost** | The right fix, by the right vendor, at the right price, at the right time |
| **Keep the record** | A ticket for every problem, a photo for every fix, a warranty and a permit filed where the next manager can find them |

Tenants ask for the second job; nobody asks for the first and fourth until a roof fails. Do all four.

## The Owner's Decisions and the Manager's

Greens owns its sites, so the owner is the company, and its decisions come through the budget and the approvals.

| The owner decides | The manager decides |
|---|---|
| The capital plan: a new roof, a repave, a new gate operator | How to keep today's roof, pavement and gate working until then |
| The annual budget and the spending limits at each level | What to spend inside those limits, and when to ask |
| Which vendors are approved for big work, and the contracts | Which approved vendor to call, and whether the work was done right |
| Insurance, claims and legal matters | The first response, the photos, the report, the call up the line |

When in doubt, ask up. A manager who asks about a large repair is doing the job; one who signs for it without asking is not.

**Asset Management** holds the property on four tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Details, company, Egnyte folder, the map point, and the **Needs Attention** card |
| **Maintenance** | Preventive Maintenance schedules, the maintenance records, the open tickets on the property |
| **Records** | Seven collections: Warranties, Inspections, Plans & Docs, Utilities, AHJ, Vendors, Permits |
| **Timeline** | Everything that happened, in order, with who did it |

Two rules make the rest of this course work:

- **Tickets are the work orders.** There is no second maintenance queue. A problem becomes a ticket (from Help Center, from a site tag's **Report a Problem**, or filed by a preventive schedule), the ticket is worked, and closing it writes the maintenance record.
- **Two vendor lists, two jobs.** The property's Records > Vendors is its own list of who services it, with contacts and insurance dates. The vendor Accounting PAYS is the Intacct vendor record under Accounting > Records > Vendors (see the lesson Vendors: Who Does What and How to Qualify Them). Nobody keys money into Nexus; what a vendor was paid is read from the books.

> [!NOTE]
> Nothing in Asset Management is ever deleted by code. A property moved to the Trash keeps its log. Write freely; the record is the point.

## At Greens

On her first week at Temecula, Priya reads the Overview. Two open tickets about the same gate, an overdue gate service on Needs Attention, and the gate vendor's insurance certificate expiring in five weeks. She does not touch the gate. She calls the approved gate vendor, asks for the renewed certificate, books the overdue service and both gate tickets into one visit, and writes the plan in the ticket. Four jobs, one morning.

## Common Mistakes

- **Treating the building as someone else's problem.** At a storage site it is the product.
- **Fixing the same thing three times** and never making it a preventive schedule.
- **Keeping the record in your head.** The next manager, the insurer and the auditor cannot read it there.
- **Deciding the owner's decisions**: signing for capital work, picking an unapproved vendor, settling a claim.

## Key Points

- A storage site sells dry, secure, reachable space; the building is the product.
- Four jobs: protect the asset, keep it rentable, control the cost, keep the record.
- The owner sets the capital plan, budget, approved vendors and claims; the manager runs the site inside them and asks up.
- Asset Management holds the property (Overview, Maintenance, Records, Timeline); tickets are the work orders.
