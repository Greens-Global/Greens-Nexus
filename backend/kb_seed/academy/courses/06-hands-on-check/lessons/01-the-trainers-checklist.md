Twenty minutes later you are walking the rows checking locks, and after that you are reading move-in charges out loud to a test tenant in Cubby. You have read about all of it. Today you show it.

## What the Hands-On Check Is

The Hands-On Check is the last course in the Greens Storage Academy. There is no quiz. It is an in-person sign-off that you can do the job, not only read about it. Your trainer watches you do three things on your own site:

This page is the trainer's checklist and your study guide. Read it first so nothing is a surprise.

- **When:** pick a quiet time when another team member can cover the counter and the phone.
- **Who signs you off:** your manager or a senior team member. In Nexus the trainer opens this course, chooses **Sign Off a Person**, picks you and adds a note. Until then the course shows the In-Person Sign-Off step; afterward it shows Completed In Person, and you get a Course Completed bell with the trainer's name. The sign-off is the trainer's call and needs no score in Nexus.
- **Before you start:** finish the other courses on your plan. You will use the call and score sheet from Customer Service & Sales Fundamentals, the site checks from Self-Storage Fundamentals, the lot walk from RV & Boat Storage Fundamentals and the move-in from Cubby Fundamentals.

Nothing here is meant to trip you up. The trainer wants you to succeed.

## Part 1: The Mock Sales Call

The trainer plays a caller and you answer as you would on the job: about 10 minutes of call, then about 10 minutes of scoring and talking it through. The trainer scores you on the score sheet from **The Call Score Sheet** lesson in Customer Service & Sales Fundamentals, the same items and the same rules.

**How the trainer runs it:**

- Pick a caller from **Practice: Twenty Scenarios, Part 1** or **Part 2** in that course, such as the price-only caller or "I'll think about it."
- If the site has vehicle spaces, run a second call from someone storing an RV or a boat. It tests whether you ask the length, the height and how often they use it.
- Stay in character, sit where you cannot see the learner's notes, and push back once when the learner asks for the rental.
- Mark each item Yes, No or N/A. Not asking is a No. Score = Yes divided by the items that applied.

**The pass mark** is 70% of the items that applied. A wrong price or fee, calling the protection plan "insurance," or promising something outside your authority is not a pass, whatever the percentage.

**Trainer's tally:**

- [ ] Score worked out: Yes divided by the items that applied, N/A removed
- [ ] Score is 70% or higher
- [ ] No wrong price or fee, no "insurance," no promise outside the learner's authority
- [ ] Learner and trainer picked the two items that would help most next time

## Part 2: The Guided Site Walk

You lead the trainer around the site for about 30 minutes, talking through what you check and why. The trainer asks questions and points out anything you pass. The checks are the ones from **Running the Site: Open to Close** (Self-Storage Fundamentals) and **Walking the Lot** (RV & Boat Storage Fundamentals).

**Rules for the walk:** bring your phone, charged, with Nexus and Cubby open. Never move a tenant's vehicle or open a tenant's unit. Watch for traffic, especially a rig backing up. Record exceptions with a dated photo and a note, in Cubby and in Nexus.

**The lock check:**

- [ ] Walks every row in the site's route order
- [ ] Checks each rented unit for a lock and a fully closed door
- [ ] Knows the steps for a cut or damaged lock: photos, the manager, the tenant, camera and gate logs
- [ ] Reconciles what is seen to Cubby: a lock on a vacant unit, no lock on a rented one, overlocks that are wrong or missing
- [ ] Changes no overlock without the manager's go-ahead

**The vacant unit check:**

- [ ] Opens a vacant unit: empty and clean; floor, walls, door and lights free of damage, leaks and pests
- [ ] Confirms the facility lock is on and Cubby shows it vacant and rentable
- [ ] Says what happens before it is rented again

**The vehicle lot walk** (skip if the site has no vehicle spaces and the trainer agrees):

- [ ] Matches each vehicle to its Cubby record: type, plate, the vehicle on the lease
- [ ] Spots an unauthorized vehicle, one in the wrong space, or anything past the line or in a fire lane
- [ ] Checks tags, tires, jacks, leaks, covers and tarps
- [ ] Records one exception with a dated photo and a note
- [ ] Walks it safely, on foot or in a cart at walking speed

**Trainer's tally:**

- [ ] All checks done, with the why behind each step
- [ ] The learner stopped and asked the manager at the right moments

## Part 3: The Supervised Move-In in Cubby

You do a move-in in Cubby with the trainer beside you, for about 30 minutes, following **Cubby How-To: Move-In**.

> [!WARNING]
> Never practice on a real tenant's ledger without the manager. Use a test tenant if your manager has set one up. A real move-in needs the manager present, a real new renter, and the manager's OK on every charge before payment. Never run a test on a real, occupied unit or a real person's card.

**Steps the trainer watches for:**

- [ ] Starts from the lead or reservation if there is one
- [ ] Picks the unit type, then the exact unit, and checks it is vacant and rentable on the site map
- [ ] For a vehicle space, checks the fit: length plus a margin, and height for covered or enclosed spaces
- [ ] Reads each line of the move-in charges out loud and checks the total against the quote, using **Greens Fees and Charges** for the current fees
- [ ] Sets coverage: the Greens plan, the tenant's own policy (with proof) or none, asking the manager before None
- [ ] Takes the payment and waits for Cubby to show it succeeded; knows what a decline and a pending payment mean
- [ ] Offers autopay
- [ ] Gets the lease signed and approved, records the ID and the vehicle type
- [ ] Sets the gate code, only after the payment succeeded, and attaches the lock if one is sold
- [ ] Adds a useful note, walks the tenant to the unit, and leaves nothing showing Incomplete

**Trainer's tally:**

- [ ] Charges read to the customer line by line
- [ ] No gate code or lock handed out before the payment succeeded
- [ ] Every document complete; any test lease cleaned up with the manager

## If a Part Is Not Ready Yet

You do not pass or fail the Hands-On Check as a whole. Each part stands alone.

- The trainer tells you which items to work on and the two or three habits that would change the most.
- You redo only that part, on another day. Parts you did well stay done.
- Under 70% on the call means practice with a coworker, then another try, often with a different caller.
- The sign-off waits until all three parts are done. The course keeps showing the In-Person Sign-Off step in the meantime.

## Key Points

- In person, about 90 minutes: a mock sales call, a guided site walk and a supervised Cubby move-in. No quiz.
- Your manager or a senior team member signs you off with Sign Off a Person; the course then shows Completed In Person.
- The call is scored on The Call Score Sheet: 70% of the items that applied, and no wrong price, no "insurance," no promise outside your authority.
- Never practice on a real tenant's ledger without the manager. No gate code or lock before the payment succeeds.
- A part that is not ready is redone on its own later. Nothing is failed.
