## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Explain what happens in the Hands-On Check, how long it takes and who signs you off.
- Run a mock sales call and be scored on the 22-item call score sheet, with 70% as the pass mark.
- Do a lock check, a vacant unit check and a vehicle lot walk on a real site with your trainer.
- Do a move-in in Cubby with your trainer beside you, without giving out a gate code before the payment succeeds.
- Know what happens if a part is not ready yet.

## What the Hands-On Check Is

The Hands-On Check is the last course in the Greens Storage Academy. It is not a quiz. It is an in-person sign-off that you can do the job, not only read about it. Your trainer watches you do three things on your own site:

1. A mock sales call.
2. A guided site walk.
3. A supervised move-in in Cubby.

This page is the trainer's checklist and your study guide. The trainer works from it, and you can read it first so nothing is a surprise.

**How long:** about 90 minutes in all. Plan roughly 30 minutes for each of the three parts. Pick a time when the site is quiet and another team member can cover the counter and the phone.

**Who signs you off:** your manager or a senior team member. In Nexus the trainer opens the Hands-On Check course, chooses Sign Off a Person, picks you from the list and adds a note. Until then the course shows Waiting for Review. When the trainer signs you off, the course completes and you get a Course Completed bell with the trainer's name. The sign-off is the trainer's call, and it does not need a score in Nexus.

**Before you start:** finish the first five courses. You will use what is in them: the nine parts of a call and the score sheet from Customer Service and Sales Fundamentals, the walk-throughs from Self-Storage Fundamentals, the lot walk from RV and Boat Storage Fundamentals and the move-in from Cubby Fundamentals.

> [!NOTE]
> Nothing here is meant to trip you up. The trainer wants you to succeed, and any part can be done again.

## Part 1: The Mock Sales Call

**What happens:** the trainer plays a caller and you answer the phone as you would on the job. The call lasts about 10 minutes, followed by about 10 minutes of scoring and talking it through. The trainer scores you with the same 22-item Greens call score sheet from lesson 6 of Customer Service and Sales Fundamentals.

**How the trainer runs it:**

- Pick a caller from the practice scenarios in lessons 16 and 17 of that course, for example the Price-Only Caller, the Competitor Is $20 Cheaper or "I'll Think About It."
- Play one caller who rents a unit and, if the site has vehicle spaces, one who stores an RV or a boat, such as Scenario 20, An RV Owner Shopping Covered Parking. Run at least one call; run both if time allows. The RV or boat caller is a good test of item 8, asking the length, the height and how often they use it.
- Stay in character. Sit where you cannot see the learner's notes. Ask the questions a real caller would ask, and push back once when the learner asks for the rental.
- Mark each item Yes, No or N/A. Not asking is a No. Score = Yes divided by the items that applied.

**The pass mark:** 70% of the items that applied. A wrong price or fee, calling the protection plan "insurance," or promising something outside your authority is not a pass, whatever the percentage.

**The 22 items:**

- [ ] 1. Answered within 3 rings
- [ ] 2. Greeting with property name and own name
- [ ] 3. Asked why they were calling or offered help
- [ ] 4. Asked whether they had rented before
- [ ] 5. Asked what they are storing
- [ ] 6. Asked move-in date
- [ ] 7. Asked length of stay
- [ ] 8. Asked about climate-sensitive items (for vehicles: length, height and how often they use it)
- [ ] 9. Recommended a specific size and explained why
- [ ] 10. Named 2 or more features tied to stated needs
- [ ] 11. Stated what makes this property different
- [ ] 12. Quoted price, special and fees accurately
- [ ] 13. Mentioned the protection plan
- [ ] 14. Mentioned supplies or locks
- [ ] 15. Asked for the rental or reservation
- [ ] 16. Offered an appointment with a set time
- [ ] 17. Captured name, phone and email
- [ ] 18. Created urgency honestly
- [ ] 19. Closed with the property name and thanks
- [ ] 20. Tone: warm, unhurried, not scripted
- [ ] 21. Took control of the call
- [ ] 22. Rapport

Items 20 to 22 are judgments. Rate each from 1 to 5, and a 4 or 5 counts as a Yes.

**Trainer's tally:**

- [ ] Yes marks counted and N/A items removed
- [ ] Score worked out: Yes divided by the items that applied
- [ ] Score is 70% or higher
- [ ] No wrong price or fee, no "insurance," no promise outside the learner's authority
- [ ] The learner and trainer picked the two items that would help most next time

## Part 2: The Guided Site Walk

**What happens:** you and the trainer walk the site together for about 30 minutes. You lead and talk through what you check and why; the trainer asks questions and points out anything you pass. This is practice with the same checklists you learned, on the real site.

**Rules for the walk:**

- Bring your phone, charged, with Nexus and Cubby open.
- Never move a tenant's vehicle or open a tenant's unit.
- Watch for traffic, especially a rig backing up.
- Record exceptions with a dated photo and a note, in Cubby and in Nexus, as you learned.

**The lock check** (Self-Storage Fundamentals, Running the Site):

- [ ] Walks every row in the same order as the site's route
- [ ] Checks each rented unit for a lock, and that the door is fully closed
- [ ] Spots a cut or damaged lock and knows the next steps: photos, report to the manager, notify the tenant, check camera and gate logs
- [ ] Reconciles what is seen to Cubby: a tenant lock on a vacant unit, no lock on a rented unit, an overlock on a paid tenant, no overlock where one is needed
- [ ] Does not remove or put on an overlock without the manager's go-ahead during the check

**The vacant unit check:**

- [ ] Opens a vacant unit and checks it is empty and clean
- [ ] Checks the floor, walls, door and lights for damage, leaks and pests
- [ ] Confirms the facility lock is on and the unit shows vacant and rentable in Cubby
- [ ] Says out loud what would be done before this unit is rented: confirm the tenant removed their lock and everything inside, clean, repair, reset the cylinder core if the site uses them, put on the facility lock

**The vehicle lot walk** (RV and Boat Storage Fundamentals, Walking the Lot; skip if the site has no vehicle spaces and your trainer agrees):

- [ ] Walks every row and matches each vehicle to its Cubby record: type, plate and the vehicle on the lease
- [ ] Spots an unauthorized vehicle or one in the wrong space
- [ ] Checks that nothing sticks past the line, into the aisle or a fire lane
- [ ] Checks registration tags, tires, jacks, leaks, covers and tarps
- [ ] Records one exception with a dated photo and a note in Cubby and in Nexus
- [ ] Walks it safely: in a cart at walking speed or on foot, and never moves a tenant's vehicle

**Trainer's tally:**

- [ ] All three checks done
- [ ] The learner explained the why behind each step, not only the step
- [ ] The learner stopped and asked the manager at the right moments

## Part 3: The Supervised Move-In in Cubby

**What happens:** you do a move-in in Cubby with the trainer next to you, for about 30 minutes. It follows Cubby How-To: Move-In from Cubby Fundamentals.

> [!WARNING]
> Never practice on a real tenant's ledger without the manager. Use a test tenant if your manager has set one up. If you do a real move-in, the manager must be present, the customer must be a real new renter, and the manager signs off every charge before you take payment. Do not run a test move-in on a real, occupied unit or a real person's card.

**Steps the trainer watches for:**

- [ ] Starts from the lead or reservation if there is one, so the lead converts
- [ ] Picks the unit type, then the exact unit, and checks it is vacant and rentable on the site map
- [ ] For a vehicle space, checks the fit: vehicle length plus a margin, and the height for covered or enclosed spaces
- [ ] Opens the move-in charges preview and reads each line out loud
- [ ] Checks the total against what was quoted, and stops to ask if something looks wrong
- [ ] Knows the Administration Fee is $25.00 ($10.00 on a mailbox) and that jump day 26 changes the charges
- [ ] Sets coverage: Greens plan, own policy (with number, expiration and a copy) or none, and asks the manager before choosing None
- [ ] Takes the payment, and waits for Cubby to show it succeeded
- [ ] Explains what happens if the payment is declined (Cubby undoes the lease and keeps the lead) and if it shows pending (do not run it again; ask the manager)
- [ ] Offers autopay
- [ ] Gets the lease signed and approved, checks and records the ID, and fills the vehicle type
- [ ] Sets the gate code and checks it is enabled, only after the payment succeeded
- [ ] Attaches the lock if one is sold or given
- [ ] Adds a useful note for the next person
- [ ] Walks the tenant to the unit or space and shows them the gate, the hours and the rules
- [ ] Leaves nothing on the lease showing Incomplete

**Trainer's tally:**

- [ ] The charges were read to the customer line by line
- [ ] No gate code or lock was handed out before the payment succeeded
- [ ] Every document is complete
- [ ] Any test lease or test tenant was cleaned up with the manager afterward

## If Something Is Not Ready Yet

You do not pass or fail the Hands-On Check as a whole. Each part stands alone.

- If a part is not ready, the trainer tells you exactly which items to work on and the two or three habits that would change the most.
- You redo that part later, on another day. You do not redo the parts you already did well.
- A score under 70% on the mock call means practice with a coworker on the same scenarios, then another try. The trainer can use a different caller.
- The sign-off in Nexus waits until all three parts are done. The course stays at Waiting for Review in the meantime, and there is nothing to fail.

The goal is a new team member who is safe on the phone, on the lot and on the ledger. Taking another go is part of getting there.

## Key Points

- The Hands-On Check is in person and takes about 90 minutes: a mock sales call, a guided site walk and a supervised move-in in Cubby. There is no quiz.
- Your manager or a senior team member signs you off. In Nexus the course shows Waiting for Review until the trainer signs you off.
- The mock call uses the same 22-item score sheet as Customer Service and Sales Fundamentals: 70% of the items that applied, with no wrong price or fee.
- The site walk covers the lock check, a vacant unit check and the vehicle lot walk.
- Never practice on a real tenant's ledger without the manager, and never give out a gate code or lock before the payment succeeds.
- If a part is not ready yet, you redo that part later. Nothing is failed.
