## How to Use This Page

Keep it open at the counter and on the lot. Each line points to the lesson that teaches it.

## The Fit Check

1. "What are you storing?" Type, year, make, model.
2. "Overall length, hitch to bumper, with anything on the back?" A boat: tongue tip to the back of the motor. A fifth wheel: front of the nose or pin box.
3. Add **3 to 4 ft**. Offer the smallest space at least that long.
4. Covered or enclosed: height to the **highest point**, compared with the lowest point on the path, with room to spare. Your manager has the clear heights until the site profile is filled in. Never guess.
5. Mirrors, slides and awnings: a wide rig wants a 12 ft space.
6. "How do you usually park it?" Hates backing: a pull-through.
7. Write it in Cubby; tape it on move-in day.

(Measuring for the Right Fit; The Shape of a Space)

## The Move-In

- [ ] Photo ID matching the registration and insurance card
- [ ] Current registration for every vehicle, boat **and** trailer, in the tenant's name
- [ ] The tenant's own insurance; policy number and expiration in Cubby
- [ ] VIN or HIN matching on every document, or nothing goes in
- [ ] Make, model, year, color, plate, plate state, Cubby vehicle type
- [ ] Title holder and every lienholder
- [ ] Not running, or on PNO: your manager's OK first
- [ ] Authorized drivers listed; gate access for them only
- [ ] Photos: four sides, damage, plate and VIN or HIN, the space
- [ ] House rules walked through

(Paperwork and Move-In; Greens Vehicle Storage Rules)

## The Lot Rules, With the Reason

| Rule | Say |
|---|---|
| No living or sleeping | "A lot isn't a campground; it's a fire and safety risk for everyone." |
| No repairs or oil changes | "The runoff goes straight into the storm drain." |
| No fuel or propane containers; tanks under 1/4 | "A small fire becomes a lot fire." |
| No generator without written permission | "Fire, fumes and noise." |
| Trailers on blocks, chocked, hitch lock on | "So it can't roll or sink into the ground." |
| Only the leased vehicle, nothing beside it | "Spaces are sized for one vehicle." |

Gas or smoke: **leave and call 911**, then your manager. (Lot Rules and Risks)

## The Walk

- Same route, every row, every space; match each vehicle to Cubby.
- Tags, tires, jacks and chocks, leaks, covers, hatches, slides, cords, signs of living, items beside, site damage, height signs.
- Every exception: a dated photo and a note.
- Tenant issue: Cubby note and a call. Site issue: scan the QR tag, **Report a Problem**. The walk: **Fill a Form**, the form your manager picks.
- Incident: safety, photos, names and insurance, no fault, Incident Report the same day, your manager.

(Walking the Lot)

## The Vehicle Lien Path

- Never the unit auction.
- Until the 61st day: the unit ladder (Delinquency and the Lien Process).
- The 61st day task goes to your manager, with the lease, VIN or HIN, plate, lienholders, insurance and fresh photos.
- Never tow, move, lock or boot a vehicle; never promise a date; never take "keep it" as permission.

(Vehicle and Boat Liens)

## Selling and Keeping

- Ask before you quote; quote from Cubby.
- Nothing open in the product they want: open space now, waitlist with the measurements, upgrade later.
- Lot full: check a sister site in Cubby before you send anyone.
- Never "we're insured," never "nothing will happen to it."

(Selling Vehicle Storage; Waitlists and Upgrades)
