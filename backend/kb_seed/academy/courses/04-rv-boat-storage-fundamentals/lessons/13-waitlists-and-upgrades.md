## The Moment

A caller wants a covered 12x50 at Temecula for a 44 ft fifth wheel. Every long covered space is leased. "Call back in six months" loses her today. An open space that fits, a full waitlist entry and a clear upgrade path keep her on a Greens lot tonight.

## Scarce Spaces

Some spaces are almost always full: **covered** (Temecula only), **enclosed vehicle garages** (Valley Center only), **pull-throughs** (Murrieta and Temecula), **spaces 40 ft and longer** at every site, and nearly any space at **Murrieta**. When one opens, the right customer should get it, found fast.

## A Good Waitlist Entry

A name and a phone number are not enough. Capture what lets you call and say "I have your space" without a second fit-check:

- [ ] Name, phone and email
- [ ] Type, year, make and model
- [ ] Length hitch to bumper, and whether a tongue folds
- [ ] Height to the highest point, for covered or enclosed
- [ ] The product: covered, enclosed, pull-through, a size
- [ ] The sites they would accept
- [ ] The open space they rented meanwhile, if any
- [ ] The date they asked

Where Greens keeps the waitlist is your manager's call. Use one place only, so nobody gets skipped. When you call someone from it, re-check the length and height; customers trade up to bigger rigs.

## When Your Lot Is Full

| The caller needs | Try |
|---|---|
| 40 ft and longer | Temecula, with the most long spaces; Fairfield's 12x48s |
| Covered | Temecula only |
| Enclosed vehicle space | Valley Center (usually a waitlist) |
| A pull-through | Murrieta or Temecula |
| The lowest price | Fairfield |
| Paved | Murrieta; paved rows at Temecula and Escondido |

Check the other site's open spaces in Cubby before you send anyone. A caller sent to a sister site that turns out full is a caller lost twice.

## Seasonal Move-Outs

Some owners take the rig away for a season and want to stop paying. Be honest: the rental is month to month, so they may leave, but **the space may not be there when they come back**, especially a long, covered or pull-through space. Offer the waitlist for their return date; some choose to keep the space. Let them decide, and note the reason in Cubby.

## Keeping an Unhappy Vehicle Tenant

A unit tenant who leaves has to empty a unit. A vehicle tenant hitches up and is gone in 15 minutes. You may get one conversation.

They leave over a rate increase, damage they blame on the site, a neighbor's rig parked too close or leaking, a gate code that fails at 4:00 AM, a dark lot, or a better offer.

1. **Listen first.** Let them finish before you explain anything.
2. **Thank them for telling you.** Most unhappy tenants just leave.
3. **Fix what you can now:** reset a gate code, report the leaking neighbor.
4. **Offer what you are allowed to:** a better space, the covered waitlist, another site. Credits follow Greens Waivers, Credits and Refunds.
5. **Escalate the rest** (rate questions, damage claims) to your manager, with notes.
6. **Call back when you said you would.**

> [!WARNING]
> Never admit fault for damage or promise to pay for it while trying to keep a tenant. Document it and pass it on.

## At Greens

**Temecula.** The 44 ft fifth wheel needs 47 ft with the margin, longer than Temecula's open spaces. You check Cubby for a 12x48 at Fairfield or a 12x50 at Murrieta, offer what is open, and add her to Temecula's covered waitlist with the length and height.

**Murrieta.** A 27 ft trailer needs 30 ft, and nothing that long is open. You find an 11x35 at Temecula in Cubby and offer it.

**Escondido.** A snowbird will take her motorhome to the desert from November to March. You explain the space may not be there in March and note her return date for the waitlist.

## Common Mistakes

- **A waitlist entry with no measurements.** You cannot make an offer from it.
- **Upgrading without re-checking the height.**
- **Sending a caller to a sister site without checking it in Cubby.**

## Key Points

- Covered, enclosed, pull-through and long spaces are scarce; keep one waitlist with the fit details.
- Open now, covered later keeps the customer; re-check the fit at the upgrade.
- Know which Greens site fits a caller yours cannot, and check it first.
- A vehicle tenant can leave in 15 minutes: listen, fix, offer what you may, escalate, follow up.
