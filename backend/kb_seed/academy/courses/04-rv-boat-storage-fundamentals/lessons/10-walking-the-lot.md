## The Moment

On your walk at Fairfield, row C looks normal until you stop: a cover is gone, a stain is spreading under a fifth wheel, and the trailer in C14 is not the one Cubby says is there. Found today, each is a phone call. Found next month, each is a claim. The walk is how you find them today.

## Before You Start

- Phone charged, with Nexus and Cubby open.
- **The same route every time,** so no row is ever skipped. Cubby can sort spaces in the site's walk order.
- Walk the lot, or drive a cart or company vehicle at walking speed. Never a tenant's vehicle.
- A driver backing a 40 ft rig cannot see you. Stay out of the swing.

## The Checklist

At every space:

- [ ] The vehicle matches the Cubby record: type, plate, the vehicle on the lease.
- [ ] No wrong-space or extra vehicle, and no car parked in an RV space while the RV is out.
- [ ] Nothing past the line, in the aisle or in a fire lane.
- [ ] Tags current (month and year on the sticker), or PNO noted in Cubby.
- [ ] No flat tires, sunk jacks, missing chocks or a vehicle leaning.
- [ ] No leaks or new stains.
- [ ] Covers whole and tied down; no tarps holding water.
- [ ] Hatches and windows closed; slides in, awnings closed.
- [ ] No cords to outlets, no generator running.
- [ ] No signs of anyone living there: lights at night, pets, trash.
- [ ] Nothing stored beside or under the vehicle.
- [ ] Site damage: hit posts, bent beams, faded lines, missing space numbers, clear height signs missing or unreadable.

At the gate: it opens, closes and holds long enough for a long rig; the keypad and lights work; nothing blocks the stacking lane.

## Where Each Finding Goes

Every exception gets **a dated photo and a note**. A problem with no photo did not happen, as far as a later dispute is concerned.

| You found | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| Something about a tenant's vehicle or account: a leak, a flat, expired tags, wrong space | A note on the lease in Cubby with the photo, and a call to the tenant |
| Something broken at the site: the gate, a light, a post, a canopy panel | Nexus: scan the site's QR tag and tap **Report a Problem**, or file a ticket |
| The walk itself | Nexus: **Fill a Form** on Workday (also in the + menu) |
| Damage, injury or theft | The incident steps below, the same day |

**Which form?** Nexus has no single lot-walk form for every site. Your manager picks the form your site uses: it sits in Fill a Form under its category, usually **Inspection** or **Vehicle Check**, and an incident goes on an **Incident Report** form. Fill a Form works with no signal and sends when you are back in range, and it keeps your photos with the place and the time.

## A Damage Incident, in Order

A tenant backs into a post, a trailer swings into a neighbor, wind throws a canopy panel, someone steals a battery.

1. **Safety first.** Anyone hurt? Anything leaking, smoking or unstable? 911 if needed.
2. **Photograph everything:** both vehicles, plates, the space, the post, the ground, near and far.
3. **Get names and insurance information.**
4. **Do not admit fault or promise payment.** "I'm documenting everything and passing it to my manager."
5. **File the Incident Report form the same day,** with the photos.
6. **Tell your manager,** who decides whether to notify Greens' insurer.

> [!WARNING]
> Never say "we'll pay for that" or "that's our fault," even to be kind. Your manager and the insurers decide.

## Weather Walks

Walk after any weather event, before tenants call you.

| Weather | Look for |
|---|---|
| **Wind** (Fairfield's open lot especially) | Loose or torn covers, awnings out, canopy panels lifted, debris in aisles |
| **Heat** | Soft pavement and sinking jacks, tires, batteries |
| **Rain** | Standing water, low gravel spaces, boat covers pooling, chocks washed out |
| **Freezing nights** | RVs not winterized; tell those tenants |

Then photograph every problem, call the affected tenants (say what you saw, not who is responsible), report site damage in Nexus, and tell your manager about anything big.

## At Greens

**Temecula.** Space 214's record shows a travel trailer; a pickup is parked there. It is the tenant's truck, left while the trailer is out. You explain that the space is for the vehicle on the lease and note the call.

**Murrieta.** A tenant clips a bollard and cracks a taillight. Nobody is hurt. You photograph the rig, the bollard and the space, take his insurance details, say you are passing it on, and file the Incident Report that day.

**Escondido.** An extension cord runs from a light pole to a trailer. You photograph it, unplug nothing, and call your manager.

## Common Mistakes

- **Walking a different route each day.** The row you skip is the one with the leak.
- **A finding with no photo.** It cannot be proved later.
- **Logging a broken gate on a tenant's lease.** Site problems go to Report a Problem, where maintenance sees them.
- **Telling a tenant who is at fault** after a storm or a collision.

## Key Points

- Same route, every row, every space; match each vehicle to Cubby.
- Every exception gets a dated photo and a note.
- Tenant issues in Cubby; site issues through Report a Problem; the walk on the form your manager picks in Fill a Form.
- Incident: safety, photos, names and insurance, no fault, Incident Report the same day, tell your manager.
