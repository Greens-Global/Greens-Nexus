Keep this page open the next time a number on a report surprises you. Each line points to the lesson that teaches it.

## The Rules That Never Change

- **Intacct is the ledger.** Nexus Accounting reads a copy and never posts. (One Ledger, Two Doors)
- **Every posted figure clicks.** Figure, then its General Ledger lines, then the entry. (The Reports and the Drill-Down)
- **Nobody keys money into Nexus.** Questions and requests go in; Accounting decides and posts.
- **Only the bookkeeper pushes a reclass.** Anyone may ask. (Ask My Accountant and the Audit Pages)

## Where to Look

| You want to know | Open |
|---|---|
| Why a cost is high | The figure on the Income Statement, then its lines |
| What one vendor was paid | Reports > Ledger & Banking > Spend by Vendor, or the vendor's Ledger Lines tab |
| What a Construction job cost | Income Statement by Job, filtered to its Project-Job |
| A line you know was posted is missing | Sync, then ask Accounting |
| Why two reports disagree | The Book picker and the entity picker on each |
| A vendor's insurance and papers | Records > Vendors > the vendor > Compliance |
| Whether a recurring bill came in | MRE: Paid, Short, Over, Missed, Upcoming |
| A tenant's balance | Cubby, or Operations > Reports > Aged Receivables |
| Which deposit holds which card payments | Operations > Reports > Merchant Services Disbursements |
| Whether a bank account is reconciled | Dashboard > Close > Account Reconciliations |

## What to Click

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| Ask about a line | The question mark at the end of the line, then Send Question |
| Move a line to the right account | On AMA: Pick GL, then Send To Bookkeeper |
| Keep a report view | Memorize |
| Build your own report | Reports > Report Builder; Share With Everyone to share it |
| Change a vendor's details | Edit the record; it waits as Pending Approval, then Approved - Awaiting Intacct |

## Who Does What

| You | Accounting |
|---|---|
| Send tickets and invoices with the property and the ticket number | Code, pay and post in Intacct |
| Ask on the line | Answer, reclass, resolve |
| Answer variance questions the same day | Run the close and reconcile the accounts |
| Send a new vendor's papers before the first visit | Set the vendor up in Intacct |
