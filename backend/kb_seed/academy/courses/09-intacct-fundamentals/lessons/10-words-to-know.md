## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Use the Intacct and Nexus Accounting words correctly with the accounting team.
- Find the page a word belongs to.

## How to Use This Lesson

The general accounting words (accrual, allowance, depreciation, NOI) are in the Fundamentals of Accounting glossary. Here are the words that belong to Intacct and to the Nexus Accounting module.

## The Ledger and Its Shape

| Word | What it means | Where you meet it |
|---|---|---|
| **Sage Intacct** | The accounting system Greens' ledger lives in | Everywhere; the one set of books |
| **Greens Accounting** | The standalone app, built to look like Intacct, where the accounting team works in the ledger | Launch, from Nexus Accounting |
| **Mirror** | The accounting app's copy of the Intacct ledger that Nexus reads | Why figures refresh every few minutes |
| **Entity (location)** | A set of books inside the ledger: a company, a property, a family matter | The picker on every page; Records > Entities |
| **Sub-entity, child code** | A part of an entity kept on its own under the parent's code with a suffix (15001-1) | Ledger lines; the Entities page |
| **Consolidated** | Every entity the person may read, added up with intercompany removed | The Dashboard tabs; a report with no entity chosen |
| **Entity access** | The list of entities a person may read; All Entities by default | Tools > Access, Full level only |
| **Dimension** | A tag on every ledger line: entity, department, vendor, customer, employee, Project-Job, item, journal | The filters on every report; Buckets |
| **Project-Job** | The dimension a construction job's costs carry | A job's Money tab; Buckets by Project-Job |
| **Journal** | The door an entry came through: APJ, ARJ, PRJ, cash journals, GJ, user-defined, statistical | The journal filter; Records > Journals |
| **Statistical journal** | Quantities, not dollars; never adds into a money total | Rent per square foot; occupancy on Performance |
| **Book** | A timing rule for reading the ledger: Accrual, Cash, a user-defined book | The Book picker; Both for side by side |
| **User-defined book** | Accrual plus the book's own journals, kept for a purpose | Named by the accounting team |

## Reports and Records

| Word | What it means | Where you meet it |
|---|---|---|
| **Report Center** | The shelf of statements and shared memorized reports | Reports |
| **Memorized report** | A saved report definition; a named period moves with the calendar | Report Center; Packages |
| **Drill-down** | A figure opens its General Ledger lines; a line opens its entry | Every posted figure, everywhere |
| **General Ledger lines** | Doc, memo, entity, vendor, customer, item, employee, department, debit, credit, running balance | Under every figure; Ledger Search |
| **Report Builder** | Accounts, entities, dimensions, period, book, row groups, compare; save, publish, share | Report Center |
| **Row group** | A named total gathering several accounts | Report Builder |
| **Package** | A lender's set of memorized reports, built to one PDF | Reports > Packages; Loans |
| **Buckets** | An account group's lines grouped by one dimension | Reports |
| **Trial Balance** | Every account's balance; where the close starts | Reports |
| **Vendor request** | A new vendor asked for in Nexus: Pending Approval, then Approved, Awaiting Intacct | Records > Vendors |
| **Compliance tab** | A vendor's insurance certificate, workers comp, license and W-9, with the one state word | A vendor record; a construction job's Subs |
| **Leasing tenant** | A commercial tenant who is an Intacct customer; rent received is what posted | Records > Customers; MRI |

## The Recurring Picture and the Audit Pages

| Word | What it means | Where you meet it |
|---|---|---|
| **MRI** | Monthly Recurring Income: leases, interest and loan payments coming in, expected beside actual | Audit & Insights |
| **MRE** | Monthly Recurring Expenses: a vendor paid on a schedule from the same accounts, expected beside actual | Audit & Insights |
| **Ended** | A lease whose tenant left; kept, never overwritten | MRI |
| **Loans, Planning, Stress** | Each loan's terms and schedule; what a refinance would do; what a higher rate would do | Financing |
| **Debt service coverage** | NOI divided by debt service; what the lender watches | Loans > Stress |
| **Management fee** | What the operating company charges a property entity for running it | Management Fees; a site's income statement |
| **Intercompany** | Balances between Greens entities, removed on consolidation | Financing |
| **Ask My Accountant (AMA)** | The suspense account and the page where questions on lines are asked and answered | Audit & Insights; any drill-down |
| **Other Side** | The account the money came from or went to, shown with the question | Ask My Accountant |
| **GL Reclass** | A request to move a line to another account; pushed only by the bookkeeper | Ask My Accountant |
| **Push** | The bookkeeper's edit of the original entry, previewed first | Ask My Accountant |
| **Manual Journal Entries** | Every entry a person keyed or changed, by whom | Audit & Insights |
| **Allocations** | Wages spread across entities by hours by work site, exported as an import file | Tools |
| **Import Hub** | A bank statement turned into an Intacct import template | Tools |
| **Close tab** | The month's steps and where the close stands | Dashboard |
| **PFS** | Personal financial statements; owners and the PFS grant only | Not part of this course |

## Key Points

- The ledger's shape: Intacct, the accounting app, the mirror, entities and child codes, consolidation, entity access, dimensions, journals and books.
- Reports and records: Report Center, memorized reports, the drill-down, Report Builder and row groups, Packages, Buckets, vendor requests and the Compliance tab, leasing tenants.
- The recurring picture and the audit pages: MRI, MRE, Loans with planning and stress, management fees, intercompany, Ask My Accountant with the Other Side and the reclass push, Manual Journal Entries, Allocations and Import Hub.
