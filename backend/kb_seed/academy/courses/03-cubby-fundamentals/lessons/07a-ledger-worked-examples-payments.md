The phone rings at Fairfield on the 2nd: a parking tenant has not paid since September and wants to know "what it takes to make this go away." With the ledger open and the four examples below in your head, you can give him one honest number. Every rent here is made up; the rules are Greens' real settings.

## Example 1: A Partial Payment

**Valley Center.** Rent **$120.00** with a **$14.00** protection plan. On 12/01/2026 both post, **$134.00**, and autopay is declined. On **12/03** the tenant pays **$100.00** at the counter.

Rent and protection posted together, so rent is paid first:

| Charge | Amount | Paid by the $100 | Still open |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rent, December | $120.00 | $100.00 | $20.00 |
| Protection, December | $14.00 | $0.00 | $14.00 |
| **Total** | **$134.00** | **$100.00** | **$34.00** |

- **Paid through:** stays 11/30/2026; December is not fully paid.
- On **12/11** the $34.00 is still open, so CA Late Fee posts. For this rent Greens' rule gives **$20.00** (the arithmetic is in How a Storage Site Makes Money). New balance **$54.00**.

What to say on 12/03: "You've paid $100 today; $34 is still open. If it isn't paid by the 10th, a $20 late fee posts on the 11th."

## Example 2: A Prepayment

**Temecula.** A parking tenant at **$180.00** a month, own insurance, paid through **11/30/2026**. On **11/15** he pays **$540.00**. Nothing is open, so all of it becomes prepaid.

| Date | What happens | Prepaid left | Paid through |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11/15 | $540.00 in, all to prepaid | $540.00 | 11/30/2026 |
| 12/01 | Rent $180.00 posts, paid from prepaid | $360.00 | 12/31/2026 |
| 01/01 | Rent $180.00 posts, paid from prepaid | $180.00 | 01/31/2027 |
| 02/01 | Rent $180.00 posts, paid from prepaid | $0.00 | 02/28/2027 |
| 03/01 | Rent $180.00 posts; normal billing resumes | $0.00 | 02/28/2027 until paid |

Tell him: "Your account will show paid through November 30th until December's rent posts. The money is there." And if his rent goes up while he is prepaid, the prepaid runs out sooner: at $190.00 from 01/01, February leaves $190.00 - $170.00 = **$20.00** due on 02/01.

## Example 3: A Late Fee and a Waive

**Murrieta.** A parking tenant at **$160.00**, own insurance. On 12/01 rent posts and autopay is declined.

| Date | What happens | Balance |
|---|---|---|
| 12/01 | Rent posts; autopay declined, nothing posts for it | $160.00 |
| 12/02 | "2nd - Call Failed Autopay Tenants": voicemail, noted | $160.00 |
| 12/06 | "6th - Call Past Due Tenants": no answer, noted | $160.00 |
| 12/11 | CA Late Fee posts, $24.00 for this rent; Cubby changes the gate code that night | $184.00 |
| 12/12 | First late payment, inside your limit: you waive the $24.00, reason "first late payment, goodwill" | $160.00 |
| 12/12 | The tenant pays $160.00 by card | $0.00 |

Paid through is now 12/31. Before he hangs up, make sure he has a working gate code. Had he waited until 12/16, the $25.00 CA Preliminary Lien Notice (posted on the 15th) would be on the balance too: $209.00 before any waive. Whether you may waive, and how much, is on Greens Waivers, Credits and Refunds; above your limit, say "Let me ask my manager today and call you back."

## Example 4: Aging Buckets

**Fairfield.** The caller from the opening: **$130.00** a month, own insurance, paid through **09/30/2026**. **Today is 11/02/2026.**

**Days past due:** 09/30 to 11/02 is 33 days, minus 1 = **32**.

| Charge | Posted | Age on 11/02 | Bucket | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| October rent | 10/01 | 32 days | 30-60 | $130.00 |
| CA Late Fee | 10/11 | 22 days | 0-30 | $20.00 |
| CA Preliminary Lien Notice | 10/15 | 18 days | 0-30 | $25.00 |
| November rent | 11/01 | 1 day | 0-30 | $130.00 |
| **Total** | | | | **$305.00** |

**By bucket:** 0-30 = **$175.00**; 30-60 = **$130.00**.

- On Aged Receivables he appears in **two** buckets; on the collections view the whole lease is 32 days past due.
- If he pays $175.00 today, it pays the **oldest** charges first: October rent, the late fee, the notice fee. November rent stays open, so he is still past due.
- The number that "makes it go away" is the full **$305.00**. Around day 35 the next lien step and its fee arrive (Greens Delinquency Timeline).

## Common Mistakes

1. **Saying a partial payment stops the late fee.** Only payment in full does.
2. **Telling a prepaid tenant he is behind.**
3. **Promising a waive above your limit.**
4. **Quoting the newest charges as what a payment pays.** It pays the oldest first.

## Key Points

- A partial payment pays the oldest charges first and leaves the rest open; the steps go on.
- Prepaid money moves the paid-through date one month at a time.
- A waive takes one charge off now, with a reason, inside your limit.
- Aging sorts each charge by age; the full balance is what stops the process.
