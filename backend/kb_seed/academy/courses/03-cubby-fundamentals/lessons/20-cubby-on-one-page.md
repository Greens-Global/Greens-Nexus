## Check Before You Act

| Before you... | Check |
|---|---|
| Change anything | Right site, right tenant, right lease |
| Hand over a gate code or lock | The move-in payment **succeeded** |
| Run a payment again | It is not **pending** (never run a pending payment twice) |
| Make a collection call | Paid since the task was made? An exemption? |
| Waive | Your limit on Greens Waivers, Credits and Refunds; a real reason |
| Refund | Who approves; waive the charge too if it should not be owed |
| Overlock | Still unpaid, no exemption, the right unit |
| Start a lien | Payment, exemption, military, bankruptcy, death, dispute, signed lease, address |
| Give a code | You confirmed who you are speaking with |
| Close the day | Drawer matches Cubby; tasks completed with notes; move-ins complete |

## Ledger Rules

- Charges push the balance up; payments, waives and write-offs bring it down.
- A payment pays the **oldest charge first**; posted together, rent before protection.
- **Paid-through** moves only when a whole month is paid.
- **Days past due** = today minus the paid-through date, minus 1.
- A **partial payment** does not stop the late fee or the lien steps.
- A **declined** card posts nothing; a returned bank payment reopens what it paid.
- A **waive** cancels one charge now; a **credit** waits for the next bill; a **refund** reopens the charge unless it is also waived.
- **Prepaid** money is used as charges post.

## Move-In Math

- Rent and protection are prorated: monthly amount x days used / days in the month.
- On or after the **26th**, the tenant also pays all of next month.
- Plus the Administration Fee (Mailbox Administration Fee on a mailbox). No deposit.
- Read Cubby's total to the customer; never quote from memory.

## Cards by Phone

Straight into Cubby. Never written down, never read back in full, never by email or text, never in a note.

## What to Say

| Situation | Say |
|---|---|
| A small first bill | "Everyone pays on the 1st, so your first month was shortened to match." |
| A partial payment | "You've paid $___ today; $___ is still open, and the late fee posts on the 11th if it isn't paid." |
| A waive you are not sure of | "Let me check what I can do and call you back today." |
| A dispute about lockout timing | "I'll have my manager call you today." |
| A failed AutoPay | "Your autopay didn't go through, and I wanted to help you sort it before any late fee." |
