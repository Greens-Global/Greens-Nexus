## How a Course Reaches a Person

Training is built on **learning paths**: the onboarding path, **role paths** for a job title, **department training** and custom paths. A path is stages of courses and policies, and a person sees their ladder on My Learning.

| Way in | How |
|---|---|
| **By rule** | A path or rule for their role, department or company |
| **By hand** | You assign it, with a date or the course's default days |
| **By enrollment** | They start it themselves from the Catalog |
| **By renewal** | A certificate that renews puts the course back before it expires |

A rule is due either **within so many days of the person's start date** or **by one date for everyone**, with the days as grace for someone who joins after that date. Authors build paths from **Learning Paths** on the Courses screen, where every job title and department with no path is suggested one.

## Due Dates and Escalation

Reminders go to the learner. When a course is still not done some days after its due date, the **escalation** comes to you, once and then weekly. It is a nudge to a conversation, not a mark on the person: ask what is in the way (no time on the schedule, a locked quiz, a course that will not load) and fix that.

## Your Team's Compliance

Under **Knowledge Base & Training**, the **Manage** tab holds **Compliance** for your people: each course and policy, its status (Not Started, In Progress, Completed, Overdue, Expired), due dates, validity, and who is locked out of a quiz. It shows your direct reports by default. (Training > Compliance is your own record.) Act weekly on two things: **Overdue**, and **Locked**.

## A Course's Report

A course has a **report**: who is assigned, who is where, scores, tries used, who is locked, and the **Hardest Questions**, the ones people miss most. A hard question is a lesson that did not land or a question that is wrong; either way it is worth telling the course owner. The report also carries learners' ratings and comments, without names.

**Unlock** gives a locked person another round. The person's manager, the course owner and the Knowledge Base manager may unlock. Have the conversation first: what to reread, and why the tries went.

## Sign-Offs, Read and Sign, the Refresher

- **In-person courses** are completed when you or a trainer watch the person do the work and record it with **Sign Off a Person**, or for several in a session. The course then shows Completed In Person. Nothing is signed off that was not seen.
- **Read and Sign** policies are pages a person must read and sign. A new version asks for a new signature; a small fix does not.
- The **Refresher** asks each person three questions a day from courses they passed, on a widening schedule. It is never a score and never yours to chase.

## Keeping a Course Current

Every course says which Nexus modules it **teaches**. When What's New releases a change to one of them, the course is **flagged** for its owner. The owner reads the lessons that teach that module against the new screen and either publishes edits or clicks **Mark Reviewed**. The Help panel's **Learn This Screen** card offers the course on the screen it teaches, so your team can find the lesson from where they are stuck.

## At Greens

Dana asks Sam on Teams why he is behind. He has had no office time on the schedule, so she puts two hours of training on Thursday's shift in the planner. She opens the Customer Service course's report: Marco's misses all sit in one lesson, which is also among the course's Hardest Questions. She talks it through with him, clicks **Unlock**, and tells the course owner the lesson may need a clearer example.

## Common Mistakes

- **Assigning by hand with no path behind it**, so the next hire in that job gets nothing.
- **Reading an escalation as a mark on the person.**
- **Unlocking without the conversation.**
- **Signing someone off** for work you did not see.

## Key Points

- A course reaches a person by rule, by hand, by enrollment or by renewal.
- Escalations come to you once, then weekly; Manage > Compliance shows your team. Act on Overdue and Locked weekly.
- A course's report shows the Hardest Questions; unlock after a conversation.
- Sign Off a Person only for work seen. Courses are flagged when the screens they teach change.
