## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Find a person's record and know what a manager changes, what HR changes and what Ask HR is for.
- Explain the hiring packet's signing order and why it never changes.
- Run onboarding checklists and give a probation recommendation.
- Use the Performance tab as a leader: numbers, goals, reviews and 1:1s.
- Start an exit the one way and know what Nexus does with it.

## The People Module

**People** (the HR module, for managers with the grant and HR) holds every person's record: their job, department, company, manager, work site, start date, employment type, and the tabs that grow with them: Documents, Time, Checklists, Performance. Its tabs are People, Hiring, Organizational Chart, Time, Checklists, Analytics, Reports, Exits and Performance; which ones you see depends on your grant.

A leader reads the record and changes what the team's day needs; the record itself (name, title, department, pay, documents) is HR's, and a team member asks for a change through **Ask HR**, the confidential desk you cannot see into. That is deliberate: a pay question should not need to go through the person's manager.

## Hiring

Hiring runs under **People > Hiring**: the candidate, the votes and screens that come to you as approvals, the offer, and the **hiring packet**. One rule governs every document in it and never changes:

> [!NOTE]
> **The company never signs before the employee.** An offer letter is signed by the candidate only; HR's Verify And Send is a review, not a signature. Hiring packets, separation packages and every other HR packet are signed by the person first and countersigned by the company after. No pre-signed or countersigned document is ever sent out. (Neil, 10/22/2026.)

US employees' federal forms (W-4, I-9 with Section 2 by HR, direct deposit) are filled and signed in Nexus Sign from the packet; their sensitive fields are masked to everyone but the person and the form, and never appear in a bell, an email or on the record.

## Onboarding and the Checklists

A new hire's **checklist** is the onboarding plan by role: the steps, who owns each, the due days. A leader ticks their steps as they are done and sees the person's whole list. The Academy's onboarding path (the six new-hire courses) is one of those steps, assigned by rule from the start date; the Training Your Team lesson covers it.

## Probation

New employees start a **probation window** from their start date (the company's length, or the person's own), and a reminder comes before it ends. A manager's **Confirm**, **Extend** or **End** is a **recommendation**; HR finalizes. Confirm or Extend sends a letter through Nexus Sign that the employee alone signs; End opens the Offboard flow, never a second path. Contractors, externals and guests are never tracked.

The **Performance** tab is one page per person: **My Numbers** (tiles Nexus works out from what it already records, such as on-time arrival and Time Sheets signed on time; never time off, sick days, pay or HR requests), **My Goals** (up to five open), **My Reviews** (a review HR starts, with the person's self review and signature) and **My 1:1s**. A leader's part: set goals with the person, keep the 1:1 notes there and not in a notebook, and read the numbers as a conversation, not a verdict. Only the person, the people they report to and HR see it.

## Exits

An exit starts **one way**: the Offboard flow in People > Exits, by HR or from a probation End. Nexus then does the rest in order: the separation package through Nexus Sign (the person first), the access that ends on the date, the leaver's future shifts cleared from the planner, their assigned equipment coming due in Item Management, their unsigned Time Sheet finalized by HR with a reason, and the record kept. A leader's part is to start it through HR, collect the equipment with photos, and never improvise a version of it.

## A Greens Scenario

A new assistant manager at Fairfield reaches the end of her probation window; the reminder lands in Sam's bell. He opens her record: the checklist complete, the Academy courses passed, two 1:1s with notes, on-time arrival strong. He recommends Confirm. HR finalizes; the confirmation letter goes to her in Nexus Sign and she signs it; the probation record closes and the Academy's onboarding checklist ticks its last step.

## Common Mistakes

- **Changing a person's title or pay yourself**, or telling them to ask you rather than Ask HR.
- **Any document the company signs first.**
- **Keeping 1:1 notes in a notebook** the next manager will never see.
- **Ending someone's employment any way but the Offboard flow.**

## Key Points

- People holds the record; the person changes their contact details, the manager changes the team's day, HR changes the record itself; Ask HR is the confidential channel.
- The company never signs before the employee; federal forms' sensitive fields are masked everywhere.
- Checklists carry onboarding; probation's Confirm, Extend or End is a recommendation HR finalizes.
- Performance is numbers Nexus works out, goals, reviews and 1:1s, seen by the person, their managers and HR.
- An exit starts in the Offboard flow and Nexus handles the rest in order.
