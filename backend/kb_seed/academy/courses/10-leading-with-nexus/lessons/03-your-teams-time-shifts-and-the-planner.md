Sunday evening. Dana builds next week for Temecula and wants Sam on Saturday to cover two move-ins. The planner warns her: that makes six days in a row for Sam. She can share anyway with a reason, or fix it. Knowing what the warning is for decides which.

## Two Tabs Under Team

**Team** > **Shifts** is your group's week: you pinned at the top, a green dot on whoever is on shift now, and under each person their shared shifts, usual hours, time off and, on past days, how their punches matched (On Time, Late, Left Early, No-Show, Excused). Everyone sees it; nobody but a manager changes it.

**Team** > **Shift Planner** (managers and above) is where a week is built.

Place a shift on a person and a day and pick its **shift type**, which carries its hours, unpaid break and grace minutes; place an **open shift** where your company allows them. Two tools do the heavy lifting:

- **Fill Automatically** lays each person's usual hours across a date range.
- **Copy Week** lays a past week down again.

Both skip each person's company holidays and approved time off, and say how many days they skipped. A request still waiting does not take the day away.

A dashed border is a draft the team cannot see. **Conflicts To Review** under the grid lists shifts that may be mistakes in plain words: outside someone's availability, overlapping another shift, on approved time off.

## The Rules Warn, Never Block

Before anything is shared, Nexus checks the week against the company's rules: rest between shifts, days in a row, daily overtime (people on the California rule), weekly overtime on the payroll week, and any weekly hour cap. They **warn** on the grid and at Share, and **never block**.

**Save & Share** lists every change and every warning per person first. Sharing over a warning, approved time off or a holiday asks for a **reason**. Only managers see it, in the shift's History; staff never do. The warning is for the person's sake; the reason is for the record. Every schedule change is logged.

## Confirm and the Nudge

A share sends each person a bell and an email and asks them to **Confirm** in Workday > My Schedule. The grid shows a check beside everyone who has, and a chip such as 5 Of 8 Confirmed lists the rest with **Nudge** and **Nudge Everyone**. Someone who confirms and then has a problem talks to you, or uses a shift request where your company has them on.

## How the Day Reaches You

From the shared schedule Nexus knows when each person is expected:

| Notice | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Late** | It shows on Team > Shifts and the widgets. A pattern is a conversation. |
| **No-Show** | One bell per team and day to you, your backup and the group's schedulers once the alert window passes with no clock-in. Call the person. |
| **Early punch** | Recorded, never blocked; one grouped bell per team and day. A habit is a conversation about who asked them to start early. |
| **Still on Break?** | The person is told first; you are told if it stays open twice as long. |

None of these change pay by themselves. They are facts the Time Sheet review and the Workforce Scorecard read.

## Trading, Calendars and Weather

Swaps, offers, releases, open-shift requests and cross-team open shifts are **off by default**, switched on per company in Settings > Shifts & Scheduling. When on, a request goes to the teammate first and then to you; the original shift stands until you approve. Weather calls apply only to departments with Weather Calls on, Construction by default, so a storage team's week is not called off for rain.

## What the Planner Never Shows

The planner shows hours, never wages. The scheduled labor cost and the labor budget are for HR and above; a manager gets neither the figure nor a flag. Build the week for coverage and hours.

## At Greens

Dana runs **Fill Automatically**; it skips Marco's approved Saturday off. Sam on Saturday trips the six-days warning, so she asks Priya instead and the warning clears. She shares. By Monday the chip reads 7 Of 9 Confirmed and she clicks **Nudge Everyone**. Tuesday the No-Show bell says Sam has not punched in for 7:30 AM; she calls, he is at the gate with a dead phone, and the bell resolves itself when he punches at 7:52 AM.

## Common Mistakes

- **Sharing over a warning without thinking.** The reason is for the record; the warning is for the person.
- **Promising a shift by text.** It does not exist until it is shared.
- **Reading an early punch as misconduct.** It is a fact; a pattern is a conversation.
- **Looking for labor cost in the planner.**

## Key Points

- Team > Shifts shows the week to everyone; the Shift Planner builds it.
- Fill Automatically and Copy Week skip holidays and approved time off.
- The rules warn, never block; sharing over a warning asks for a reason only managers see.
- Share asks everyone to Confirm; Nudge the rest. Late, No-Show and early punches reach you as facts.
