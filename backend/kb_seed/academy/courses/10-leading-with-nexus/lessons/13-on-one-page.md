Keep this page open for your first months leading a team. Every line is taught in full earlier in this course.

## Every Morning

- [ ] Check who is here: Team Clocked-In, Time Exceptions and any No-Show bell. Call a no-show before you plan around them.
- [ ] Clear what is waiting on you in Approvals: time off within a day, punch fixes on the evidence, a note on every no.
- [ ] Work the ticket desk in order: Needs Reply first, then Unassigned to a person, then Mine.

## Every Week

- [ ] Build and share next week in the Shift Planner. Read every warning before sharing past it, and Nudge whoever has not confirmed.
- [ ] Read the Workforce Scorecard with the schedule beside it. A red week is a conversation, never a pay figure.
- [ ] Check your team's Compliance in Knowledge Base & Training > Manage. Talk about Overdue and Locked before you unlock.

## Every Pay Period

- [ ] Review Time Sheets the day after the period ends. Decide change requests first, then Review & Approve or Send Back with a note.

## Whenever It Comes Up

- [ ] Decide in Nexus, not in the hallway or by text. Talk on Teams; record the decision in Nexus.
- [ ] Send pay and record questions to Ask HR. Never answer them yourself.
- [ ] Recommend on probation; HR finalizes. An exit runs only through HR's Offboard flow.
- [ ] Ask for access through an Access Group. Never share a sign-in.
- [ ] Set Delegate My Approvals before you are away.
