The gate hours change on 12/01 at every California site. By Wednesday, 31 of 44 people have acknowledged the announcement. Dana does not need to post it again; she needs the list of the 13 who have not, and two of them are hers.

## Announcements

An **announcement** is the company's word to staff: new gate hours, a holiday schedule, a safety notice. Administrators post them, from the Announcements widget's **New Announcement**; a leader asks their administrator, often with the wording drafted.

- **Audience**: everyone, a company, a department, or a **work site**. A site announcement reaches the people who work there.
- **Notify**: Bell, Bell and Teams, or Bell and Email. The bell is also the push to the phone, in every mode.
- **Acknowledge**: when the post must be read. The poster can see who has not acknowledged, so the follow-up is a short list, not a second broadcast.
- **Scheduled** posts stay hidden from staff until they publish.

Announcements show on the Dashboard through the **Announcements widget**. Make sure every new team member has it on their Dashboard.

## Conversations Are on Teams

Greens talks on **Microsoft Teams**, and Nexus deliberately has **no chat of its own**, so there is one place for conversations. Wherever Nexus shows a person (the Directory, a ticket, an approval, your team) it offers **Message on Teams** and **Call on Teams**, which open Teams straight to that person. The team's Beginning and End of Day messages post to its Teams chat.

The conversation happens on Teams; the **decision** happens in Nexus. "Approved, see Nexus" is a good Teams message. "Approved" alone is not an approval.

## Notes and Boards for Meetings

| A Note | A Board |
|---|---|
| Words, lists, tasks | A drawing |
| A meeting's agenda and minutes, a 1:1, a shift handoff | A lot layout, a repair sketch, a plan drawn together |
| **Make a Task** turns a line into a task | **Mark Up** a photo or PDF and attach it back to its ticket |
| Voice notes and dictation on a phone | Comments as numbered pins |

Share a meeting's Note with the people who were there, so they and the next manager can find it. Boards live inside Notes; there is no separate whiteboard module.

A note is private to its owner and the people it is shared with, and typing a name in one notifies nobody. A note is not a report: a problem written in a note is a problem nobody else will read. It goes in a ticket or a form.

## The Directory

**Team** > **Directory** is everyone at Greens, by **Departments**, **Reporting Line** or **Org Chart**, with each person's job title, today's availability (such as Off Today) and the Teams buttons. For a leader it is the quickest answer to "who do I ask," and availability shows who is out before you plan the day.

## The One Channel for Pay and the Record

Anything about a person's pay, title, department, bank details or HR documents goes through **Ask HR**, the confidential desk the person opens from Workday. Not through you, not on Teams, not in a note. When a team member brings you a pay question, the right answer is "Open Ask HR and HR will have it today."

## At Greens

Dana opens the not-yet list for the gate hours post, messages her two on Teams ("Please read and acknowledge the gate hours post before Friday"), and asks the other site managers to do the same with theirs. Her Saturday move-in plan is a Board drawn over the lot photo, shared with Sam and Priya. The Friday team meeting is a Note, with two lines turned into tasks.

## Common Mistakes

- **An announcement without Acknowledge** for something that must be read.
- **Not following up the not-yet list** by name.
- **Looking for a chat in Nexus.** It is on Teams by design.
- **A decision made on Teams** and never recorded in Nexus.

## Key Points

- Announcements reach everyone, a company, a department or a site, with acknowledgment when it matters and a list of who has not.
- Conversations happen on Teams from the buttons beside every person; decisions happen in Nexus.
- Meetings and plans live in Notes and Boards, shared with the people in the room; a note is never a report.
- Ask HR is the one channel for pay and the record.
