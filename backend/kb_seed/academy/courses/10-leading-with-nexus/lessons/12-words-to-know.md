## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Use the leader's Nexus words correctly with your team, HR and the managers above you.
- Explain each one in a sentence to a new manager.

## Decisions and Time

| Word | What it means | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **Approvals** | The Dashboard tab that gathers every decision waiting on you, counted once | Decide from the record it opens |
| **Recommendation** | Recommended: Approve or Check First on a time off request, with reasons | Advice, never a decision; nothing approves by code |
| **Punch fix** | A request to change a punch, with a reason | Decided on evidence; never edit a punch yourself |
| **Delegate My Approvals** | Handing your approvals to someone while you are away | Set it before you go |
| **Shift Planner** | Where the week is built and shared | Fill Automatically, Copy Week, Clear Week |
| **Shift type, zone, open shift** | Hours and grace; the site; a shift anyone may take | The planner's building blocks |
| **Share** | Publishing the week; everyone is belled to Confirm | Past a rules warning it needs a reason |
| **The rules** | Rest, days in a row, daily and weekly overtime, week caps, time off and holiday conflicts | Warn, never block |
| **Confirm** | A person's "I have seen it" on a shared schedule | The planner shows who has not; a nudge follows |
| **Late, No-Show, Left Early, Excused** | How punches matched a shift | Facts on Team > Shifts; none change pay |
| **No-show rollup** | One bell per team and day for people not punched in | Resolved in place as they arrive |
| **Early punch** | A clock-in well before the shift | Never blocked; one grouped bell |
| **Attention strip** | What to look at on a Time Sheet before approving | Missing punches, location words, break reports |
| **Review & Approve, Send Back, Approve and Next** | The three Time Sheet decisions | A send-back carries a note |
| **Only the employee signs** | The signing order of a Time Sheet | Your approval is a review the PDF records |
| **Missed-break report, premium pay** | A California hourly person's report instead of signing; the pay added for a missed break | The card goes back for signature |
| **Workforce Scorecard** | Punched hours against expected, weekly | Direct reports by default; the Monday email |
| **Expected hours** | Published shift, else preset, else the country's day; No Schedule never guessed | Read a red week with the schedule beside it |

## The Desk, People and Training

| Word | What it means | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **Queue** | A filter preset over every ticket, counted over the whole workspace | Needs Reply, Unassigned, Mine, All Open as tabs |
| **Needs Reply** | The requester spoke last, or nobody has replied | First, every hour |
| **Next Clock** | The one SLA wording on a row: first reply, update or resolution due | Red only when overdue |
| **Waiting for Vendor** | A status, with the vendor's name and promised date | The desk still owes the requester an update |
| **Preventive queue** | Tickets preventive schedules filed; its count is the overdue ones | Zero overdue means the building is being kept |
| **Confidential HR tickets** | Ask HR tickets, visible to HR's audience only | Never ask to see them |
| **People** | The HR module: the record and its tabs | The manager changes the team's day; HR changes the record |
| **The signing order** | The company never signs before the employee | Every HR document |
| **Checklist** | Onboarding steps by role, with owners and due days | Tick yours |
| **Probation** | A window from the start date; Confirm, Extend or End is a recommendation | HR finalizes; End opens the Offboard flow |
| **Performance tab** | My Numbers, Goals, Reviews, 1:1s | Numbers Nexus works out; seen by the person, their managers and HR |
| **Offboard flow** | The one way an exit starts | Nexus clears shifts, access, equipment and the unsigned Time Sheet in order |
| **Learning path** | Onboarding, role, department or custom; stages of courses and policies | Suggested for every job title and department without one |
| **Due mode** | Within days of the start date, or by one date for everyone | The days are the grace for a later joiner |
| **Escalation** | A course still not done after its due date, raised to you once then weekly | A nudge to a conversation |
| **Compliance** | Your team's training status, exportable | Act on Overdue and Locked weekly |
| **Course report, hardest questions** | Who is where; the questions people miss most | The author's next edit |
| **In-person sign-off** | Completion by a trainer or manager who saw the work | Only for work seen |
| **Refresher** | Three questions a day from the courses a person passed, on a widening schedule | Theirs, not a score |
| **Teaches, review flag, Mark Reviewed** | The modules a course teaches; the flag when What's New changes one; the author's confirmation | Courses follow the product |
| **Learn This Screen** | The Help panel's card offering the course that teaches the current screen | Learning in the flow of work |

## Communication, Access and the Record

| Word | What it means | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **Announcement** | The company's word to staff: audience, notify mode, Acknowledge | Follow up the not-yet list by name |
| **Announcements widget** | The only place announcements show on a Dashboard | Not on Home by default |
| **Message on Teams, Call on Teams** | The two buttons beside every person | Conversations on Teams; decisions in Nexus |
| **Note, Board** | Words and lists; a drawing; both in Notes, shared with the room | A note is never a report |
| **Ask HR** | The confidential desk for pay and the record | Never through the manager |
| **Tier** | Employee, Supervisor, Manager, Administrator, Super Administrator | Reach and approval; the mark beside a name |
| **Company wall** | Each company's people, departments, approvers and settings kept to itself | An Administrator is always walled |
| **Grant, Access Group** | A module opened at a level; the group that carries it | Most "I can't see it" is a missing grant |
| **Settings, scope** | One tab; every setting at one scope: organization, company, or a default a company may override | Administrators change; leaders ask |
| **Audit log** | The record of sensitive changes with a name and a time | What lets a decision stand |
| **The record** | Everything a leader decides in Nexus, with the reason | Protects the leader who decides in Nexus |

## Key Points

- Decisions and time: approvals and the recommendation, the planner and its rules, Confirm, the attendance facts, Time Sheet review and the signing order, the scorecard.
- The desk, people and training: queues and clocks, confidential tickets, the record and the signing order, probation, performance, the Offboard flow, paths, escalation, Compliance, the Refresher, the review flag.
- Communication, access and the record: announcements, Teams, Notes and Boards, Ask HR, tiers and walls, grants, Settings, the audit log.
