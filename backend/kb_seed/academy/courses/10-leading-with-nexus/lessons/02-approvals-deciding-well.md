Two punch fixes arrive on Wednesday. Marco asks for a 5:00 PM clock-out: "Forgot to punch out, locked the gate at 5." Sam asks for 6:30 PM: "Stayed late." One is a quick yes. The other needs one question first. Telling them apart is the skill of this lesson.

## What Comes to You

**Approvals** (on the Dashboard) gathers every decision waiting on you, oldest first, counted in one place so the badge and the list always agree. Chips filter by kind: Time Off, Time Sheets, Shifts, Money, Hiring, Signatures, People.

| Kind | What you are deciding | Read first |
|---|---|---|
| **Time off** | Can the site run without them those days? | The recommendation and its reasons; the Team Leave Calendar |
| **Punch fix** | Did the time they ask for really happen? | The reason, their Beginning and End of Day messages, the shift, the location words |
| **Time Sheet** | Are the hours right and complete? | Reviewing and Approving Time Sheets |
| **Shift request** | Does the trade keep the schedule whole? | Both people's shifts; the teammate accepts first |
| **Reimbursement** | Was it a company expense, with a receipt? | The business purpose, the receipt, the amount |
| **Purchase request** | Does the site need it, and does the company already own one? | The justification; Item Management |
| **Hiring vote, review, goal** | Your read on a person | The record itself |

Click **Review** on a row to read the whole request and decide there, or **Open In** the module to see it where it lives. A decision leaves the list at once and is sent after a few seconds, so **Undo** catches a slip. Rows whose yes is one click can be ticked and decided together with **Approve Selected**. A Reject or Send Back asks for a note, which the person sees.

Every time off request carries a **recommendation**: **Recommended: Approve** when coverage, notice, the balance and the calendar all hold, or **Check First** with the reasons in plain words. It is advice. Nothing in Nexus approves or rejects a request by itself. Read the reasons, look at the calendar, decide. Where your company requires HR's second approval, your yes reads Awaiting HR Approval until HR decides.

Answer **within a day**: a request that waits a week is a plan the person cannot make. When it is a no, **say why** in the note, so they can ask for different days.

## Punch Fixes Are Decided on Evidence

A punch fix asks you to change a pay record. The request carries the reason; the record around it carries the rest. An End of Day message posted at 5:02 PM supports a 5:00 PM clock-out. A shift that ended at 5:30 PM does not support 6:30 PM on its own. A punch marked Out of Location is a question, not an accusation.

Approve what the record supports. Send back what it does not, with what you need: "What kept you until 6:30 PM? A ticket number or your End of Day message would do." Never edit a punch yourself to save time; the request, the reason and your decision are the record that protects you both.

## On a Phone, and When You Are Away

On a phone, Approvals is one card at a time with two decisions and a Filter sheet. There is no bulk approve on a phone, on purpose: each decision is read before it is made.

**Delegate My Approvals**, at the bottom of Workday > Time Off, hands your approvals to a colleague (your manager, a peer under the same manager, or someone at your level or above, never your own team) for the days of your approved time off. Set it before you go. A week away with no delegate is a week of requests nobody can answer.

## A Decision That Will Stand

- **In Nexus**, on the record.
- **On time**: within a day for a request, by the review date for a Time Sheet.
- **From the evidence**, not the badge.
- **With the reason written** when it is a no or a send-back.
- **The same rule for everyone.**

## At Greens

Marco's End of Day message posted at 5:02 PM and his shift ended at 5:00 PM: Dana approves. Sam's request has no message and no ticket after 5:30 PM, so she sends it back asking what kept him. He replies with the late move-in and the tenant's name, and she approves the corrected request.

## Common Mistakes

- **Approving from the badge** without opening the record.
- **Treating Check First as a no**, or Recommended: Approve as already decided.
- **Editing a punch yourself** instead of deciding the request.
- **A no with no reason**, or leaving with no delegate.

## Key Points

- Approvals gathers every decision; **Review** opens the record, **Undo** catches a slip.
- The recommendation is advice. Decide within a day and say why when it is a no.
- Decide punch fixes on evidence; send back what the record does not support.
- One card at a time on a phone; set **Delegate My Approvals** before you are away.
