A Fairfield tenant's wife calls: he deployed last month, the card on AutoPay was replaced, and the account is 18 days late. Cubby will keep charging late fees and walking toward a lien unless someone tells it to pause. That someone is your manager, through a delinquency exemption, and your job is to spot the call and pass it on the same day.

## Before You Start

- A **delinquency exemption** tells Cubby to pause some or all of its automatic collection steps on one lease, for a set time.
- One **active** exemption per lease, and every exemption has an **end date**.
- Adding one needs the "Can manage delinquency exemptions" permission, and at Greens it is a manager decision, because it changes what the law requires Greens to do.

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

## What an Exemption Can Pause

| Pause | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Late fees** | No automatic late fee posts |
| **Overlock** | No Overlock Needed flag; an overlock already on comes off |
| **Lien eligibility** | The lease is not ready for a lien |
| **Collection messages** | No automatic collection texts or emails |

**Rent still posts.** An exemption pauses the collection steps, not the bill.

## The Five Reasons

| Reason | When | What to pause |
|---|---|---|
| **Bankruptcy** | A bankruptcy notice arrived | Your manager decides with counsel; stop every collection step until then |
| **Deceased** | The tenant has died | Your manager decides while the family or estate is reached |
| **Military Service** | The tenant is on active duty, or within 90 days after it | Lien eligibility at least: no lien without a court order |
| **Payment Arrangement** | Your manager agreed a catch-up plan | What the plan says, often late fees and overlock |
| **Other** | Anything else your manager approves | Write the reason in a note |

## Steps: Before Any Collection Step

Before a lien call, an overlock or a lien, open the lease and look for an **active exemption**: its reason, what it pauses, its end date. If it covers the step, do not take the step; if Cubby still raised the task, tell your manager.

## Steps: Add an Exemption

```figure

```

1. Confirm with your manager: which reason, what it pauses, until when.
2. Open the lease and add a delinquency exemption.
3. Choose the **reason** and what it **pauses**.
4. Set the **end date**: for a payment arrangement, the day after the last agreed payment; for military service, as your manager sets from the orders.
5. Save. If the unit was overlocked and you paused overlock, take the lock off and record it removed (Cubby How-To: Overlock and Lockout).
6. Note who approved it, what was agreed and what paperwork you saw (orders, a bankruptcy notice, a death certificate). Keep the paperwork where your manager says; never copy its contents into the note.
7. Set a task for the day before the end date.

## Steps: End or Extend

On the day before the end date, check with your manager: is the reason over, was the arrangement kept? To extend, change the end date and note why. To end it early (a broken arrangement), end it and note why; Cubby's steps resume from where the lease stands.

## At Greens

**Valley Center, a payment arrangement.** A tenant at a made-up $140.00 owes October and November, a $21.00 late fee and the $25.00 preliminary lien notice fee: $326.00. On 11/03 your manager agrees $163.00 on 11/15 and $163.00 on 11/30, with December paid on time. You add an exemption, reason Payment Arrangement, pausing late fees, overlock and lien eligibility, ending 12/01/2026; you note the plan and set tasks for 11/15 and 11/30. Both payments arrive and the exemption ends on 12/01. Had 11/15 been missed, you would tell your manager, who may end it so the steps resume.

**Fairfield, a deployed tenant.** The call from the opening: the military flag is not set. You tell your manager the same day. Your manager asks for a copy of the orders, sets the flag, and adds an exemption for Military Service with an end date from the orders. You note it and send the wife a payment link for when the family is ready.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Overlocking or starting a lien** without checking for an exemption.
2. **No end date**, or one nobody watches.
3. **Pausing less than was agreed**, so a late fee posts anyway.
4. **Missing the military question.** A mention of deployment goes to your manager the same day.
5. **Copying sensitive paperwork into notes.**

## Key Points

- An exemption pauses chosen collection steps on one lease; rent still posts.
- Reasons: Bankruptcy, Deceased, Military Service, Payment Arrangement, Other.
- It can pause late fees, overlock, lien eligibility and collection messages.
- One active exemption per lease, always with an end date, added on your manager's decision.
- Check for an exemption before every overlock and lien step.
