## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Explain what a delinquency exemption does to Cubby's automatic collection steps.
- Choose the right reason: Bankruptcy, Deceased, Military Service, Payment Arrangement or Other.
- Choose which steps to pause: late fees, overlock, lien eligibility, collection messages.
- Add an exemption with an end date, and check for one before any collection step.
- End or extend an exemption.

## Before You Start

- A **delinquency exemption** tells Cubby to stop some or all of its automatic collection steps on one lease, for a set time.
- A lease can have **one active exemption** at a time.
- Every exemption needs an **end date**.
- Adding an exemption is usually a manager decision, because it changes what the law requires Greens to do or not do. Your role may let you add one; your manager says when you should.
- Where a button's exact name is not given, the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot.

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

## What an Exemption Can Pause

When you add an exemption you choose what it pauses. It can pause any of these:

| Pause | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Late fees** | Cubby does not post the automatic late fee |
| **Overlock** | Cubby does not flag Overlock Needed, and an overlock already on is lifted |
| **Lien eligibility** | The lease does not appear on liens to initiate and is not lien eligible |
| **Collection messages** | Cubby does not send its automatic collection texts and emails |

Rent still posts while a lease is exempt. An exemption pauses the **collection steps**, not the bill.

## The Five Reasons

| Reason | When it applies | What to pause |
|---|---|---|
| **Bankruptcy** | A bankruptcy notice for the tenant has arrived | Your manager decides with counsel. Stop every collection step until they do |
| **Deceased** | The tenant has died | Your manager decides, while the family or estate is reached |
| **Military Service** | The tenant or their spouse is on active military duty, or within 90 days after it | Lien eligibility at least: a lien may not be enforced without a court order (federal SCRA). Cubby also has a military flag on the tenant |
| **Payment Arrangement** | Your manager agreed a plan to catch up | Whatever the arrangement says, often the late fee and overlock while payments come in |
| **Other** | Anything else your manager approves | Write the reason in a note |

> [!NOTE]
> Texas law gives Texas Guard members on active duty the same protection as federal servicemembers. The Texas sites are not in Cubby yet; until they are, ask your Area Manager.

## Steps: Before Any Collection Step

Before you make a collection call about a lien, overlock a unit or start a lien:

1. Open the lease.
2. Look for an **active exemption**: the reason, what it pauses and the end date.
3. If one is active and covers the step, do not take the step. If Cubby still raised the task, tell your manager.

## Steps: Add an Exemption

1. Confirm with your manager that an exemption is right, which reason, what it pauses and until when.
2. Open the lease.
3. Add a delinquency exemption (the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot).
4. Choose the **reason**.
5. Choose what it **pauses**: late fees, overlock, lien eligibility, collection messages.
6. Set the **end date**. For a payment arrangement, the day after the last agreed payment. For military service, as your manager sets from the orders.
7. Save. If the unit was overlocked and you paused overlock, the overlock is lifted: take it off and mark it removed (Cubby How-To: Overlock and Lockout).
8. Add a note: who approved it, what was agreed, what paperwork you have (a bankruptcy notice, military orders, a death report).
9. Keep the paperwork where your manager says. Do not copy it into the note.

## Steps: End or Extend an Exemption

1. Watch the end date. Set a task for the day before it.
2. On that day, check with your manager: is the reason over? Has the arrangement been kept?
3. To extend, change the end date and note why.
4. To end it early (for example, a payment arrangement that was broken), end it and note why. Cubby's automatic steps resume from where the lease stands.

## A Payment Arrangement, Worked Through

**Valley Center.** A tenant at a made-up $140.00 a month owes October and November: $280.00 plus a $21.00 late fee (15% of $140.00; Greens' rule is the greater of $20.00 or 15%, From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026) and the $25.00 preliminary lien notice fee: $326.00 in all. On 11/03, before the day-35 lien notice, your manager agrees an arrangement: $163.00 on 11/15 and $163.00 on 11/30, with December rent paid on time.

1. You add an exemption with the reason **Payment Arrangement**, pausing late fees, overlock and lien eligibility, ending **12/01/2026**.
2. You note: "Arrangement approved by manager 11/03: $163 on 11/15, $163 on 11/30. December rent due 12/01."
3. You set tasks for 11/15 and 11/30 to check the payments.
4. Both payments arrive. The balance is zero on 11/30, the exemption ends on 12/01, and normal billing goes on.

Had the 11/15 payment not arrived, you would tell your manager, who may end the exemption so the steps resume.

## Example: A Military Tenant

**Fairfield.** A parking tenant's wife calls: he was deployed last month and autopay failed because the card was replaced. You check the lease: the military flag is not set, and the account is 18 days past due. You tell your manager the same day. Your manager asks for a copy of the orders, sets the military flag, and adds an exemption with the reason Military Service, pausing late fees, overlock and lien eligibility, with the end date set from the orders. You note it and send the wife a payment link for when the family is ready.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Overlocking or starting a lien** without checking for an exemption.
2. **No end date**, or an end date nobody watches.
3. **Pausing only part** of what was agreed, so a late fee posts anyway.
4. **Missing the military question.** When a tenant mentions deployment, stop and tell your manager.
5. **Copying sensitive paperwork into notes.**
6. **Adding an exemption on your own** without your manager.

## Key Points

- An exemption pauses chosen collection steps on one lease; rent still posts.
- Reasons: Bankruptcy, Deceased, Military Service, Payment Arrangement, Other.
- It can pause late fees, overlock (lifting one already on), lien eligibility and collection messages.
- One active exemption per lease, always with an end date.
- Check for an exemption before every overlock and lien step.
