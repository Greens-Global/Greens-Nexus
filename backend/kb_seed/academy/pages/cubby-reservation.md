A caller at Murrieta wants a pull-through space for a 34 ft fifth wheel "sometime next month." He will not rent today, but he will rent from someone. If he is in Cubby as a lead with a follow-up task, that someone is Greens.

## Before You Start

- **Every caller and walk-in who does not rent today is a lead.** Record them before you hang up or before they leave.
- Search for the person first. If Cubby says **Active lead found**, choose **Continue lead** rather than making a second one.
- Greens charges **no reservation fee**.

## Lead Sources

Pick the source that is true; it tells Greens which advertising works. "Other" is Greens' most-used source today, and it tells the company nothing. Ask "How did you hear about us?" every time.

| Source | Use it when |
|---|---|
| Walk-in | They came to the office |
| Phone | They called the site |
| Referral | A tenant or someone else sent them |
| Website | They found Greens' website and contacted you |
| Search engine | They say they searched online |
| Sparefoot | They came through SpareFoot |
| Digital ad, Billboard, Physical ad | They name the ad |
| Other | Only when none of the above is true |

Cubby sets some sources itself: a website contact form, an online rental that was started and not finished, and calls its AI voice agent answered.

## Lead Statuses

| Status | Who sets it | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| New | You, or Cubby | Not contacted yet |
| In progress, hot | You | Likely to rent soon |
| In progress, cold | You | Interested, not soon |
| On hold | You | Waiting on the customer |
| Unqualified | You | Cannot rent from us, for example a size no site has |
| Reservation | Cubby, when you reserve | A unit is held |
| Converted | Cubby, when the rental is created | Rented |

## Steps: Create a Lead

1. Search by phone or email. If they exist, use their contact.
2. Choose **+ Create**, then **Lead** (or **+ Add Lead** on the Leads tab).
3. Enter name, phone and email, and ask the best way to reach them.
4. Choose the site and the pricing group they want (Cubby How-To: Find an Open Unit). For a vehicle, note its type and length hitch to bumper.
5. Enter the target move-in date, the **real** source and the rate you quoted.
6. Set the status, add a short note (what they store, what they cared about, what you promised) and choose **Save lead**.

## Steps: Reserve a Unit

1. From the lead, choose **Create reservation**.
2. Pick the unit. It must be vacant and rentable.
3. Set the move-in date and confirm the rate.
4. Save. The unit shows as **Reserved** and the lead's status becomes Reservation.
5. Tell the customer the unit, date and rate, and what to bring: photo ID and, for a vehicle, the registration and proof of insurance.

## Steps: Convert or Cancel

- **They arrive:** open the lead and choose **More actions**, then **Convert to rental**, and run the move-in (Cubby How-To: Move-In).
- **They cancel:** cancel the reservation so the unit goes back on the market, and note the reason.
- **The date passes and you cannot reach them:** follow your manager's rule for how long to hold a unit, then cancel and note it.

## Steps: Follow Up

1. From the lead, create a task with a plain title: "Call back about 12x35 RV space."
2. Set the due date you promised and assign it.
3. Save. It appears on the Tasks tab that day.

You can also send the price in writing from the lead with **Send quote**.

## Leads From the Website

An online rental that was started and not finished becomes a lead by itself. These are the warmest leads you have: the person was minutes from renting. Call the same day: "I saw you started a rental online and wanted to help you finish it."

## At Greens

**Murrieta.** The fifth-wheel caller: you create a lead, Murrieta, the pull-through pricing group, target date 12/01, source Phone, rate quoted, status In progress, hot. He will visit Thursday, so you set a Thursday task. Thursday he likes the space; you choose Create reservation for 12/01. On 12/01 you convert it.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Not recording the lead at all.**
2. **Choosing Other** when the true source is known.
3. **A second contact** for an existing tenant.
4. **Leaving a canceled reservation open**, so the unit sits off the market.
5. **Promising a call back without a task.**

## Key Points

- Every caller and walk-in who does not rent today becomes a lead, with a real source.
- You set New, In progress, On hold and Unqualified; Cubby sets Reservation and Converted.
- Record the rate you quoted; reserve with Create reservation; convert with Convert to rental.
- Every promise to call back is a task with a due date.
