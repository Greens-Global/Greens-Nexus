## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Create a lead in Cubby with the contact, site, unit type, move-in date and a real source.
- Set and change a lead's status, and know which statuses Cubby sets by itself.
- Record the rate you quoted.
- Reserve a unit for a future move-in, and convert or cancel the reservation.
- Set a follow-up task so no lead is forgotten.
- Work the leads Cubby creates by itself from online rentals that were not finished.

## Before You Start

- **Every caller and walk-in who does not rent today is a lead.** Record them before you hang up or before they leave.
- A lead is a **contact**. If the person is already a tenant, use their existing contact instead of making a second one.
- Greens charges **no reservation fee** (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026).
- The source, status and reason names on this page are Cubby's values written in plain words; the exact labels in Greens' Cubby are on the screenshots.
- Cubby's activity log calls a lead an "Interest." The screen may say Lead or Interest.
- Where a button's exact name is not given, the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot.

## Lead Sources

Pick the source that is true. The source tells Greens which advertising works.

| Source | Use it when |
|---|---|
| Walk-In | The person came to the office |
| Call | The person phoned the site |
| Website | They found Greens' website and contacted you |
| Referral | A tenant or someone else sent them |
| Search Engine | They say they searched online |
| SpareFoot | They came through SpareFoot |
| Contact Form | Cubby sets this when they filled the website contact form |
| Website Checkout, Abandoned Checkout, Failed Checkout | Cubby sets these from online rentals |
| AI Voice Agent | Cubby sets this when its voice agent took the call |
| Other | Only when none of the above is true |

"Other" is the most common source at Greens today, 54 leads, ahead of walk-ins (36) and calls (21) (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). Most of those could have been a real source. Ask "How did you hear about us?" every time.

## Lead Statuses

| Status | Who sets it | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| New | You, or Cubby when it creates a lead | Not contacted yet |
| In Progress, Hot | You | Likely to rent soon |
| In Progress, Cold | You | Interested, not soon |
| On Hold | You | Waiting on the customer, for example until they sell a house |
| Unqualified | You | Cannot rent from us, for example needs a size no site has |
| Duplicate | You | Same person already has a lead |
| Reservation | Cubby, when you reserve a unit | A unit is held |
| Converted | Cubby, when a lease is made | Rented |

You never set Reservation or Converted yourself. Cubby sets them when the reservation or the move-in happens.

## Steps: Create a Lead

1. Search for the person first by phone or email. If they exist, open their contact.
2. Start a new lead (the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot).
3. Enter the name, phone and email. Ask for the best way to reach them.
4. Choose the site and the unit type (pricing group) they want. For a vehicle, note the type and the length hitch to bumper.
5. Enter the target move-in date.
6. Choose the **real** source.
7. Record the rate you quoted.
8. Set the status: Hot if they want to rent this week, Cold if later.
9. Add a short note: what they are storing, what they cared about, what you promised.
10. Save.

## Steps: Reserve a Unit

A reservation holds a specific unit for a future move-in.

1. Open the lead.
2. Choose to reserve a unit (the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot).
3. Pick the unit. Check on the site map that it is vacant and rentable.
4. Set the move-in date the customer gave you.
5. Confirm the rate. There is no reservation fee at Greens.
6. Save. Cubby sets the lead's status to Reservation.
7. Tell the customer the unit number, the move-in date, the rate, and what to bring: photo ID and, for a vehicle, the registration and insurance.

## Steps: Convert or Cancel a Reservation

- **The customer arrives:** start the move-in from the reservation. The lease is created and the reservation is converted. See Cubby How-To: Move-In.
- **The customer cancels:** cancel the reservation (the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot) so the unit goes back on the market, and add a note with the reason.
- **The date passes and you cannot reach them:** follow your manager's rule for how long to hold a unit, then cancel and note it.

## Steps: Set a Follow-Up Task

1. From the lead, create a task.
2. Title it plainly: "Call back about 12x35 RV space."
3. Set the due date you promised the customer.
4. Assign it to yourself, or to whoever will be on shift.
5. Save. It appears on the shared task list on that day.

## Leads From the Website

When a customer starts renting online and does not finish, Cubby makes a lead by itself, with the source Abandoned Checkout or Failed Checkout. These are warm leads: the person was minutes from renting. Call them the same day.

1. Open the lead.
2. Check the unit type and rate they were looking at.
3. Call: "I saw you started a rental online and wanted to see if I could help you finish it."
4. If they rent, run the move-in. If not, set the status and a follow-up task.

## Example

**Murrieta, Tuesday.** A caller asks about storing a 34 ft fifth wheel from next month. You search his number: no contact. You create a lead, choose the site Murrieta, the unit type for a 40 ft or longer space, the target date 12/01, source Call, and record the rate you quoted. You mark him Hot because he wants a pull-through space, which Murrieta has (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). He says he will come Thursday to see it, so you set a follow-up task for Thursday. On Thursday he likes the space, and you reserve it for 12/01. Cubby sets the lead to Reservation. On 12/01 you convert it in the move-in.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Not recording the lead at all.** A caller who is not in Cubby cannot be followed up.
2. **Choosing Other** when the true source is known.
3. **Creating a second contact** for an existing tenant.
4. **Reserving a unit that is not rentable.** Check the site map first.
5. **Leaving a cancelled reservation open**, so the unit sits off the market.
6. **Promising a follow-up and not making a task for it.**

## Key Points

- Every caller and walk-in who does not rent today becomes a lead, with a real source.
- You set New, Hot, Cold, On Hold, Unqualified and Duplicate; Cubby sets Reservation and Converted.
- Record the rate you quoted and a short note.
- Greens charges no reservation fee (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026).
- Abandoned online rentals become leads by themselves; call them the same day.
- Every promise to call back is a task with a due date.
