It is 8:00 AM at Valley Center and you have ten minutes before the first walk-in. In that time you can sign in, read what Cubby says needs doing, and open the first lease on your list, if you know where things live.

## Before You Start

- You need your **own** Cubby sign-in; your manager requests it. Every action is recorded under the name of the person signed in.
- What you see depends on your role. A screen missing here is usually a permission; ask your manager.
- If you work at more than one site, check which site you are looking at before you change anything.

## Signing In

Go to app.cubbystorage.com in a web browser, or open Cubby's phone app, and sign in with your own account. The **notification bell** in the top bar shows alerts such as "AutoPay failed," "Task due today" and "Unsigned document."

## The Home Tab

The **Home** tab is where every shift starts. It shows what needs someone today: open tasks, today's move-ins and move-outs, and new online rentals or reservations, along with counts such as leads waiting for a follow-up.

| On the Home tab | What you do |
|---|---|
| Tasks due today or overdue | Work them, collection calls first |
| Today's move-ins and move-outs | Make sure the units are ready |
| New online rentals and reservations | Finish their paperwork; call any who stopped partway |
| Leads with no follow-up | Call them back |

Two more lists you will check each morning: **document issues** (leases with an unsigned agreement, missing ID, vehicle details or proof of insurance) and **overlocks needed** (see Cubby How-To: Overlock and Lockout). Every new move-in should leave nothing on the document issues list.

## The Sitemap

The **sitemap** draws each unit or space where it sits, colored by its state. Use it to show a walk-in where the open spaces are, and to spot units that are off the market. Clicking a vacant unit lets you start a rental from it (**+ Create rental**). Finding a unit by size is on Cubby How-To: Find an Open Unit.

A unit's state is two separate flags: **leased** or not, and **rentable** or not. A **vacant** unit is not leased **and** rentable. An unleased unit can still be unrentable, with one of Cubby's reasons: Repairs Required, Cleaning Required, Under Renovation, Used by Facility or Other. When you set Other, write the real reason in the note.

## The Tenant Page

Search for the tenant and open their page. Almost everything about a rental lives there:

| Part | What you find |
|---|---|
| **Rentals** | Each lease: unit, standing (how overdue), balance due, AutoPay status, last payment |
| **Ledger** | Every charge, payment, waive and transfer, with the running balance |
| **Payments** | The saved cards and bank accounts ("Payment methods") |
| **Coverage** | The Greens plan, the tenant's own policy with its number and expiration, or none |
| **Documents** | Lease agreement, ID, vehicle details and proof of insurance, each with a status |
| **Gate code** | The tenant's access code and whether it is enabled |
| **Activity** | Every change, who made it and when |
| **Notes** | Staff notes, newest first, and any pinned note |

Document statuses use Cubby's words: Not Required, Incomplete, Signature Needed, Pending Approval, Rejected, Approved.

## Search

Type a name, phone number, email or unit number to jump to a contact, lease or unit. One contact can hold several leases, at more than one site: confirm the person **and** the lease before you change anything.

## The Tasks Tab

The **Tasks** tab is one shared list for the site, on the computer and the phone. A task is Open, Completed or Archived; archived means dismissed, **not** done. Most collection tasks are made by workflows and name their day, such as "2nd - Call Failed Autopay Tenants." How to work them is on Cubby How-To: Complete a Task.

## Walkthroughs and Reports

A **walkthrough** is a site check done on the phone app, unit by unit in walkthrough order; it works offline and syncs later. Reports live under **Reporting**; the ones you use are on Cubby How-To: Open and Close Reports.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Working in the wrong site.** Check before you change anything.
2. **Opening the wrong lease** for a contact with several.
3. **Archiving a task you did not do.**
4. **Ignoring document issues.** An unsigned lease weakens Greens' position if the account ever goes to lien.

## Key Points

- Start every shift on the Home tab: tasks, move-ins, move-outs, new online rentals, leads.
- Vacant = not leased and rentable; an unrentable unit carries a reason.
- The tenant page holds rentals, ledger, payment methods, coverage, documents, gate code, activity and notes.
- Archived is not done.
