## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Sign in to Cubby on a computer or a phone.
- Read the Home page and know what each count is asking you to do.
- Use the site map to see which units are occupied, vacant or need attention.
- Open a lease and find the ledger, the paid-through date, the gate code, the documents, the coverage and the notes.
- Find any tenant, unit or lease with search.
- Open the shared task list and see what is due today.

## Before You Start

- You need your own Cubby sign-in. Your manager requests it. Never use someone else's: every action in Cubby is recorded under the name of the person signed in.
- What you see depends on your role and your site. If a screen on this page is missing for you, ask your manager.
- Cubby is cloud software and its screens change. The screenshot slots on this page are filled from Greens' own Cubby. Where a button's exact name is not given, the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot.

## Signing In

1. Open a web browser and go to app.cubbystorage.com.
2. Sign in with your own account.
3. If you work at more than one site, check which site you are looking at before you do anything. Cubby can show one site or several.
4. On a phone, use Cubby's app with the same account. It can also send push notifications.

## The Home Page

The Home page is your starting point every shift. It shows counts of things that need someone's attention. Cubby's own reporting notes name counts such as **document issues** and **liens to initiate**; the proposed opening routine on Cubby How-To: Open and Close Reports also looks at tasks due today and overlocks needed.

| Count | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Tasks due** | Tasks on the shared list due today or overdue | Work them in order, starting with collections calls |
| **Document issues** | Leases whose paperwork is not complete: an unsigned lease, missing ID, missing vehicle details or proof of insurance | Contact the tenant and finish the paperwork |
| **Liens to initiate** | Leases that owe money, are past the site's lien waiting period, are not exempt and have no lien started yet | Tell your manager; see Cubby How-To: Start a Lien |
| **Overlocks needed** | Units Cubby has flagged for an overlock | Put the overlock on and mark it applied; see Cubby How-To: Overlock and Lockout |

> [!NOTE]
> Paperwork is a known gap at Greens. Most leases brought over from the old system show their documents as incomplete: 1,850 of 2,042 active leases at the last count, with 28 missing a signature (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). Every new move-in should leave nothing incomplete.

## The Site Map

Cubby's site map is a visual layout of the site. Each unit or parking space is drawn where it sits, colored by its state. Use it to:

- Show a walk-in customer where the available spaces are.
- See at a glance which units are occupied, vacant or need attention.
- Spot units marked unrentable and why.

A unit's state is made of separate flags. A unit can be **leased** or not, and **rentable** or not. A **vacant** unit is one that is not leased **and** is rentable. A unit can be not leased and still not rentable, for one of Cubby's reasons: repairs required, cleaning required, under renovation, used by the facility, or other.

## The Lease Page

Almost everything about a rental lives on its lease. Learn where each part is.

| Part of the lease | What you find there |
|---|---|
| **Tenant** | The contact: name, phone, email, any pinned note |
| **Unit** | Which unit or space, and its pricing group |
| **Rent and charges** | The current rent, recurring fees, any discount |
| **Ledger** | Every charge, payment, waive and transfer, with the running balance |
| **Paid-through date** | The last day the lease is paid up to |
| **Autopay** | Whether it is on, and the saved card or bank account (with the card's expiration) |
| **Coverage** | The Greens plan and its premium, the tenant's own policy (with its number and expiration), or none |
| **Documents** | The lease agreement, ID, vehicle details and proof of insurance, each with its status |
| **Gate code** | The tenant's access code and whether it is enabled |
| **Vehicle** | For a parking lease: plate, state and vehicle type |
| **Activity** | Every change to the lease, who made it and when |
| **Notes** | Notes from staff, newest first |

### Document Statuses

Each required document on a lease carries a status. These are Cubby's words:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not Required | This lease does not need it |
| Incomplete | Not provided yet |
| Signature Needed | Sent, waiting for the tenant to sign |
| Pending Approval | Provided, waiting for staff to approve |
| Rejected | Provided but not acceptable, for example an expired ID |
| Approved | Done |

## Search

Cubby has a universal search. Type a name, a phone number, an email or a unit number to jump to a contact, a lease or a unit. Before you change anything, confirm you have the right person and the right lease: one contact can have several leases, at more than one site.

## The Task List

Cubby keeps **one shared task list** for the site, on the computer and the phone. Tasks have three states:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Still to do |
| **Completed** | Done |
| **Archived** | Dismissed, not done |

Most collection tasks at Greens are made by workflows, with the day of the delinquency count in the title, such as "2nd - Call Failed Autopay Tenants" and "6th - Call Past Due Tenants" (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). Staff also make their own tasks, such as a reminder to move a tenant out on a date. How to work a task is on Cubby How-To: Complete a Task.

## Walkthroughs

A walkthrough is a site inspection done from a phone. Units have a walkthrough order, and you check each one as you pass it. Walkthroughs work offline and sync when the phone is back on signal. Your site's lock check and vacant unit check are done this way.

## Reports

Reports live in their own area. The ones you will use are covered on Cubby How-To: Open and Close Reports.

## Example

**Valley Center, Monday, 8:00 AM.** You sign in and check that the site picker shows Valley Center. The Home page shows 6 tasks due, 3 document issues and 0 liens to initiate. You open the task list: four "6th" past-due calls and two of your own reminders. You open the first lease from the task, find the paid-through date on the lease page and the open charges in the ledger, and make the call. Then you open each document issue, see that two leases are missing a signature, and resend the lease for signing.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Working in the wrong site.** Check the site picker first.
2. **Archiving a task you did not do.** Archived means dismissed, not done.
3. **Opening the wrong lease** for a contact with several leases.
4. **Ignoring document issues.** An unsigned lease weakens Greens' position if the account ever goes to lien.
5. **Using someone else's sign-in.** Every action carries the name of whoever is signed in.

## Key Points

- The Home page counts tell you what needs doing: tasks due, document issues, liens to initiate, overlocks needed.
- A vacant unit is not leased and rentable; unrentable units carry a reason.
- The lease page holds the ledger, paid-through date, autopay, coverage, documents, gate code, vehicle, activity and notes.
- Tasks are Open, Completed or Archived. Archived is not done.
- Always check you are in the right site and on the right lease.
