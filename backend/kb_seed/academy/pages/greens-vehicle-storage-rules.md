## Where This Applies

These are the rules at Greens' five California sites, which run on Cubby: Escondido, Fairfield, Murrieta, Temecula and Valley Center. Greens' Texas sites are run by CubeSmart on CubeSmart's own rules and system.

## What to Collect at Move-In

- [ ] Photo ID, checked against the registration and the insurance card.
- [ ] Current registration for every vehicle, boat and trailer (a boat and its trailer are registered separately), in the tenant's name or a business they sign for.
- [ ] Proof of the tenant's own vehicle insurance. Greens' tenant protection plan does not cover vehicles.
- [ ] VIN or hull number (HIN), make, model, year, color, plate and plate state, entered in Cubby. They must match the registration.
- [ ] Title holder and lienholder (the lender), entered in Cubby. A vehicle lien notice must reach them.
- [ ] The vehicle's full length (hitch included) and height (to the top of the air conditioner, antenna or tower), checked against the space.
- [ ] Everyone allowed to drive it out, listed on the lease.

## House Rules

| Topic | Greens' rule |
|---|---|
| Condition and documents | The vehicle must be drivable, with no flat tires, broken windows or fluid leaks, and registered and insured in the tenant's name. No vehicle goes in until the documents are in |
| A vehicle that does not run, or is on Planned Non-Operation (PNO) | Only with the manager's approval; the Non-Operational Vehicle Deposit then applies. A PNO vehicle leaves on a public road only after it is registered again |
| Work on site | Only what makes it road-ready, such as a tire or battery change. No repairs or major mechanical work |
| Washing | Not by the tenant. An approved mobile detailer may wash on site |
| Dumping tanks | Only at a dump station, where the site has one (see the Site Profile) |
| Generators and power | No generators or other power sources without written permission |
| Sleeping or staying overnight | Never. A vehicle left after closing is locked in until the next business day |
| Fuel and hazardous materials | Fuel tanks under 1/4 full. No gasoline, diesel or propane containers unless empty and disconnected. No flammables, paint, chemicals or hazardous waste |
| Trailers | A hitch lock, on wood blocks, wheels chocked |
| What stays in the space | Only the vehicle on the lease, and the blocks, pads and chocks it sits on. Nothing stored beside or under it. A car may not hold the space while the RV is out; tell the office if the vehicle will be away more than 24 hours |
| Slides and awnings | Slides in, awnings closed and secured, so nothing reaches into a lane or the next space |
| Truck campers off the truck on jacks | Only with the manager's OK |
| Who may rent | The vehicle must be registered and insured in the tenant's name (or a business they sign for) |

Staff never drive or move a tenant's vehicle.

## Fees That Apply to Vehicles

- **Administration Fee** at move-in.
- **Vehicle Lien Fee**, posted with the 61st day task.
- **Non-Operational Vehicle Deposit**, when the manager approves a vehicle that does not run.

The amounts are on Greens by the Numbers; every other fee is on Greens Fees and Charges.

## When a Vehicle Tenant Stops Paying

The collections ladder is the same as for a unit until the 61st day (Greens Delinquency Timeline). Then Cubby raises "61st - Vehicle Lien Filing/Tow Notice," and the account goes to the manager. A registered vehicle or vessel never goes through the unit auction: in California it takes the DMV lien sale (Civil Code 3071), the vessel lien sale (Harbors and Navigation Code 503) or the tow option (Bus. & Prof. Code 21702.5). The lesson Vehicle and Boat Liens teaches each path.

Photograph the vehicle's condition before any tow.

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

## Key Points

- Collect ID, registration, insurance, VIN or HIN, lienholder and measurements for every vehicle.
- Vehicle tenants carry their own insurance.
- A vehicle or boat never goes through the unit auction.
- Never move a tenant's vehicle.
