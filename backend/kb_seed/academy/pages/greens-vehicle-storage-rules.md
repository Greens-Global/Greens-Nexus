## What to Collect at Move-In

- [ ] Photo ID, checked against the registration and the insurance card.
- [ ] Current registration for every vehicle, boat and trailer (a boat and its trailer are registered separately), in the tenant's name or a business they sign for.
- [ ] Proof of the tenant's own vehicle insurance. Greens' protection plan does not cover vehicles: only one vehicle lease at Greens is on it (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026).
- [ ] VIN or hull number (HIN), make, model, year, color and plate, entered in Cubby. They must match the registration.
- [ ] Title holder and lienholder (the lender), entered in Cubby. A vehicle lien notice must reach them.
- [ ] The vehicle's full length (bumper to the rear-most item, hitch included) and height (to the top of the AC, antenna or tower), checked against the space.
- [ ] Everyone allowed to drive it out, listed on the lease.

## House Rules

| Rule | Greens' rule |
|---|---|
| Must the vehicle run and have current tags? | Yes. It must be drivable, with no flat tires, broken windows or fluid leaks, and registered and insured in the tenant's name. No vehicle goes in until the documents are in (From greensstorage.com Rules and Regulations, 11/2026) |
| Working on vehicles on site (oil changes, repairs) | Only what makes it road-ready, such as a tire or battery change (From greensstorage.com Vehicle Storage FAQs, 11/2026). No repairs or major mechanical work (From greensstorage.com Rules and Regulations, 11/2026) |
| Washing, dumping tanks, running generators | No washing or rinsing by the tenant; an approved mobile detailer may wash on site (From greensstorage.com Vehicle Storage FAQs, 11/2026). No generators or other power sources without written permission (From greensstorage.com Rules and Regulations, 11/2026). Dump stations vary by site: see the site profile |
| Sleeping or staying overnight in a vehicle | Never. A vehicle left after closing is locked in until the next business day (From greensstorage.com Rules and Regulations, 11/2026) |
| Extra fuel cans, propane, hazardous materials | Fuel tanks under 1/4 full. No gasoline, diesel or propane containers unless empty and disconnected, and no flammables, paint, chemicals or hazardous waste (From greensstorage.com Rules and Regulations, 11/2026) |
| Leaving a car in the space while the RV is out | DRAFT: no. Only the vehicle on the lease parks in the space. Tell the office if the vehicle will be away more than 24 hours (From greensstorage.com Rules and Regulations, 11/2026) |
| Slides in and awnings closed while stored | DRAFT: yes, slides in and awnings closed and secured, so nothing reaches into a lane or the next space |
| Truck campers stored off the truck on jacks | DRAFT: only with the manager's OK. Trailers must have a hitch lock and sit on wood blocks with wheels chocked (From greensstorage.com Rules and Regulations, 11/2026) |
| Renting only to the title holder (or a business they sign for) | The vehicle must be registered and insured in the tenant's name (From greensstorage.com Rules and Regulations, 11/2026) |
| Non-Operational Vehicle Deposit: when it applies | DRAFT: only when the manager approves storing a vehicle that does not run, which the rules otherwise refuse. Seen once, at Murrieta, $350.00 (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026) |

Staff never drive or move a tenant's vehicle.

## When a Vehicle Tenant Stops Paying

The collections ladder is the same as for a unit until the 61st day; then Cubby raises "61st - Vehicle Lien Filing/Tow Notice" (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). See Greens Delinquency Timeline.

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

| Step | California | Texas |
|---|---|---|
| Which law | Bus. & Prof. Code 21702.5 sends a registered vehicle to Civil Code 3071 (DMV lien sale) and a vessel to Harbors and Navigation Code 503. Never the normal unit auction | Property Code 59.0445 adds steps for titled vehicles and boats |
| Who must be told | The registered owner and the legal owner (lender), through the DMV process | The last known owner and every recorded lienholder, within 30 days after taking possession; they may pay until the 31st day |
| Towing | Allowed once rent is 60 days unpaid and the preliminary lien notice was sent, with at least 10 days' notice by first-class mail with a certificate of mailing naming the tow company, its address and phone (21702.5) | Allowed only if the rental agreement authorizes towing, the tenant did not pay by the 14th day after notice, and a licensed vehicle storage facility agrees in writing (59.052) |
| Greens' practice | Vehicle Lien Fee $350.00 on the 61st day task (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026) | Set when the Texas sites join Cubby. Until then, ask your Area Manager. |

Photograph the vehicle's condition before any tow.

## Key Points

- Collect ID, registration, insurance, VIN or HIN, lienholder and measurements for every vehicle.
- Vehicle tenants carry their own insurance.
- A vehicle or boat never goes through the unit auction; it takes the vehicle lien process.
- Never move a tenant's vehicle.
