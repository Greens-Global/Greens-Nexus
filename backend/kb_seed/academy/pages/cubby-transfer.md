## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Explain what Cubby does in a transfer: one lease ends, a new one starts, and the balance moves with the tenant.
- Run a transfer from one unit or parking space to another at the same site.
- Check the new lease's rent, coverage, documents, gate code and lock.
- Answer the tenant's question "What will I pay now?"

## Before You Start

- A **transfer** is for a tenant who is staying with Greens but moving to a different unit or space: a bigger unit, a covered space instead of an open one, a longer space for a new RV.
- A transfer is **not** a move-out plus a move-in. Cubby records it as a transfer, so the tenant's history, the site's move-in and move-out counts and the reports stay right. Never close the old lease and rent a new one by hand to "transfer" a tenant.
- **How Cubby prorates the old and new unit in the transfer month is not documented.** Read the charges Cubby shows before you confirm, and ask your manager if anything looks off.

## What a Transfer Does

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Lease A on the old unit | Lease A ends, and Cubby records which lease it was transferred to |
| | Lease B starts on the new unit, for the same tenant |
| Any balance on Lease A | Moves to Lease B, shown on the ledger as a balance transfer between leases |
| The old unit | Becomes vacant, or unrentable with a reason if it needs work |

The balance moving across is why a transfer is safer than closing one lease and opening another: nothing owed is left behind on a closed lease.

## Steps: Before the Transfer

1. Open the tenant's current lease. Check the balance and the paid-through date.
2. If the tenant owes money, ask your manager whether the transfer should wait until it is paid. A balance moves with the tenant, but a past-due tenant moving to a more expensive unit is a risk.
3. Find the new unit on the site map. Check it is vacant and rentable.
4. For a parking space, check the fit again: vehicle length plus a margin, and height for covered or enclosed spaces.
5. Tell the tenant the new rate and that their bill on the 1st will change.

## Steps: Run the Transfer

1. On the current lease, start a transfer (the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot).
2. Choose the new unit.
3. Set the transfer date.
4. Read the charges Cubby shows for the new lease: rent for the rest of the month on the new unit, and how the old unit's month is handled. Check against what you told the tenant.
5. Take any payment due.
6. Confirm the transfer.
7. Open the new lease and check that the balance moved across and the paid-through date is right.

## Steps: After the Transfer

1. **Coverage:** check the new lease carries the right coverage. A tenant moving to a bigger unit may want a higher plan; a tenant with their own policy should update the policy for the new address or space.
2. **Documents:** check the new lease's documents. If the new lease needs a signature, send it now.
3. **Vehicle:** for a parking space, check the plate, state and vehicle type are on the new lease.
4. **Gate code:** check the code works for the tenant. See Cubby How-To: Gate Code.
5. **Lock:** if Greens provided a lock, move it to the new unit and record it. Make sure the old unit has no tenant lock left on it.
6. **Autopay:** check autopay is still on for the new lease.
7. **The old unit:** walk it. If it is empty and clean, leave it rentable. If not, mark it unrentable with the reason (cleaning required or repairs required).
8. **Note:** add a note on the new lease: "Transferred from unit 214 on 11/12/2026, tenant wanted drive-up."

## Example

**Temecula.** A tenant in an open 10x20 at a made-up $124.00 buys a bigger trailer, 28 ft hitch to bumper. She wants a covered space. 28 ft plus a 3 ft margin is 31 ft, so you look for a covered 12x35, the smallest covered size that fits (Temecula's covered sizes are 10x20, 11x30, 12x35, 12x40 and 12x50, From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). The site map shows a covered 12x35 vacant and rentable. You check the posted clear height against her trailer's height. She owes nothing, so you start the transfer, choose the 12x35, and read the charges Cubby shows for the rest of the month. She pays. You check the new lease: balance zero, her own policy carried over, the vehicle details on the lease, autopay on, gate code working. You walk the old 10x20; it is clean, so it stays rentable. You add a note.

## Transfer or Move-Out and New Rental?

| Situation | Use |
|---|---|
| Same tenant, different unit at the same site | Transfer |
| Same tenant adds a second unit and keeps the first | A new move-in for the second unit; no transfer |
| The tenant leaves and someone else, even family, takes over | Move-out, then a new move-in for the new person |
| The tenant moves to a different Greens site | Ask your manager |

## Common Mistakes

1. **Moving out and moving in by hand** instead of transferring. The balance and the history break.
2. **Transferring before checking the fit** for a vehicle.
3. **Forgetting the old unit**: a lock left on it, or a dirty unit left rentable.
4. **Not checking coverage and documents** on the new lease.
5. **Promising a price** for the transfer month without reading Cubby's charges.

## Key Points

- A transfer ends the old lease, starts a new one and moves the balance across.
- Never fake a transfer with a move-out and a new move-in.
- Check the fit, the rate and the charges Cubby shows before you confirm.
- After the transfer: coverage, documents, vehicle, gate code, lock, autopay, the old unit and a note.
- How Cubby prorates the transfer month is not documented: read the charges, and ask if unsure.
