## At a Glance

Murrieta is a paved vehicle lot that runs nearly full. It is the only Greens site where every space is paved, and it has many pull-throughs.

| What | Murrieta |
|---|---|
| Surface | All paved, no canopy |
| Kinds of space | Drive-in (back-in) and pull-through |
| Depths | 19 to 50 ft |
| Covered or enclosed vehicle space | None |
| Storage units | Two, plus the dump station agreements below |

## What This Means When You Sell

- **Murrieta has few open spaces at any time.** Check Cubby before you quote, and offer the waitlist or Temecula, which is close, when the size is gone.
- **Pull-throughs are the premium product.** A big rig drives in one end and out the other. Sell the no-backing convenience, not only the length.
- **Many spaces are 10 ft wide.** A rig with wide mirrors or a slide that sticks out wants 12 ft or more.
- **A rig over 35 ft** needs a 12x45 or 14x45 drive-in or a 12x50 pull-through.

## Storage Units

One 12x25 drive-up unit and one 25x25. Cubby also lists six "Dump Station Agreement" entries with no size: they are not storage, they are how Cubby bills a customer for monthly use of Murrieta's dump station. Your manager has the 25x25's use until the site profile is filled in.

## Clear Heights

The lot is open, so height matters only where something crosses the path overhead. Your manager has the clearance on the path from the gate until the site profile is filled in.

## Amenities

| Amenity | Murrieta |
|---|---|
| Dump station | Yes, sold as a monthly Dump Station Agreement |
| Wash bay | None. Tenants may not wash; an approved mobile detailer may |
| Power at spaces | At least one space has metered power |
| Air, propane | Not offered on the website |

Your manager has which spaces have power, and how it is billed, until the site profile is filled in.

## Gate and Access

- 25698 Adams Avenue, Murrieta, CA 92562; rentals (951) 698-8577.
- Gate and office hours: Greens Site Hours and Access. The office closes earlier on Saturday than the other sites.
- Your manager has the gate width, the turning room and the pull-through lane direction until the site profile is filled in. Walk the lane with a new pull-through tenant the first time.

## Local Parking Rules

The City of Murrieta lets residents bring an RV onto their lot only through a city-permitted driveway opening, one reason Murrieta owners call us. Never quote city code; tell the customer to check with the city and their HOA.

## Key Points

- All paved, nearly full: check Cubby first, then offer the waitlist or Temecula.
- The 12x50s are pull-throughs; the 12x45s and 14x45s are drive-ins.
- 10 ft wide spaces stop at 35 ft and are tight for wide mirrors.

Space sizes and counts: Greens' Cubby, 11/2026. Amenities and address: greensstorage.com, 11/2026.
