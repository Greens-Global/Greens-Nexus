## Murrieta at a Glance

Murrieta is a paved vehicle lot that runs nearly full. It is the only Greens site where every parking space is paved, and it has the most pull-through spaces.

What this means when you sell:

- Murrieta has few open spaces at any time. Check Cubby before you quote, and offer a waitlist or another Greens site (Temecula is close) when the size is gone.
- Pull-through spaces are the premium product: a big rig drives in one end and out the other with no backing. Sell the convenience, not just the length.
- Many 10 ft wide spaces: a rig with wide mirrors or a slide that sticks out needs a 12 ft or wider space.

Cubby's vehicle type is filled in on more Murrieta leases than at any other site: 26 trailers and 14 RVs among 149 vehicle leases (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). Keep it that way at every move-in.

## Storage Units

Murrieta has 8 enclosed units in Cubby: one 12x25 drive-up storage unit, one 25x25 and six "Dump Station Agreement" entries (no size, $39.50 a month street rate, 4 leased). The six are not storage: they are how Cubby bills a customer for monthly use of Murrieta's dump station (Re-check 11/05/2026, From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026).

## Local Parking Rules

The City of Murrieta lets residents bring an RV onto their lot only through a city-permitted driveway opening. That is one reason Murrieta owners call us. Never quote city code to a customer; tell them to check with the city and their HOA.
