## Valley Center at a Glance

Valley Center, in unincorporated San Diego County, is a storage-unit and mailbox site with a very small vehicle side.

| What | Count | Leased |
|---|---|---|
| Enclosed storage units | 568 | 465 |
| Mailboxes | 279 | 43 |
| Parking spaces | 3 | 3 |
| Active leases that store a vehicle | 4 | |

All counts: From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026.

## Parking Spaces

| Kind | Size | Spaces | Street rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enclosed vehicle garage, paved, roll-up door | 12x38 | 2 | $289 |
| Open, gravel | 11x35 | 1 | $149 |

All three are leased (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). Valley Center's two enclosed garages are the only enclosed vehicle spaces at Greens. When a caller wants one, take their details for the waitlist and offer an open space at another site meanwhile.

The 12x25, 12x35 and 12x50 units are vehicle-sized, but none is tagged for vehicles in Cubby. greensstorage.com lists enclosed RV storage units and indoor vehicle storage at Valley Center, with 12 ft and 14 ft tall storage doors, extra tall ceiling clearance and wide drive aisles (From greensstorage.com, 11/2026), and the FAQ names Valley Center as the site with enclosed RV units.

## Key Points

- Valley Center has 3 vehicle spaces, all leased: two 12x38 enclosed garages and one 11x35 open space (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026).
- The enclosed garages are the only enclosed vehicle spaces at Greens: keep a waitlist.
- The site is mostly storage units (568) and mailboxes (279).
