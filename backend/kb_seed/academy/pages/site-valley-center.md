## At a Glance

Valley Center, in unincorporated San Diego County, is a storage-unit and mailbox site with a very small vehicle side.

| What | Valley Center |
|---|---|
| Surface | Paved garages and one gravel space |
| Kinds of space | Two enclosed garages and one open drive-in |
| Depths | 35 to 38 ft |
| Covered vehicle space | None |
| Enclosed vehicle space | Two 12x38 garages with roll-up doors, the only ones at Greens |
| Storage units | Hundreds, most of them 10x10, and mailboxes |

## Parking Spaces by Size

| Size | Spaces | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| 12x38 | 2 | Enclosed garage, paved, roll-up door |
| 11x35 | 1 | Open, gravel |

## What This Means When You Sell

- **All three are usually leased.** A caller who wants a garage goes on the waitlist with a full entry (length, height, the product), and you offer open space at Escondido or Temecula meanwhile.
- **A garage is a drive-in with a door.** The rig must fit the measured door opening as well as the 38 ft depth with the margin.

## Storage Units

Mostly 10x10, 5x10 and 10x20, many drive-up, none climate controlled, plus mailboxes. The website offers enclosed RV units and indoor vehicle storage here, with 12 ft and 14 ft tall doors, and some units are vehicle-sized (12x25, 12x35, 12x50). None is set up for vehicles in Cubby, so never offer one for a vehicle; your manager has which units take a vehicle, and each one's door, until the site profile is filled in.

## Clear Heights

The garage doors are advertised as 12 ft and 14 ft tall; a door is smaller once hung. Your manager has the measured openings, height and width, until the site profile is filled in.

## Amenities

| Amenity | Valley Center |
|---|---|
| Wash bay | None. Tenants may not wash; an approved mobile detailer may |
| Air and water | An air and water station for RV prep |
| Dump station; power in the garages | Not offered on the website. The site runs on solar power; that is not a hookup |

Your manager has whether there is a dump station or power in the garages until the site profile is filled in.

## Gate and Access

- 28407 Lizard Rocks Road, Valley Center, CA 92082; (760) 749-2000.
- Gate and office hours: Greens Site Hours and Access.
- Your manager has the turning room in front of the garages until the site profile is filled in.

## Local Parking Rules

Never quote county code; tell the customer to check with San Diego County.

## Key Points

- Three vehicle spaces: two 12x38 enclosed garages and one open 11x35, usually all leased.
- The garages are the only enclosed vehicle spaces at Greens: keep a full waitlist entry.
- Fit the measured door opening, not the advertised one.
