## How the Count Works

Rent is due on the 1st of the month at every Greens site, and every site uses jump day 26 (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). Day 1 is the due date. Cubby runs the ladder below by itself: it posts the fees, raises the tasks on the task list and flags the overlock. Your job is to do each task the day it appears and record what happened.

A payment in full stops the ladder at any step. A partial payment does not: Cubby applies money to the oldest charge first, so the lease stays past due until every charge is paid.

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

## Example

A Murrieta boat tenant pays $184 a month by autopay. On 03/01 the card is declined.

1. 03/02: you call from the failed autopay task. No answer; you leave a voicemail and note the call.
2. 03/06: the past due task appears. You reach the tenant, who promises to pay Friday. You note it.
3. 03/11: nothing came in. Cubby posts a $27.60 late fee (15% of $184) and suspends the gate code.
4. 03/13: the tenant pays $211.60 in full. The ladder stops and nothing else posts. Check that their gate code works again before they hang up.

## What California Law Requires

- No late fee until rent is at least 10 days past due, and only if the rental agreement says so (Bus. & Prof. Code 21713.5).
- The preliminary lien notice may go out once rent has been unpaid 14 consecutive days, to the tenant's last known address and the alternate contact (21703).
- The lien attaches on the termination date in that notice if the balance is still unpaid. The owner may then deny access (21705(a)).
- A vehicle or boat is never sold through the normal unit auction. It goes through the DMV vehicle lien process (21702.5, Civil Code 3071; vessels Harbors and Navigation Code 503).
- A tenant on active military duty, or within 90 days after it, may not have a lien enforced without a court order (federal SCRA). Stop and escalate.

## What Texas Law Requires

- Texas Property Code ch. 59 lets the facility sell without a court only if the lien is underlined or in bold in the written rental agreement (59.041).
- The notice of claim goes in person, by verified mail or by email (email only if the agreement allows it), and the tenant has 14 days to pay (59.042, 59.043).
- Titled vehicles and boats need their own notice to the owner and every lienholder (59.0445). Towing is allowed only if the agreement says so (59.052).
- Texas Guard members on active duty get the same protection as federal servicemembers (59.010).

## Stop and Escalate

Stop the ladder and call your manager the same day if any of these happen:

- A Declaration in Opposition arrives (California).
- The tenant says they or their spouse are on active military duty.
- A bankruptcy notice or a death report arrives.
- The tenant disputes the balance in writing.

## Key Points

- Rent is due on the 1st; call on the 2nd and the 6th; late fee and gate suspension on the 11th.
- Preliminary lien notice on the 15th, overlock on the 20th, final call on the 30th, lien notice on the 35th.
- Vehicles go through the vehicle lien process on the 61st; the sale ad publishes on the 62nd.
- Payment in full stops everything. Partial payment does not.
- Every day and fee here is From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026.
