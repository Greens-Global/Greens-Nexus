## How the Count Works

Rent is due on the 1st at every Greens site. Day 1 is the due date. Cubby runs the ladder by itself: it posts the fees, raises the tasks and flags the overlock. Your job is to do each task the day it appears and record what happened. Why each step exists is taught in the lesson Delinquency and the Lien Process; the fee amounts are on Greens by the Numbers.

A payment in full stops the ladder at any step before the sale. A partial payment does not: Cubby applies money to the oldest charge first, so the lease stays past due until every charge is paid.

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

## The Ladder

| Day | In Cubby | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | Rent and protection post; autopay runs | Nothing yet |
| 2nd | Task "2nd - Call Failed Autopay Tenants" (with Mailbox Customer and Waste Dump User versions) | Call every tenant whose autopay was declined |
| 6th | Task "6th - Call Past Due Tenants" | Call everyone still unpaid |
| 11th | At 12:00 AM the late fee posts and the gate code changes | Explain the balance, take payment, check the code works after |
| 15th | The CA Preliminary Lien Notice fee posts and the notice goes out | Check the address and alternate contact on the lease |
| 20th | At 12:00 AM the unit is flagged Overlock Needed | Put the overlock on, usually the next day, and mark it applied |
| 30th | Task "30th - Final Collection Call" | Call and record everything |
| 35th | Task "35th - CA Lien Notice"; the notice fee posts | Your manager checks the file before the sale steps |
| 61st | Task "61st - Vehicle Lien Filing/Tow Notice" | Vehicles, boats and trailers: your manager runs the vehicle path |
| 62nd | Task "62nd - Publish 2nd Lien Sale Ad" | Your manager handles the ad |
| Sale | Sale fees post | See the lesson Auction Day |

Follow Cubby's day-11 gate change and day-20 overlock, but never explain that timing to a tenant as the law. A tenant who disputes it goes to your manager. When a tenant pays in full, remove the overlock when Cubby raises Overlock Remove Needed.

## Example

A Murrieta boat tenant pays $184 a month by autopay. On 03/01 the card is declined.

1. 03/02: you call from the failed autopay task, leave a voicemail and note it.
2. 03/06: you reach the tenant, who promises to pay Friday. You note it.
3. 03/11: nothing came in. Cubby posts a $27.60 late fee (15% of $184) and changes the gate code.
4. 03/13: the tenant pays $211.60 in full. The ladder stops. You check the gate code works before they hang up.

## Stop and Escalate

Stop the ladder and call your manager the same day if:

- a Declaration in Opposition arrives;
- the tenant says they or their spouse are on active military duty;
- a bankruptcy notice or a death report arrives;
- the tenant disputes the balance in writing.

## What the Law Requires

- **California:** no late fee until rent is at least 10 days past due; the preliminary lien notice once rent has been unpaid 14 consecutive days, to the last known address and the alternate contact; access may be denied when the lien attaches; vehicles and boats go through the DMV lien process, never the unit auction; no lien enforcement on an active-duty servicemember, or within 90 days after, without a court order.
- **Texas** (chapter 59): sale without a court only if the lien is underlined or bold in the agreement; notice of claim in person, by verified mail or by email, with 14 days to pay; separate notice for titled vehicles and boats; towing only if the agreement allows; Texas Guard members on active duty protected like federal servicemembers.

## Key Points

- Calls on the 2nd and 6th; late fee and gate change on the 11th; preliminary notice on the 15th; overlock on the 20th; final call on the 30th; lien notice on the 35th; vehicles on the 61st; sale ad on the 62nd.
- Payment in full stops everything. A partial payment does not.
- Five events stop the ladder the same day.
