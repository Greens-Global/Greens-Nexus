## How to Read This Page

Read from Greens' Cubby and greensstorage.com in November 2026, last checked November 8, 2026. Every Greens number that changes lives here; the courses teach the rule and link this page. When Cubby and this page disagree, Cubby is right and this page is out of date: tell your manager.

## Sites

Greens runs five sites in California, all on Cubby. All are in the Pacific time zone and all bill on the 1st of the month.

| Site | Mostly |
|---|---|
| Escondido | Storage units, plus a vehicle lot and mailboxes |
| Fairfield | Vehicle parking |
| Murrieta | Vehicle parking, with pull-through spaces |
| Temecula | Vehicle parking, the largest lot, with some covered spaces |
| Valley Center | Storage units and mailboxes, with two enclosed vehicle garages |

Greens' three Texas stores (Austin, Sachse and Georgetown) are run by CubeSmart on CubeSmart's own rules and system. They are not in Cubby and none of the numbers below include them.

|---|---|---|---|
| Escondido | 717 (697) | 90 (80) | 77 (28) |
| Fairfield | 4 (1) | 303 (211) | none |
| Murrieta | 8 (6) | 161 (150) | none |
| Temecula | 34 (25) | 481 (333) | none |
| Valley Center | 568 (465) | 3 (3) | 279 (43) |
| **All five** | **1,331 (1,194)** | **1,038 (777)** | **356 (71)** |

Of the active leases, 759 store a vehicle. Storage units plus mailboxes make 1,687, the figure Cubby shows as enclosed units. Valley Center's three vehicle spaces are two enclosed garages and one open space; its site page also counts a fourth lease that stores a vehicle.

## Length of Stay

Greens' current tenants (2,044 active leases) have been with Greens a median of about **21 months** so far, and an average of about 30 months, counted from each lease's original start date. A true average for finished stays needs more move-out history in Cubby than Greens has had since moving to it in September 2026. For a customer: "Most of our tenants stay well over a year."

## Proration

Rent and the protection plan are both prorated from move-in day to the end of the month; every lease then bills on the 1st.

Every fee, with what to tell a customer, is on Greens Fees and Charges.

## Late Fee

Greens' late fee equals California's legal maximum and posts on the 11th.

| Monthly rent | Late fee |
|---|---|
| $60 or less | $10.00 |
| Over $60 and under $100 | $15.00 |
| $100 or more | The greater of $20.00 or 15% of rent |
| Mailbox | $2.50 |

## The Delinquency Ladder

| Day | Step |
|---|---|
| 1st | Rent due; autopay runs |
| 2nd | Failed autopay calls |
| 6th | Past due calls |
| 11th | Late fee posts and the gate code changes, at 12:00 AM |
| 15th | Preliminary lien notice and its fee |
| 20th | Overlock Needed flagged, at 12:00 AM |
| 30th | Final collection call |
| 35th | Lien notice and its fee |
| 61st | Vehicle lien filing or tow notice |
| 62nd | Lien sale ad |

Move-ins on or after the 26th (jump day 26) pay the rest of this month plus the whole next month.
