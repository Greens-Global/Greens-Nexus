It is the 6th at Escondido and the Tasks tab shows fourteen "6th - Call Past Due Tenants." One of those tenants paid at 11:00 last night and another is on a payment arrangement. Calling them anyway wastes your morning and annoys two customers; checking the lease first takes ten seconds.

## Before You Start

- The **Tasks** tab is one shared list for the site, on the computer and the phone app.
- A task is **Open**, **Completed** or **Archived**. Archived means dismissed, **not** done.
- A completed or archived task cannot be edited until it is reopened. Completing a **repeating** task makes the next one.

## The Tasks Workflows Create

The number in the title is the day of the delinquency count the step falls on:

| Task | What you do |
|---|---|
| 2nd - Call Failed Autopay Tenants | Call each tenant whose AutoPay was declined on the 1st (mailbox and waste dump versions too) |
| 6th - Call Past Due Tenants | Call everyone still unpaid |
| 30th - Final Collection Call | The last call before the lien notice |
| 35th - CA Lien Notice | The lien notice step (Cubby How-To: Start a Lien) |
| 61st - Vehicle Lien Filing/Tow Notice | The vehicle lien step |
| 62nd - Publish 2nd Lien Sale Ad | The second sale ad |
| Remind Tenant of POI Obligation | Ask for current proof of insurance |
| Enroll Tenant in Protection Plan | Offer the plan to a tenant with no current proof of insurance |

A task's due date can look one day earlier than its title, because Cubby makes it the evening before. Work the "2nd" task on the 2nd.

## Steps: Work Today's Tasks

1. Open the Tasks tab, filtered to today and overdue.
2. Collection tasks first, oldest first; they lose value every day they wait.
3. Open each task and the lease it links to.
4. **Check before you call**: paid since the task was made? An exemption? If so, complete the task with a note saying so.
5. Do the work: the call, the payment link, the document.
6. Choose **Complete**, with a note: what you did and what happens next. To hand a task to someone else, use **Reassign** and say why.

## Completed or Archived?

| Use | When | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Complete** | You did the work, even if nobody answered | You called and left a voicemail |
| **Archive** | It should not be done at all | A duplicate, or one made by mistake |

Never archive a collection task because the tenant did not answer. A call with no answer is a call made: complete it with "No answer, voicemail left, payment link sent."

## Steps: Your Own Task

1. Create the task from the lease, lead or contact it is about, so it links there.
2. Give it a plain title ("Call back about covered space") and a due date.
3. Assign it, or leave it for whoever is on shift.

A task is a reminder, not the action. A "Move TNT Out" task does not schedule a move-out; schedule it on the lease too (Cubby How-To: Move-Out). To change a finished task, reopen it, change it and complete it again.

## At Greens

**Escondido, the 6th.** The first tenant paid through the portal at 11:00 PM: complete with "Paid 11/05 via portal, no call needed." The second has a Payment Arrangement exemption: complete with "Exemption active, arrangement on track per notes." You call the rest and note each, then return a customer's call about a 10x15 and complete that task too.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Archiving instead of completing.**
2. **Completing with no note.**
3. **Calling a tenant who already paid.**
4. **Letting collection tasks go overdue.**
5. **A task instead of the real action**, such as a move-out reminder with no scheduled move-out.

## Key Points

- One shared Tasks tab; Open, Completed, Archived. Archived is not done.
- Workflow tasks name their day: the 2nd, 6th, 30th, 35th, 61st and 62nd.
- Check the lease before every collection call.
- Complete every task with a note; reopen to change one.
