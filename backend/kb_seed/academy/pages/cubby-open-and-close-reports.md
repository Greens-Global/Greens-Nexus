It is Friday close at Valley Center. Cubby says you took $412.00 in cash and $260.00 in checks today; the drawer has to say the same before you lock the door. The open and the close are two short routines that keep Cubby and the site telling the same story.

## Before You Start

- Cubby has no "end of day" button; it does much of its work by itself. The routine below is the standard one; your manager's checklist for your site wins where it differs.
- Which reports you may open depends on your role ("Can use reports page"). Report figures are business records: never send them outside Greens.

## The Opening Routine

1. **Sign in** and check you are on your site.
2. **Home tab**: tasks due, today's move-ins and move-outs, new online rentals and reservations, leads waiting.
3. **Tasks tab**, oldest first, collection calls first. On the 2nd, the failed AutoPay calls.
4. **Overlocks** to apply or remove (Cubby How-To: Overlock and Lockout).
5. **Today's move-ins and move-outs**: the units are ready.
6. **Walk the site** with the walkthrough on your phone: locks, vacant units, the vehicle lot. A vacant unit with a lock on it, or a leased unit standing open, is something to fix today.
7. **Count the drawer's** starting cash.

## The Closing Routine

1. **Payments**: compare today's cash and check payments in Cubby with the drawer.
2. **Deposit**: make it up as your manager showed you, and record it.
3. **Tasks**: every task due today completed with a note, or reassigned with a reason.
4. **Leads**: everyone you spoke to has a status and, if needed, a follow-up task.
5. **Today's move-ins**: no document Incomplete, coverage set, AutoPay offered, gate code working.
6. **Tomorrow**: check tomorrow's move-ins, and confirm today's walkthrough is closed.
7. **Notes** for the next shift, then sign out.

> [!TIP]
> If the drawer and Cubby do not match, never "fix" it by changing a payment. Count again, look for a payment on the wrong lease or a check entered as cash, and tell your manager before you leave.

## The Reports

Reports are under **Reporting**, on the **Reports** page; a report can also be scheduled (the **Scheduled** tab).

| Report | What it shows | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| **Rent roll** | Every unit, its tenant and rent, occupied or vacant | Managers, accounting |
| **Aged Receivables** | Each open charge sorted by age: 0-30, 30-60, 60-90, 90-120, over 120 days | Managers, collections |
| **General Ledger**, cash and accrual | Every posting by account; cash counts money received, accrual counts money charged | Accounting |
| **Merchant Services Disbursements** | What the card processor paid out to Greens' bank, and when | Accounting |
| **Management summary** | Occupancy and the month at a glance | Managers |

**Rent roll** is the list you walk the site against: every leased unit has a tenant and rent; every vacant unit is rentable or carries a reason. Look for a unit leased in Cubby but found empty and open, or vacant in Cubby with a lock on it.

**Aged Receivables** shows who owes what and how old it is. One tenant can be in two buckets. Money in 30-60 and older is close to or in the lien process (see Paid-Through Date and Days Past Due).

**General Ledger.** A month with many unpaid rents shows more on accrual than on cash. You rarely need it at the counter.

**Merchant Services Disbursements.** Card payments reach Greens' bank in batches, not the minute you take them. Accounting matches each payout to the bank. When a tenant asks why a refund has not reached their card, payout timing is part of the answer.

## Steps: Run a Report

1. Open **Reporting**, then **Reports**.
2. Choose the report, the site and the date or period.
3. Run it; read it on screen or export it as your manager prefers.
4. To get it regularly, schedule it, if your role allows.

## At Greens

**Valley Center, Friday close.** Cubby shows $412.00 in cash and $260.00 in checks; the drawer has $412.00 and two checks totaling $260.00. They match; you make up the deposit. Every task is complete with a note. One move-in still shows Signature Needed: you text the signing link and leave a note for the morning. On Monday your manager runs Aged Receivables and finds three tenants with charges in 30-60, and checks each against the leases ready for a lien.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Skipping the walk** and trusting the screen.
2. **Leaving a drawer difference unexplained.**
3. **Changing a payment** to make the drawer match.
4. **A move-in left Incomplete** at close.
5. **Sending a report outside Greens.**

## Key Points

- Open: Home tab, tasks, overlocks, move-ins and move-outs, the site walk, the drawer.
- Close: payments against the drawer, the deposit, tasks with notes, leads, move-ins complete.
- Your site's checklist from your manager wins.
- Rent roll checks the site; Aged Receivables shows who owes and how old; General Ledger and Merchant Services Disbursements are for accounting.
