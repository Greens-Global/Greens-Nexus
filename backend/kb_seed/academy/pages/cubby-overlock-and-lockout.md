## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Place the overlock and the gate lockout in Greens' delinquency ladder.
- Find the units Cubby has flagged for an overlock.
- Apply an overlock and mark it applied in Cubby.
- Remove an overlock when the tenant pays, and mark it removed.
- Explain what happens to the tenant's gate code when they fall behind and when they pay.

## Before You Start

- An **overlock** is Greens' own lock put on a unit so the tenant cannot get in until the account is brought current.
- How your site overlocks a parking space, your manager shows you.
- The **day-by-day ladder** is on Greens Delinquency Timeline, and the law behind it is in Self-Storage Fundamentals, lesson 10. This page only shows where the overlock and lockout fit and how to record them.
- An overlock is a physical step on a real person's belongings. Never overlock a unit Cubby has not flagged, and never one with a delinquency exemption.
- Where a button's exact name is not given, the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot.

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

## Where This Sits in the Ladder

Cubby runs Greens' collection steps by itself (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026):

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1st | Rent due; autopay runs |
| 2nd | Task "2nd - Call Failed Autopay Tenants" |
| 6th | Task "6th - Call Past Due Tenants" |
| 11th | At 12:00 AM the late fee posts and Cubby changes the tenant's gate code |
| 15th | CA Preliminary Lien Notice fee ($25.00) posts and the preliminary lien notice goes out |
| 20th | At 12:00 AM Cubby flags the unit **Overlock Needed** |
| 30th | Task "30th - Final Collection Call" |
| 35th | Task "35th - CA Lien Notice"; CA Lien Notice fee ($35.00) |
| 61st | Task "61st - Vehicle Lien Filing/Tow Notice" for vehicles, boats and trailers |
| 62nd | Task "62nd - Publish 2nd Lien Sale Ad" |

> [!NOTE]
> The day-11 gate code change and the day-20 overlock are what Cubby does today and are under review with counsel. Staff follow Cubby, never explain or defend the timing to a tenant as the law, and send a tenant who disputes it to the manager.

Texas sites are not in Cubby yet; their steps are set when they join. Until then, ask your Area Manager.

## The Gate Code Lockout

At 12:00 AM on the 11th, when the late fee posts, Cubby changes the delinquent tenant's gate code at all five sites (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). In practice the tenant's code stops working at the gate. You do not do anything to make this happen.

When a locked-out tenant pays in full, check before they leave that their code works again. If it does not, set it (Cubby How-To: Gate Code). Only Fairfield's gate reports its events into Cubby (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026); at the other sites, test the code at the keypad or ask your manager how your gate system is checked.

## How Cubby Tracks an Overlock

Cubby records four overlock events on a lease:

| Event | Who makes it | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Overlock Needed** | Cubby, on the 20th | The unit should be overlocked |
| **Overlock Applied** | You, after putting the lock on | The overlock is on |
| **Overlock Remove Needed** | Cubby, when the tenant pays | The overlock should come off |
| **Overlock Removed** | You, after taking it off | The overlock is off |

At Greens, Cubby flags most overlocks at 12:00 AM on the 20th, and staff most often apply them the next day (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). The Home page shows the count of overlocks needed.

## Steps: Apply an Overlock

1. Each morning, check the overlocks needed on the Home page or the task list.
2. For each unit, open the lease and check: is it still unpaid, and is there **no delinquency exemption**? If the tenant paid overnight or is exempt, do not overlock; tell your manager if Cubby still shows it needed.
3. Take an overlock from stock. Record which lock it is if your site tracks overlocks by number.
4. Go to the unit. Check the unit number twice.
5. Put the overlock on next to the tenant's lock, without cutting or touching the tenant's lock.
6. Take a photo of the overlock on the door, with the unit number in the picture.
7. In Cubby, mark the overlock **applied** on the lease (the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot).
8. Add a note: date, time, lock number, photo taken.

## Steps: Remove an Overlock

1. When the tenant pays in full, Cubby flags **Overlock Remove Needed**.
2. Remove the overlock the same day, or as soon as the tenant needs access. A paying tenant should not wait.
3. Return the lock to stock.
4. In Cubby, mark the overlock **removed** (the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot).
5. Check the gate code works.
6. Add a note.

## Locks in Cubby

Cubby keeps track of locks too. A lock has a status: **Available**, **Operator Use**, **Tenant Use** or **Disabled** (damaged or missing). Lock actions are register, attach, return, transfer and disable. An overlock is a lock in operator use. If an overlock goes missing or breaks, disable it with the reason so nobody counts on it.

## Example

**Escondido, the 20th.** Cubby shows four overlocks needed. The first tenant paid at 7:30 AM through the portal, and Cubby already shows Overlock Remove Needed, so you skip that unit. The second lease has a delinquency exemption with the reason Payment Arrangement; you do not overlock it and tell your manager Cubby flagged it. You overlock the other two, photograph each, mark them applied and note the lock numbers. On the 22nd one of those tenants pays in full at the counter. You walk out with her, take the overlock off, mark it removed, and check her gate code at the keypad before she leaves.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Overlocking the wrong unit.** Check the number twice.
2. **Overlocking a tenant who paid overnight** or who has an exemption.
3. **Not marking the overlock applied or removed** in Cubby, so the counts are wrong.
4. **Leaving an overlock on after payment.**
5. **Cutting or touching the tenant's own lock.** Only your manager decides that, at the sale step.
6. **Explaining the timing as the law** to an upset tenant. Send disputes to your manager.

## Key Points

- Cubby changes the gate code on the 11th and flags Overlock Needed on the 20th (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026), both under review with counsel.
- Before you overlock: still unpaid, no exemption, right unit.
- Mark every overlock applied and removed in Cubby, with a photo and a note.
- Payment in full: overlock off the same day, gate code checked.
- The full ladder is on Greens Delinquency Timeline.
