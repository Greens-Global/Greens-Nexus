It is the 21st at Escondido and Cubby lists four units that need an overlock. One tenant paid at 7:30 this morning, and one has a payment arrangement. Overlock the wrong one and you have locked a paying customer out of her belongings. This page is the check, the lock and the record.

## Before You Start

- An **overlock** is Greens' own lock put on a unit beside the tenant's, so they cannot get in until the account is brought current. Your manager shows you how your site overlocks a parking space.
- The days on which Cubby changes the gate code and flags an overlock are on Greens Delinquency Timeline; the law behind them is in Delinquency and the Lien Process. This page is only where those steps show in Cubby and how to record them.
- Never overlock a unit Cubby has not flagged, or one with a delinquency exemption.

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. The gate code change and overlock timing are what Cubby does today. Never explain or defend the timing to a tenant as the law; send a tenant who disputes it to your manager.

## The Gate Lockout

When a tenant falls far enough behind, Cubby changes their gate code by itself, so it stops working at the gate; Cubby also sends the tenant a gate access notice. You do nothing to make it happen. When a locked-out tenant pays in full, make sure they leave with a working code (Cubby How-To: Gate Code). The gate lockout and the overlock are separate steps in Cubby.

## How Cubby Tracks an Overlock

| Event | Who | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Overlock Needed** | Cubby, on its day | The unit should be overlocked |
| **Overlock Applied** | You, after the lock is on | The overlock is on |
| **Overlock Remove Needed** | Cubby, when the tenant pays | It should come off |
| **Overlock Removed** | You, after it is off | It is off |

You find the list in two places: the **Delinquency** section filtered to the **Overlock** stage, and on a phone walkthrough, the **All units** menu set to **Overlocks needed** (and **Overlocks to remove** for the ones to take off).

## Steps: Apply an Overlock

1. Each morning, open the overlocks needed list.
2. For each unit, open the lease: still unpaid, and **no delinquency exemption**? If they paid overnight or are exempt, skip it and tell your manager if Cubby still lists it.
3. Take an overlock from stock; note its number if your site tracks them.
4. At the unit, check the number twice. Put the overlock on beside the tenant's lock, without touching the tenant's lock.
5. Photograph the overlock on the door with the unit number in the picture.
6. In Cubby, record it applied (**Add Overlock** on the walkthrough).
7. Note the date, time, lock number and that a photo was taken.

## Steps: Remove an Overlock

1. When the tenant pays in full, Cubby flags Overlock Remove Needed.
2. Take it off the same day, or as soon as they need access. A paying tenant does not wait.
3. Return the lock to stock and record it removed in Cubby.
4. Make sure their gate code works, and add a note.

## Locks in Cubby

Cubby tracks locks too: **Available**, **Operator Use**, **Tenant Use** or **Disabled** (damaged or missing). An overlock is a lock in operator use. If one goes missing or breaks, disable it with the reason so nobody counts on it.

## At Greens

**Escondido.** Of the four flagged units, the first tenant paid at 7:30 AM through the portal and Cubby already shows Overlock Remove Needed: skip it. The second has an exemption for a payment arrangement: skip it and tell your manager Cubby flagged it. You overlock the other two, photograph them, record them applied and note the lock numbers. Two days later one of them pays in full at the counter; you walk out with her, take the overlock off, record it removed and watch her code work before she leaves.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Overlocking the wrong unit.**
2. **Overlocking a tenant who paid overnight** or has an exemption.
3. **Not recording** the overlock applied or removed.
4. **Leaving an overlock on after payment.**
5. **Touching the tenant's own lock.** Only your manager decides that, at the sale step.

## Key Points

- Cubby changes the gate code and flags Overlock Needed by itself; you put the lock on and record it.
- Before any overlock: still unpaid, no exemption, right unit.
- Record every overlock applied and removed, with a photo and a note.
- Paid in full: overlock off the same day, gate code working.
