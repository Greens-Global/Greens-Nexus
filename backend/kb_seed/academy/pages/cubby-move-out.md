## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Schedule a move-out in Cubby with the date, the date notice was given, the reason and the time window.
- Turn off autopay at the right moment.
- Complete the move-out on the day after checking the unit.
- Settle what is left: a balance owed, unused prepaid money, or a deposit.
- Return the unit to the market, or mark it unrentable with a reason.
- Remove the lock and turn off the gate code.

## Before You Start

- Cubby allows **one scheduled move-out per lease**. If the date changes, change the scheduled move-out; do not add a second.
- Many staff use tasks such as "Move TNT Out" as reminders (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). That is fine, but the move-out itself must be **scheduled in Cubby**, so reports and autopay are right.
- The reasons and time windows on this page are Cubby's values written in plain words; the exact labels in Greens' Cubby are on the screenshots.
- What notice Greens requires and how the last month is charged are set in the rental agreement and by your manager.
- Only some leases brought over from the old system at Fairfield carry a deposit; new move-ins pay none (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026).
- Where a button's exact name is not given, the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot.

## Move-Out Reasons

Cubby asks why the tenant is leaving. Pick the true one. These reasons show Greens why tenants leave.

| Reason | Use it when |
|---|---|
| Can't Afford | Money is the reason |
| No Need | They no longer need the space |
| Moving | They are moving away |
| Consolidating | Combining into fewer units |
| Bad Experience | Something went wrong with Greens. Tell your manager |
| Auction | The unit was sold at lien sale |
| Other | None of these; write the reason in a note |
| None | Only if the tenant will not say |

Cubby also asks for a **time window**: Morning, Lunchtime, Afternoon, Evening or Anytime.

## Steps: Schedule the Move-Out

1. Open the lease.
2. Schedule a move-out (the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot).
3. Enter the **move-out date** and the **date the tenant gave notice**.
4. Choose the **reason** and the **time window**.
5. Decide about **autopay**. Cubby lets you turn autopay off at the same time. Turn it off only if no further rent will be due on the 1st; if the tenant still owes a payment, leave it on until it is paid or take the payment now.
6. Save. Add a note with anything the tenant said.
7. Tell the tenant what to do on the day: empty the unit, sweep it, remove their lock, and come to the office or call.

## Steps: On the Day

1. Walk the unit or space with the tenant if you can. Check it is **empty and clean**, with nothing left behind and no damage. For a parking space: no oil, no debris, no parts.
2. Take photos.
3. **Lock:** if Greens' lock is on the unit, remove it and return it to stock in Cubby. The tenant takes their own lock.
4. Complete the move-out on the lease (the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot).
5. **Gate code:** check the code is turned off. See Cubby How-To: Gate Code.
6. **The unit:** if it is ready to rent, leave it rentable. If not, mark it unrentable with Cubby's reason: Cleaning Required, Repairs Required, Under Renovation, Used by Facility or Other.

## Steps: Settle the Balance

After the move-out, look at the ledger. One of four things is true:

| The ledger shows | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Zero** | Nothing to do |
| **A balance owed** | Ask the tenant to pay it before they leave. If they cannot, it stays on the lease for collections. A balance that cannot be collected is written off by your manager as bad debt |
| **Unused prepaid money** | Your manager decides the refund under Greens Waivers, Credits and Refunds. A refund goes back to the card or account it came from |
| **A deposit** (old Fairfield leases only) | Returned or kept, as the agreement and your manager say. Cubby records which |

On Greens' ledgers some small leftover balances leave as a "move-out balance withdrawn" line. Ask your manager before you clear any balance.

> [!WARNING]
> A balance is never "forgiven" by just leaving it. It is either paid, waived with a reason, refunded or written off by a manager. Each one leaves a clear line on the ledger.

## If the Tenant Does Not Show

1. Call on the move-out day and note it.
2. Check the unit. If it is still full, do **not** complete the move-out: the tenant still has their things there and rent continues.
3. If it is empty but the tenant did not come in, ask your manager before completing the move-out and cutting the lock.

## Example

**Escondido, 01/31/2027.** A tenant in a 5x10 at a made-up $85.00 gave notice on 01/10 that she is moving away. On 01/10 you scheduled the move-out for 01/31, reason Moving, time Afternoon, and turned off autopay because January was already paid. On 01/31 you walk the unit with her: empty, swept. She takes her lock. You complete the move-out, check her gate code is off, and the unit stays rentable. The ledger shows zero. You add the note "Moving to Oregon; happy with Greens."

## Common Mistakes

1. **Using only a task as the move-out** and never scheduling it. Autopay keeps charging.
2. **Turning off autopay too early**, when the tenant still owes a payment.
3. **Completing the move-out on a full unit.**
4. **Leaving the gate code on** after the tenant has gone.
5. **Leaving a balance unexplained** instead of collecting, waiving with approval or writing off through your manager.
6. **Choosing Other or None** when the true reason is known.

## Key Points

- Schedule every move-out in Cubby: date, notice date, reason and time window. One per lease.
- Turn autopay off only when nothing more is due.
- On the day: check the unit, photos, lock, complete, gate code off, rentable or a reason.
- A leftover balance is paid, waived, refunded or written off, never left unexplained.
- New move-ins carry no deposit; only some old Fairfield leases do (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026).
