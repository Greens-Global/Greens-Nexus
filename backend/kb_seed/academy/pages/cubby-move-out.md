An Escondido tenant calls on 01/10: she is moving to Oregon at the end of the month. If you only write yourself a task, AutoPay charges her again on 02/01 and the unit sits empty in Cubby as "leased." Schedule the move-out in Cubby, and everything after it lines up.

## Before You Start

- Cubby keeps **one scheduled move-out per lease**. If the date changes, change it; do not add a second.
- A task such as "Move TNT Out" is a fine reminder, but the move-out itself must be **scheduled on the lease**, so AutoPay and the reports are right. Scheduling sends the tenant Cubby's scheduled move-out notice; completing it sends the move-out confirmation.
- The notice Greens requires and how the last month is charged are in the rental agreement; your manager answers questions about them.

## Steps: Schedule the Move-Out

1. Open the lease and schedule a move-out.
2. Enter the **move-out date** and the **date the tenant gave notice**.
3. Choose the **reason** and the **time window** (Morning, Lunchtime, Afternoon, Evening or Anytime).
4. Decide about **AutoPay**: turn it off only if no more rent will be due on the 1st. If a payment is still owed, leave it on or take the payment now.
5. Save, and note anything the tenant said.
6. Tell the tenant what to do on the day: empty and sweep the unit, remove their lock, come to the office or call.

**Reasons:** pick the true one; it shows Greens why tenants leave.

| Reason | Use it when |
|---|---|
| Can't Afford | Money is the reason |
| No Need | They no longer need the space |
| Moving | They are moving away |
| Consolidating | Combining into fewer units |
| Bad Experience | Something went wrong with Greens; tell your manager |
| Auction | The unit was sold at lien sale |
| Other | None of these; write the reason |
| None | Only if the tenant will not say |

## Steps: On the Day

1. Walk the unit or space, with the tenant if you can: **empty and clean**, nothing left, no damage. For parking: no oil, debris or parts.
2. Take photos.
3. A Greens lock on the unit comes off and goes back to stock; the tenant takes their own.
4. Complete the move-out on the lease.
5. Check the gate code is turned off (Cubby How-To: Gate Code).
6. Ready to rent: leave it rentable. Not ready: mark it unrentable with the reason (Cleaning Required, Repairs Required and so on).

## Steps: Settle the Balance

Look at the ledger after the move-out. The **Move-Outs** page lists completed move-outs that still carry a balance or prepaid money.

| The ledger shows | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Zero** | Nothing to do |
| **A balance owed** | Ask the tenant to pay before they leave. If they cannot, it stays for collections; only your manager writes it off ("Can write off move-out balance") |
| **Unused prepaid** | Your manager decides the refund under Greens Waivers, Credits and Refunds ("Can refund move-out balance") |
| **A deposit** (some old-system leases only) | Returned or kept as the agreement and your manager say; Cubby records which |

> [!WARNING]
> A balance is never "forgiven" by leaving it. It is paid, waived with a reason, refunded or written off by a manager, and each leaves a clear line on the ledger.

## If the Tenant Does Not Show

1. Call on the move-out day and note it.
2. Unit still full: do **not** complete the move-out. Their things are there and rent continues.
3. Empty but no tenant: ask your manager before you complete it or cut a lock.

## At Greens

**Escondido.** The tenant from the opening, a 5x10 at a made-up $85.00. On 01/10 you schedule the move-out for 01/31, reason Moving, time Afternoon, and turn off AutoPay because January is paid. On 01/31 the unit is empty and swept; she takes her lock. You complete the move-out, check her code is off, and the unit stays rentable. The ledger shows zero. Note: "Moving to Oregon; happy with Greens."

## Common Mistakes

1. **A task instead of a scheduled move-out.** AutoPay keeps charging.
2. **AutoPay off too early**, while a payment is still owed.
3. **Completing the move-out on a full unit.**
4. **Leaving the gate code on.**
5. **Choosing Other or None** when the true reason is known.

## Key Points

- Schedule every move-out on the lease: date, notice date, reason, time window. One per lease.
- Turn AutoPay off only when nothing more is due.
- On the day: walk, photos, lock, complete, gate code off, rentable or a reason.
- A leftover balance is paid, waived, refunded or written off, never left unexplained.
