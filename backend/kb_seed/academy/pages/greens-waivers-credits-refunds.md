## Three Different Tools

| Tool | What it does on the ledger | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Waive | Cancels all or part of one charge now. The balance drops at once. Needs a reason | A late fee on a first late payment, a fee posted by mistake |
| Credit | Holds goodwill money on the lease. It does not lower today's balance; Cubby uses it on the next rent, then protection, then automatic fees | A make-good for a problem, applied to next month |
| Refund | Sends money back to the card or bank account it came from | An overpayment, a charge taken twice |

A refund puts the money back on the lease as owed unless the charge is also waived. Never refund a charge the tenant still owes. Greens' sites waive and refund; credits are rarely used. How to do each in Cubby is on Cubby How-To: Waive or Credit.

## Who May Approve What

Your manager has Greens' waiver limits until they are set on this page. Until then, ask your manager before any waive, refund or write-off.

| Action | Who decides |
|---|---|
| Waive a late fee | Your manager, unless your manager has given you a limit |
| Waive a lien or auction fee | Your manager; the Operations Manager once a lien sale is advertised |
| Waive rent | Your manager |
| Refund an overpayment or a double charge | Your manager |
| Write off a balance | The Operations Manager |

## Example

An Escondido tenant at $129 a month pays on the 12th and asks you to remove the late fee that posted on the 11th. It is their first late payment in two years. If waiving it is within the limit your manager gave you, waive the late fee with the reason "first late payment, goodwill," and the balance drops to $129. If not, tell them you will ask your manager today and call them back by a set time, and do.

## Rules That Always Apply

- Every waive and refund carries a real reason in Cubby. "Customer asked" is not a reason.
- Never waive or refund on your own account or for family or friends.
- Never promise a waive before you know you may give it.
- A tenant in the lien process: waive nothing without your manager.

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

## Key Points

- A waive cancels a charge now; a credit waits for the next bill; a refund sends money back.
- Ask your manager before any waive, refund or write-off until Greens' limits are on this page.
- Write a real reason every time, and never promise a waive you do not have.
