A caller at Temecula asks, "Do you have anything for a 28-foot trailer, covered?" You have the answer in under a minute when you search by size in Cubby instead of walking the lot in your head.

## Before You Start

- Cubby sells by **pricing group**: all units of one size and kind, sharing a rate, such as "12' x 35' Parking - Covered." You pick the pricing group first, then the exact unit.
- An open unit is **vacant**: not leased and rentable. A unit that is empty but marked unrentable cannot be rented until it is fixed.
- For a vehicle, work out the space you need before you search: the length hitch to bumper plus a margin, and for covered or enclosed spaces the height. RV & Boat Storage Fundamentals teaches the fit check.

## Steps: Search by Size

1. Start a rental or a lead: **+ Create**, then **Rental** or **Lead**. Add or pick the customer.
2. Above the grid of pricing groups, use **+** to add filters: size, features (covered, drive-up, climate controlled) and site.
3. Keep the filter **Pricing group is Available** on, so only groups with an open unit show. A group with nothing open says **No availability**.
4. Read the rate. A pricing group can carry a walk-in rate and a web rate; quote the one your manager tells you to use at the counter.
5. Choose the pricing group, then the exact unit. Check on the sitemap that it is where the customer expects (a drive-up, a pull-through, near the gate).
6. If the customer is not renting today, save the lead with the rate you quoted (Cubby How-To: Leads and Reservations).

## Steps: Search From the Sitemap

1. Open the sitemap for your site.
2. Look for vacant units of the size you need; they are drawn where they sit.
3. Click a vacant unit and choose **+ Create rental** to start from it.

The sitemap is best with a walk-in at the counter: you can turn the screen and show them the space.

## When Your Site Is Full

If no pricing group has an open unit, choose **View nearby facilities** to see the same size at other Greens sites in Cubby. Tell the caller the site, the distance and the rate, and offer to reserve it. If nothing fits anywhere, save the lead as On Hold or Unqualified with a note of what they needed.

## At Greens

**Temecula.** The caller's trailer is 28 ft hitch to bumper. With a margin he needs at least a 31 ft space, so you filter to covered spaces 35 ft and longer. One covered 12x35 shows available. You check its height against his trailer, quote the rate and offer to reserve it for Saturday.

**Valley Center.** A walk-in wants "something like a one-car garage." You filter to 10x20 storage: No availability. You show her the 10x15 that is open on the sitemap and, for comparison, a 10x20 at Escondido through View nearby facilities.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Quoting from memory** when the unit was rented this morning.
2. **Offering an unrentable unit.** Vacant means not leased **and** rentable.
3. **Skipping the fit check** for a vehicle.
4. **Letting a full site lose the customer** without checking nearby sites.

## Key Points

- Search pricing groups by size, features and site, with Pricing group is Available on.
- Pick the pricing group first, then the unit, and check it on the sitemap.
- For vehicles, size the space before you search.
- When your site is full, View nearby facilities, then save a lead.
