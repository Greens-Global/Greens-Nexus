Two Temecula leases are ready for a lien. The notes on one say the tenant's daughter called last week to report that he died. Starting that lien would be a serious mistake, and the only thing between Greens and the mistake is the check you make before you click.

## Before You Start

- The lien process is the law's process for selling a tenant's goods to pay what they owe. It is taught in Delinquency and the Lien Process, and for vehicles in RV & Boat Storage Fundamentals. Greens' days and fees are on Greens Delinquency Timeline and Greens Fees and Charges.
- **Who starts liens at your site is your manager's decision.** Ask before you start one.
- Every step must happen on time, in order, with the right notice. A mistake can stop a sale or expose Greens to a claim.

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

## Where Liens Live in Cubby

- The **Collections** page lists leases ready for a lien and is where lien processes are started.
- The **Lien Management** page follows each lease through its notices, advertising and sale.
- A lease is ready when it is active, owes money, is past the lien waiting period, has **no** delinquency exemption and has no lien started yet.

Cubby posts the lien fees by itself on their days and raises tasks such as "35th - CA Lien Notice," "61st - Vehicle Lien Filing/Tow Notice" and "62nd - Publish 2nd Lien Sale Ad." It also tracks certified lien mail: when a notice was submitted for mailing and when it was mailed.

## Steps: Check Before a Lien Starts

1. **Balance and paid-through date.** Has the tenant paid anything since the list was built?
2. **Delinquency exemption.** An exempt lease is not lien eligible.
3. **Military.** Check the military flag and the notes. A tenant on active duty, or within 90 days after it, may not have a lien enforced without a court order. Stop and tell your manager.
4. **Bankruptcy notice, death report, written dispute or Declaration in Opposition** in the notes: stop and tell your manager.
5. **Documents.** Is the lease agreement signed? Many leases brought over from the old system are not. Tell your manager if it is not.
6. **Address and alternate contact** on file. Notices go to the last known address.

## Steps: Start the Lien

Only if your manager has said you start liens at your site:

1. On the Collections page, start the lien process for the lease.
2. Check the balance, address and alternate contact once more, and confirm.
3. The lease's lien status changes and it leaves the ready list.
4. Note who started it and when, and complete any task that asked for it.

## Lien and Auction Statuses

| Lease lien status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Delivering Lien | Notices are being delivered |
| Needs Advertisement | Notices done; the sale must be advertised |
| Waiting for Auction | Advertised; waiting for the sale |
| Settled Unsold | The sale happened and the unit did not sell |
| Cancelled (Cubby's spelling) | The lien was stopped, usually because the tenant paid |

At Greens most liens so far ended Cancelled. Paying in full is the best ending.

An auction has its own statuses: **Listed**, **No Bids**, **Sold**, **Payment Received**, **Complete** and **Cancelled**. Settling and completing auctions is your manager's job.

## The Vehicle Path

A vehicle, boat or trailer is never sold through the normal unit auction. "61st - Vehicle Lien Filing/Tow Notice" starts the vehicle path, which your manager runs. Your part is keeping the vehicle details on the lease complete (plate, state, type) so the filing can be done.

## Stopping the Process

The lien stops when the tenant pays **in full**, including the fees that have posted. A partial payment does not stop it. When a lien tenant pays in full:

1. Take the payment.
2. Tell your manager the same day, so the lien is canceled and any ad or listing is pulled.
3. Make sure the overlock comes off and the gate code works.

## At Greens

**Temecula.** Your manager asked you to start both liens. The first lease: unpaid since 09/30, no exemption, signed, address on file, no military flag. You start it and note it. The second: the notes report the tenant's death. You do not start it; you tell your manager, who considers an exemption with the reason Deceased.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Not checking for a payment** since the list was built.
2. **Missing a military tenant.**
3. **Ignoring a Declaration in Opposition, bankruptcy notice or death report.**
4. **Treating a vehicle like a unit.**
5. **Not telling your manager** that a lien tenant paid.

## Key Points

- Liens are started from Collections and followed on Lien Management.
- Before any lien: payment, exemption, military, bankruptcy, death, dispute, signed lease, address.
- Who starts liens at your site is your manager's decision.
- Vehicles follow the vehicle path.
- Payment in full stops the lien; tell your manager the same day.
