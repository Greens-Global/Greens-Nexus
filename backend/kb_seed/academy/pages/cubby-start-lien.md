## What You'll Be Able to Do

- Read the "liens to initiate" count and know which leases it lists.
- Check a lease before a lien is started on it.
- Start a lien in Cubby, or hand it to the person who does at your site.
- Read the lien and auction statuses on a lease.
- Recognize the vehicle lien path and the tasks that go with it.
- Know the situations that stop the lien process at once.

## Before You Start

- The lien process is the law's process for selling a tenant's goods to pay what they owe. It is covered in Self-Storage Fundamentals, Delinquency and the Lien Process (California and Texas side by side), and for vehicles in RV & Boat Storage Fundamentals. Greens' own days and fees are on Greens Delinquency Timeline and Greens Fees and Charges.
- **Who starts liens at your site is your manager's decision.** Many sites keep it with the manager. Ask before you start one.
- A lien is serious. Every step must happen on time, in order, with the right notice. A mistake can stop a sale or expose Greens to a claim.
- Where a button's exact name is not given, the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot.

## Liens to Initiate

The Home page shows a count of **liens to initiate**. A lease is on it when all of these are true:

- the lease is active and owes money;
- it is late past the site's lien waiting period;
- it has **no** delinquency exemption;
- no lien has been started on it yet.

At the 11/2026 snapshot, 20 leases were on the list (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026).

## The Fees and Tasks Around a Lien

| When | What posts or appears |
|---|---|
| The 15th | CA Preliminary Lien Notice fee, $25.00 |
| Day 35 | Task "35th - CA Lien Notice"; CA Lien Notice fee, $35.00 |
| Day 61 | Task "61st - Vehicle Lien Filing/Tow Notice"; Vehicle Lien Fee, $350.00 |
| Day 62 | Task "62nd - Publish 2nd Lien Sale Ad" (the Advertising Fee and its amount are on Greens Fees and Charges) |

(From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026. The full ladder and the questions still with counsel are on Greens Delinquency Timeline.)

Cubby also tracks certified lien mail: when a notice is submitted for mailing and when it was mailed.

## Steps: Check a Lease Before a Lien Starts

1. Open the lease from the liens to initiate list.
2. Check the **balance and the paid-through date**. Has the tenant paid anything since the list was built?
3. Check for a **delinquency exemption**. An exempt lease is not lien eligible.
4. Check the **military flag** and the notes. A tenant on active military duty, or within 90 days after it, may not have a lien enforced without a court order. Stop and tell your manager.
5. Check the notes for a **bankruptcy notice**, a **death report**, a **written dispute** or a **Declaration in Opposition** (California). Any of these: stop and tell your manager.
6. Check the **documents**: is the lease agreement signed? Many leases brought over from the old system show their paperwork as incomplete (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). Tell your manager if the lease is not signed.
7. Check the **address and alternate contact** are on file. Notices go to the last known address.

## Steps: Start the Lien

Only if your manager has said you start liens at your site:

1. On the lease, start the lien (the button name in Greens' Cubby is on the screenshot).
2. Check the balance, the tenant's address and the alternate contact once more.
3. Confirm. The lease's lien status changes and the lease leaves the liens to initiate list.
4. Add a note: who started it and when.
5. Complete any task that asked for it, with the note.

## Lien and Auction Statuses

A lease in the lien process shows a status. These are Cubby's values at Greens (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026):

| Lease lien status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Delivering Lien | Notices are being delivered |
| Waiting for Auction | Notices done; the unit is waiting for its sale |
| Settled Unsold | The sale step happened and the unit did not sell |
| Cancelled | The lien was stopped, for example because the tenant paid |

At Greens most lease liens so far ended Cancelled: at Escondido 23 cancelled, 1 settled unsold and 1 waiting for auction; Murrieta 1 and Temecula 3 delivering lien (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). Paying in full is the best ending.

An auction itself has its own statuses in Cubby: **Listed**, **No Bids**, **Sold**, **Payment Received**, **Complete** and **Cancelled**. Settling an auction records whether the unit sold or did not. Completing it records whether a cleaning deposit is refunded or kept. Settling and completing auctions is your manager's job.

## The Vehicle Path

A vehicle, boat or trailer is never sold through the normal unit auction. On day 61 Cubby raises **"61st - Vehicle Lien Filing/Tow Notice"**, and the Vehicle Lien Fee of $350.00 applies (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). In California that is the DMV vehicle lien process. Your manager handles it; your part is to keep the vehicle details on the lease complete (plate, state, type) so the filing can be done.

## Stopping the Process

The lien stops at any step when the tenant pays **in full**, including the fees that have posted. A partial payment does not stop it. When a tenant pays in full:

1. Take the payment.
2. Tell your manager the same day so the lien is cancelled and any sale ad or auction listing is pulled.
3. Check the overlock comes off and the gate code works.

## Example

**Temecula, the 4th.** The Home page shows two liens to initiate. Your manager has asked you to start them. The first lease: unpaid since 09/30, no exemption, lease signed, address on file, no military flag. You start the lien and note it. The second lease: the notes say the tenant's daughter called on 10/28 to say the tenant died. You do not start the lien. You tell your manager, who considers a delinquency exemption with the reason Deceased.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Starting a lien without checking for payment** since the list was built.
2. **Missing a military tenant.** Stop and escalate.
3. **Ignoring a Declaration in Opposition, bankruptcy notice or death report.**
4. **Treating a vehicle like a unit** in the lien process.
5. **Taking a partial payment as the end of it.**
6. **Not telling your manager** that a lien tenant paid, so the sale goes ahead.

## Key Points

- Liens to initiate = active, owes, past the lien wait, not exempt, no lien started.
- Before any lien: payment check, exemption, military, bankruptcy, death, dispute, signed lease, address.
- Who starts liens at your site is your manager's decision.
- Lien statuses at Greens: Delivering Lien, Waiting for Auction, Settled Unsold, Cancelled.
- Vehicles follow the vehicle lien path from day 61.
- Payment in full stops the lien; tell your manager the same day.
