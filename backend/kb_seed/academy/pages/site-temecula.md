## At a Glance

Temecula is Greens' largest vehicle site, the only one with **covered** spaces, and the one with the most long spaces.

| What | Temecula |
|---|---|
| Surface | Gravel and paved |
| Kinds of space | Drive-in (back-in), pull-through, and covered drive-in |
| Depths | 15 to 50 ft |
| Covered vehicle space | Yes: a roof with open sides, the only covered spaces at Greens |
| Enclosed vehicle space | None |
| Storage units | 34 drive-up units, all 8 ft wide |

### Pull-Through Spaces

28 spaces, 30 to 40 ft deep, most of them on the paved rows. The pull-through tag is on each space in Cubby.

## What This Means When You Sell

- **Temecula has the most open spaces 40 ft and longer at Greens,** most of them 11x40. A big rig that Murrieta cannot fit can often come here, but the open spaces stop at 45 ft.
- **Covered spaces are few, and the long ones are usually full.** Rent open now and offer the covered waitlist (see Waitlists and Upgrades).
- **Never put a rig under the roof** until you know its height and the covered rows' clear height.
- **Many spaces are 10 or 11 ft wide.** A wide rig wants a 12 ft space.

## Storage Units

All 34 are 8 ft wide and drive-up, from 8x10 to 8x45. They are not set up for vehicles in Cubby; your manager has their door sizes, and whether a vehicle may go inside, until the site profile is filled in.

## Clear Heights

The covered rows are where height matters most. Your manager has the clear height under the lowest beam of the covered rows, and on the entry path, until the site profile is filled in.

## Gate and Access

- Gate and office hours: Greens Site Hours and Access.
- Each space's surface, cover and pull-through tag is on the space in Cubby.
- Your manager has the gate width, turning room, stacking room and a row map until the site profile is filled in.

## Local Parking Rules

Many owners here live under an HOA. Never quote city code or HOA rules; tell the customer to check.

## Key Points

- The biggest lot, with the only covered spaces at Greens and many pull-throughs.
- The most open long spaces: a backup for big rigs when other sites are full.
- Know the covered rows' clear height before anything goes under the roof.
