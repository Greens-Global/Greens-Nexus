## Office and Gate Hours

All five sites are in the Pacific time zone. Hours are from greensstorage.com.

| Site | Office hours | Gate hours |
|---|---|---|
| Escondido | Monday to Saturday 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM, closed Sunday | Every day 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM |
| Fairfield | Monday to Saturday 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM, closed Sunday | Every day 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM |
| Murrieta | Monday to Friday 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM, Saturday 8:30 AM to 4:00 PM, closed Sunday | Every day 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM |
| Temecula | Monday to Saturday 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM, closed Sunday | Every day 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM |
| Valley Center | Monday to Saturday 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM, closed Sunday | Every day 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM |

Greens' Texas stores are run by CubeSmart, with CubeSmart's own hours and access rules.

Gate hours are longer than office hours. Tell a new tenant both before they sign. Greens' Rules and Regulations say a tenant still on site after gate hours is trespassing, a vehicle left after closing is locked in until the next business day, and access outside gate hours needs written permission (24-hour access upon approval). If the hours on a site's gate keypad ever differ from this table, tell your manager.

## Gate Codes

- Every tenant gets their own gate code, kept on the lease in Cubby. Never share one, reuse one or give one to anyone not on the account.
- Only Fairfield's gate reports entries to Cubby today. There Cubby records each try as allowed, master code, unknown code or outside of hours.
- A tenant who tries outside gate hours is refused at the gate. That is not a broken code. Check the hours before you change anything.

## When Access Is Suspended

| Event | What happens |
|---|---|
| Rent 10 full days unpaid | At 12:00 AM on the 11th Cubby posts the late fee and changes the tenant's gate code |
| Overlock | Cubby flags Overlock Needed at 12:00 AM on the 20th; staff put the overlock on |
| Paid in full | Remove the overlock when Cubby raises Overlock Remove Needed, and check that the gate code works |

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

California ties denial of access to the lien attaching, after the preliminary lien notice period (Bus. & Prof. Code 21705(a)). Follow Cubby's day-11 gate change and day-20 overlock, never explain that timing to a tenant as the law, and send a tenant who disputes it to your manager. Never lock out or overlock a tenant by hand outside what Cubby flags, and never overlock a paid-up unit. In Texas, access is denied on seizure under the notice of claim.

## Example

A Fairfield RV tenant calls at 6:00 AM on the 12th: the gate will not open. You see a balance from the 1st and a late fee from the 11th. Explain that the account is past due, take the payment, then check that the gate code works before they hang up. Do not give out a master code.

## Key Points

- Know each site's office and gate hours and tell new tenants both.
- One gate code per tenant; gate codes are passwords.
- Cubby changes a delinquent tenant's gate code on the 11th and flags the overlock on the 20th.
- Never lock out or overlock by hand outside what Cubby flags.
