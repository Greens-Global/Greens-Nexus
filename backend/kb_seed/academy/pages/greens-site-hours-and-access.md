Gate hours are longer than office hours. Tell a new tenant both before they sign. Greens' Rules and Regulations say a tenant still on site after gate hours is trespassing, a vehicle left after closing is locked in until the next business day, and access outside gate hours needs written permission (24-hour access upon approval) (From greensstorage.com, 11/2026).

## Gate Codes

- Every tenant gets their own gate code, kept on the lease in Cubby. Never share one, reuse one or give one to anyone not on the account.
- Only Fairfield's gate reports entries to Cubby today. There Cubby records each try as allowed, master code, unknown code or outside of hours (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026). The other four sites' gates do not report to Cubby yet.
- A tenant who tries outside gate hours is refused at the gate. That is not a broken code. Check the hours before you change anything.

## When Access Is Suspended

| Event | California | Texas |
|---|---|---|
| Rent 10 full days unpaid | At 12:00 AM on the 11th Cubby posts the late fee and changes the tenant's gate code (seen in Cubby) (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026) | Set when the Texas sites join Cubby. Until then, ask your Area Manager. |
| Overlock | Cubby flags Overlock Needed at 12:00 AM on the 20th; staff put the overlock on (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026) | Set when the Texas sites join Cubby. Until then, ask your Area Manager. |
| Paid in full | Remove the overlock when Cubby raises Overlock Remove Needed, and check that the gate code works | Set when the Texas sites join Cubby. Until then, ask your Area Manager. |

> [!WARNING]
> Training only, not legal advice. When in doubt, stop and ask your manager.

California ties denial of access to the lien attaching, after the preliminary lien notice period (Bus. & Prof. Code 21705(a)). Never lock out or overlock a tenant by hand outside what Cubby flags, and never overlock a paid-up unit. In Texas, access is denied on seizure under the notice of claim, as Greens' policy and counsel set it.

> [!NOTE]
> The day-11 gate code change and the day-20 overlock are what Cubby does today and are under review with counsel. Staff follow Cubby, never explain or defend the timing to a tenant as the law, and send a tenant who disputes it to the manager.

## Example

A Fairfield RV tenant calls at 6:00 AM on the 12th: the gate will not open. You see a balance from the 1st and a late fee from the 11th. Explain that the account is past due (do not explain the timing as the law; if they dispute it, send them to the manager), take the payment, then check that the gate code works before they hang up. Do not give out a master code.

## Key Points

- Know each site's office and gate hours and tell new tenants both.
- One gate code per tenant; gate codes are passwords.
- Cubby changes a delinquent tenant's gate code (seen in Cubby) on the 11th and flags the overlock on the 20th (From Greens' Cubby, 11/2026); both are under review with counsel.
- Never lock out or overlock by hand outside what Cubby flags.
