Location tracking for on-site crews — active only while you're clocked in.


  
  

    Set up this phone
    
Ask your manager to show you the pairing QR in Nexus, then scan it — that's the whole setup.

    
    
    

      
      
      
      
      
    

  


  
  

    Your consent
    

      While you are clocked in, Nexus records your location about every 5 minutes (or when you move ~100 m)
      so your team can see where the crew is. Tracking stops the moment you clock out. A notification stays
      visible the whole time it's on. You can withdraw consent any time, which stops tracking immediately.
